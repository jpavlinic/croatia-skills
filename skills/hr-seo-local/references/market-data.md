# Croatian market data for search (dated)

All figures carry their period and source. StatCounter measures page views on
sites using its tracker; treat it as an indicator, not a census. Refresh
before quoting in a client deliverable.

Desktop only, **August 2026** (StatCounter search snippet): Google 91.01%,

(Windows/Edge defaults, office users).

## Devices

StatCounter, Croatia, **September 2026**: mobile 51.5%, desktop 46.73%,
tablet 1.77%. Desktop is still close to half (unusually high compared with
many markets), so test both layouts; B2B and office-hour traffic skews to
desktop, tourism and local searches to mobile.

## Browsers

StatCounter, Croatia, **September 2026**: Chrome 68.28%, Safari 13.4%,
Edge 5.84%, Firefox 4.66%, Samsung Internet 3.71%, Opera 2.07%.

## Internet and online behaviour (DZS)

Državni zavod za statistiku, "Primjena informacijskih i komunikacijskih
tehnologija (IKT) u kućanstvima i kod pojedinaca u 2025.", ZTI-2025-1-2,
published 8 Dec 2025 (persons aged 16–74; reference period: three months
before the survey):

- Household equipment with computers and internet access has been flat for
  four years ("saturation").
- What internet users do online: messaging 94%, looking up information about
  goods and services 90%, reading news 88%, e-mail 86%, calls/video calls
  77%, e-banking 77%.
- 51% of internet users bought goods or services online.
- Problems with online purchases: late delivery 30%, missing warranty
  information 9%, wrong or damaged product 8%.
- 32% of internet users used generative AI tools (first time measured);
  mostly ages 16–34.

**VERIFY** the overall share of internet users (16–74) and of daily users
for Croatia in Eurostat dataset `isoc_ci_ifp_iu` (EU average 2025: about 90%
daily users per Eurostat). The DZS release above does not state the headline
percentage in its text. Secondary sources cite household internet access of
about 88% for 2025; confirm against Eurostat `isoc_ci_in_h` before using.

## Population

- Census 2021 (DZS): 3,871,833 inhabitants. Use the latest DZS population

## What this means for SEO

- Google first; Bing Webmaster Tools as a low-effort extra (desktop users).
- Mobile-first indexing applies, but keep desktop UX strong.
- Users research products online before buying (90% look up goods and
  services): comparison, price and review content earns traffic.
- Small market: volumes are low; business value per keyword matters more
  than raw volume (see `keyword-research-croatian.md`).

## Analytics and consent

- Croatia has no statutory analytics exemption for cookies or device access:
  ZEK čl. 43. st. 4. requires consent for GA4 and similar tags. Expect
  analytics to undercount; Search Console data (Google-side) are complete.
  Details, cookieless options and the pending EU Digital Omnibus change:
  `croatia:hr-legal-compliance` (cookies reference).
- Use Search Console for queries, pages, countries and devices; filter by
  country `Croatia` and compare with diaspora countries.
- Bing Webmaster Tools gives Bing and some Copilot-related data.
