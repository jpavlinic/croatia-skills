# Long-term renting and mortgages

Status: researched 2026-10-25. Not legal or tax advice.

Laws: Zakon o najmu stanova (ZNS, NN 91/96 … 105/20, consolidated text in force since
26 Sep 2020); ZOO čl. 550–578 (najam in general); Zakon o porezu na dohodak; Zakon o
energetskoj učinkovitosti u zgradarstvu (NN 155/25); Zakon o upravljanju i održavanju
zgrada (ZUOZ, NN 152/24, 47/26).

"Long term" = residential lease for permanent living (najam stana za stalno stanovanje).
Short-term/tourist rental (under 30 days, ZUOZ čl. 4(1)(5)) is a different regime →
`croatia:hr-tourism-rentals`.

## 1. The lease contract (ugovor o najmu stana)

- **Written form** is mandatory (ZNS čl. 4(2)).
- **Mandatory content** (ZNS čl. 5): parties; description of the flat or part let; rent and
  payment method; type of housing costs and how they are paid; persons who will live with the
  tenant; duration; maintenance; use of common areas, parts and installations and the land
  serving the building; handover.
- **Rent types** (čl. 6–9): protected rent (zaštićena najamnina, set by government criteria,
  for protected tenants and social housing) or **freely agreed rent** (slobodno ugovorena) for
  everyone else.
- **Energy certificate**: the landlord must obtain it before letting, show it to prospective
  tenants and hand over a copy; the energy class must be in any media ad (Zakon o energetskoj
  učinkovitosti u zgradarstvu čl. 14; fines čl. 70: natural person 1,000–2,000 €).
- **Deposit (polog / jamčevina)**: **not regulated** by ZNS. It is a contractual matter: agree the
  amount (market practice 1–2 months' rent), what it secures, inspection and return deadline.
  The landlord also has a statutory lien over the tenant's movables for unpaid rent and damage
  (ZNS čl. 15(2)).
- **Handover record** (zapisnik o primopredaji) on condition of the flat (čl. 12(2)): do it with
  photos and meter readings.
- **Solemnisation** of the lease by a notary makes it directly enforceable (eviction, unpaid rent)
  without a lawsuit; recommended for landlords. If a notary certifies or drafts it, the notary
  reports it to Porezna (see §3).

## 2. During and ending the lease

| Topic | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Landlord's duty | hand over the flat fit for living; maintain it and make necessary repairs | ZNS čl. 12–13 |
| Tenant's duty | careful use, no alterations without written consent, report repairs, pay rent on time | ZNS; gov.hr |
| Rent change (indefinite contract) | no change for 1 year; then written proposal; new rent may not exceed ~20 % above local average for comparable flats; tenant may go to court within 30 days | ZNS čl. 10–11 |
| Fixed-term contract | renews automatically for the same period unless a party gives written notice ≥ 30 days before expiry; changed terms → tenant has 15 days to reply | ZNS čl. 25 |
| Landlord's notice — grounds | non-payment, unauthorised sublet, disturbing others, unlisted person > 30 days, non-residential use; written warning with 30 days to remedy first (not needed after more than two breaches) | ZNS čl. 19 |
| Landlord's own need (indefinite contract) | only if another suitable flat is provided | ZNS čl. 21 |
| Notice form and period | written, with reasons, by hand against signature or registered mail; **3 months** (čl. 19 grounds) or **6 months** (čl. 21) | ZNS čl. 22 |
| Tenant's notice (indefinite) | at least **3 months** | ZNS čl. 23 |
| Eviction | lawsuit after notice expires; urgent procedure (or direct enforcement if solemnised) | ZNS čl. 22(4)–(5) |
| Sublet | tenant files the sublease with Porezna | ZNS čl. 28(2) |

**Fixed-term vs indefinite.** Most private leases are fixed-term (1 year). Because of automatic
renewal (čl. 25), a landlord who wants the flat back must send written notice at least 30 days
before expiry. Put the end date and this mechanism clearly in the contract.

**Building rules.** Owners must tell the building's representative about a new tenant within
15 days (ZUOZ čl. 41(1)(6)). Letting to more than four unrelated adults needs 2/3 co-owner
consent plus adjoining neighbours (ZUOZ čl. 35) → `apartment-buildings.md`.

## 3. Registration and tax

- **Deliver the contract** (and every rent change) to the city's housing department and to the
  competent Porezna office (ZNS čl. 26(1)). ZNS sets no deadline; tax rules require the document
  within **8 days** of signing; if a notary certified or drafted it, the notary sends it within
  30 days and the landlord has no filing duty (porezna-uprava.gov.hr, *Dohodak od najma*).
- **Income tax** (individual landlord): **12 %** on rent minus **30 %** flat expenses, paid
  monthly per Porezna's rješenje, by the last day of the month (porezna-uprava.gov.hr). No
  separate surtax (prirez) since the 2024 reform (**VERIFY** if your municipality case is unusual).
- Example 2026: 600 €/month → base 420 € → tax 50.40 €/month (604.80 €/year). Estimate only.
- Renting as a business (obrt with real accounting, d.o.o.) → `croatia:hr-business-admin`.
- A flat let long term for at least 10 months in the year is **exempt from porez na nekretnine**
  (ZLP čl. 27(1)(2), (5)). Keep the registered contract as proof.

## 4. State measures for long-term renting (2025–2026)

### Program priuštivog najma (affordable rent programme)

Adopted by the Government on 13 Nov 2025 under the Nacionalni plan stambene politike do 2030;
run by APN; legal framework now also in the Zakon o priuštivom stanovanju (NN 45/26).

- **Owners**: flat empty for at least **2 years**, with a use permit or rješenje o izvedenom
  stanju; 6 months allowed to finish basic works (tportal summary of the programme, Nov 2025).
- **Payment to owners**: the **local median rent**, paid in **two instalments**: 60 % when the flat
  is handed to APN and 40 % at half of the contract term (example in the programme summary:
  5-year contract, 60 m² Zagreb flat, 660 €/month median).
- **Tenants**: target groups (families without own home, lower/average income, young families,
  people with disabilities, deficit occupations); rent capped at 30 % of household income after
  housing costs (Zakon o priuštivom stanovanju čl. 2(1)(12), 51); APN leases for 5 years,
  renewable (čl. 50(3)–(4)).
- Public calls on apn.hr and mpgi.gov.hr (the 2026 tenant call was open until 15 May 2026).
  **VERIFY** current calls, contract length for owners and the tax treatment of the payment.

### Other levers pushing short-term → long-term

- Property tax exempts permanent living and long-term lease but hits empty and tourist flats
  (ZLP čl. 27).
- Co-owner consent for short-term rental in blocks of flats (ZUOZ čl. 34).
- Tourist-rental restrictions and flat-rate changes → `croatia:hr-tourism-rentals`.

## 5. Mortgages (stambeni krediti)

### HNB borrower-based limits

HNB Odluka o kriterijima kreditiranja potrošača, applied since **1 July 2025**:

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| DSTI (all debt service / income), housing loans | **≤ 45 %** |
| DSTI, non-housing loans | ≤ 40 % |
| LTV (loan / value of pledged property), all loans secured by real estate | **≤ 90 %** |
| Maturity, housing and real-estate-secured loans | ≤ 30 years |
| Maturity, other non-housing loans | ≤ 10 years |
| Bank exceptions per quarter (from **1 Oct 2026**) | 10 % of housing loans above DSTI 45 % (was 20 %); 5 % of non-housing above DSTI 40 % (was 10 %); 10 % of secured loans above LTV 90 % (was 20 %) |
| Exceptions must favour | buyers solving their own housing need (at least 75 % of exceptions) |

Sources: hnb.hr *Kriteriji kreditiranja potrošača – pitanja i odgovori* (updated 19 Mar 2025);
hnb.hr *Obrazloženje uz donošenje Odluke…* (19 Aug 2026).

Practical consequences: plan a deposit of at least 10 % plus all costs (RETT, agency, notary)
from own funds; overdraft use counts at 3 % of the average used balance (HNB Q&A); banks
also apply the protected minimum living cost rule.

### Subsidised loans and other aid

- **APN subsidised housing loans** (Zakon o subvencioniranju stambenih kredita): ran in yearly
  rounds **until 2023**; no new rounds found for 2024–2026 (apn.hr). Existing beneficiaries keep
  their subsidies under the original terms. **VERIFY** with APN before telling a user it is closed.
- **First-home tax refund** (2025→): see `taxes-costs-2026.md`.
- **Priuštivo stanovanje purchase model** (Zakon o priuštivom stanovanju): flats at a capped
  price, buyer pays ≥ 10 %, rest 50 % bank loan + 40 % APN/public loan, total term ≤ 35 years,
  35-year restriction on sale/rental (čl. 37–42). Watch for calls by APN.
- Local units often run their own young-family housing grants; check the city's website.
