# Building legality, legalisation and energy certificates

Status: researched 2026-10-25. Not legal advice.

Laws: **Zakon o gradnji** (new act in force **1 Jan 2026**, repealing the Zakon o gradnji
NN 153/13, 20/17, 39/19, 125/19, 145/24 — čl. 161–162; published NN 155/25 — **VERIFY** issue
number); **Zakon o postupanju s nezakonito izgrađenim zgradama** (ZPNIZ, NN 86/12 … latest
amendment NN 48/26, in force 16 May 2026); **Zakon o energetskoj učinkovitosti u zgradarstvu**
(ZEUZ, NN 155/25, in force 1 Jan 2026). Ministry: mpgi.gov.hr.

## 1. What makes a building "legal"

A building is lawful for sale, financing and registration if one of these exists:

| Document / status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Građevinska dozvola + uporabna dozvola** (building + use permit) | built and approved under the building act; use without a use permit is a misdemeanour (new Zakon o gradnji čl. 128: natural-person owner 3,000–6,000 €) |
| **Rješenje o izvedenom stanju** (legalisation decision) | an illegally built building was legalised under ZPNIZ |
| **Built before 15 Feb 1968** | deemed built with a final building permit (new Zakon o gradnji čl. 145(1)); proven with a use permit issued on request, based on e.g. pre-1968 aerial images (čl. 145(2)–(4), 146) |
| Older acts' equivalents | permits issued under earlier building acts (e.g. rješenje o uvjetima građenja, potvrda glavnog projekta) and certificates for buildings built under older regimes — ask the city's building office whether they count as lawful (**VERIFY** case by case under the new Zakon o gradnji, existing-building provisions čl. 143–153) |

"Legalizirano" in an ad should mean a **final** (pravomoćno) rješenje o izvedenom stanju exists for
the **whole** building as it stands today, and that the building is registered in the cadastre and
ZK. Ask for the decision itself, not the receipt of the application.

## 2. Legalisation (ozakonjenje) — 2026 status

- **Deadline abolished**: since 16 May 2026, requests for a rješenje o izvedenom stanju can be
  filed at any time (mpgi.gov.hr, *Legalizacija nezakonito izgrađenih zgrada*).
- **Cut-off date**: only buildings built or reconstructed **by 21 June 2011** and visible on the
  DOF5/2011 orthophoto (or other aerial images up to that date; otherwise shown on the cadastral or
  another official map by that date) (ZPNIZ as amended NN 48/26, čl. 6).
- **Filing**: electronically via **eDozvola**, with electronic attachments (geodetic survey, architectural
  survey) (NN 48/26 čl. 5, 11). Handled by the city/county building office.
- **Once finally rejected, a new request cannot be filed** (NN 48/26 čl. 10(3)), except requests
  rejected earlier only for missing documents or unpaid fees (transitional čl. 26). So file a
  complete request.
- **Excluded**: buildings in registered cultural-heritage sites without the conservation authority's
  confirmation; temporary structures (mobile homes, containers, kiosks, tents etc.) (NN 48/26 čl. 2);
  other exclusions under ZPNIZ (e.g. certain protected zones, unsafe buildings) — **VERIFY** the
  current exclusion list in the consolidated ZPNIZ.
- **Fee**: *naknada za zadržavanje nezakonito izgrađene zgrade u prostoru* must be paid (or its first
  instalment) before the decision is issued; amount and method set by the **Uredba (NN 84/26)**;
  the ministry publishes a 2026 calculator (mpgi.gov.hr).

### Effects of a rješenje o izvedenom stanju

Generally (ZPNIZ — **VERIFY** exact articles): the building may be used, connected to utilities,
entered in the cadastre and land register, and sold and mortgaged like a lawful building. It is
**not** a building permit for future works: later reconstruction needs a regular permit. Parts built
after the legalisation, or not covered by it (extra floor, terrace closure), are illegal again.

### Risks of buying a non-legal or partly legal building

- Banks will not lend; the building (or the illegal part) cannot be registered in the ZK.
- Building inspection may order removal (rješenje o uklanjanju) of works not covered by any decision.
- Utility connections and tourist-rental licences usually require proof of legality.
- Property tax still applies to illegal dwellings from first use (ZLP čl. 25(3)).
- If legalisation is still possible (built by 21 June 2011), price in the survey costs, the fee and
  time, or make legalisation a condition of the sale.

## 3. Energy certificate (energetski certifikat)

Source: ZEUZ čl. 13, 14, 69, 70.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| When required | **before sale, renting, leasing (zakup) or leasing** of a building or unit, and before the use permit for new buildings (čl. 14(1)–(2)) |
| Show and hand over | show it to prospective buyers/tenants; give the certificate or a photocopy to the buyer/tenant (čl. 14(2)) |
| Advertising | the **energy class must be stated** in media ads by the owner (čl. 14(2)(4)) and by the licensed agent (čl. 14(4)) |
| Validity | **10 years** from issue (čl. 13(4)) |
| Exempt | religious buildings; temporary buildings (≤ 2 years) and low-energy industrial/agricultural buildings; **residential buildings used less than 4 months a year**; **detached buildings under 50 m²** useful area (čl. 13(1)) |
| Fines — owner, natural person | **1,000–2,000 €** for no certificate, not handing it over, or no class in the ad (čl. 70(2)) |
| Fines — owner, legal person | 3,000–6,000 € (čl. 70(1)) |
| Fines — agent | legal person 3,000–6,000 €; natural person 1,000–2,000 € for no class in the ad (čl. 70(3)–(4)) |
| Fines — investor without certificate before use permit | legal person 5,000–10,000 €; natural person 2,500–4,500 € (čl. 69) |

Notes:
- The "under 50 m²" exemption is for **detached buildings**, not for small flats in a block: a 35 m²
  flat being sold still needs a certificate.
- Certificates are issued by authorised persons listed by the ministry; the register of certificates is
  kept by the ministry (mpgi.gov.hr, *Energetsko certificiranje zgrada*).
- A certificate issued under the old Zakon o gradnji stays valid until its 10 years expire
  (**VERIFY** transitional provision of ZEUZ).
- Energy-renovation grants → `croatia:hr-eu-funding`.
