# Foreigners buying property in Croatia

Status: researched 2026-10-25. Not legal advice.

Legal basis: Zakon o vlasništvu i drugim stvarnim pravima (ZV) čl. 354–358.a; Zakon o
poljoprivrednom zemljištu (ZPZ, NN 20/18, 115/18, 98/19, 57/22); ZPPN čl. 3, 16(5).
Competent ministry: **Ministarstvo pravosuđa, uprave i digitalne transformacije** (MPUDT),
Uprava za građansko, trgovačko i upravno pravo, Ulica grada Vukovara 49, Zagreb
(mpudt.gov.hr → *Stjecanje prava vlasništva stranih osoba*).

## Decision table

| Buyer | Residential / building land | Agricultural land | Consent needed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Croatian citizen or Croatian company | yes | yes (with state pre-emption on large plots) | no |
| **EU / EEA** (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway) national or company | **same as Croatians** | same as Croatians since **1 July 2023** | no |
| **Swiss** individual | same as Croatians; attach registration of temporary residence to the ZK filing | – | no (companies: in some cases yes) |
| **Third-country** national or company (UK, US, BiH, Serbia, Russia, etc.) | only with **reciprocity** + **ministry consent** | **no** (foreign persons cannot acquire agricultural land) | **yes** |
| Croatian company owned by foreigners | acts as a Croatian legal person | see ZPZ | generally no — **VERIFY** for indirect-acquisition concerns |

Sources: mpudt.gov.hr; informator.hr (agricultural land after 1 July 2023).

## EU / EEA buyers

- Acquire on the same conditions as Croatian citizens and Croatian-seated companies; no consent
  (ZV čl. 358.a; mpudt.gov.hr).
- The ministry page still says EU persons could not acquire agricultural land "do 30. lipnja
  2023"; that transitional period has **ended**, so EU buyers are now equal, subject to the
  general ZPZ rules: the State's **pre-emption right** on agricultural land over 10 ha
  (continental) or 1 ha (coastal), with a 30-day offer to the agriculture ministry; contracts in
  breach are void (ZPZ čl. 71).
- Proof of citizenship (ID/passport) is attached to the land-registry filing.
- Practical needs: OIB (`croatia:hr-identifiers`), Croatian or EU bank account, a notary and
  usually a court interpreter if the buyer does not understand Croatian.

### OECD amendment (pending)

NN 52/25 replaces ZV čl. 358.a so that nationals and companies of **OECD member states** (and
states adhering to the OECD liberalisation codes) also acquire without consent, except
agricultural land. It enters into force **on the day Croatia joins the OECD**; procedures already
started finish under the old rules. As of Oct 2026 Croatia was in the final phase of accession
(**VERIFY** on vlada.gov.hr / narodne-novine.nn.hr whether membership and the amendment are in
force). When in force, US, UK, Canadian, Australian, Japanese, Swiss, etc. buyers would no
longer need consent for non-agricultural property.

## Third-country buyers: reciprocity and consent

1. **Reciprocity** (uzajamnost) between Croatia and the buyer's country is a precondition. The
   ministry publishes the list (*Informacije o uzajamnosti u stjecanju prava vlasništva
   nekretnina…*, mpudt.gov.hr). Reciprocity may be full, conditional or limited to some property
   types; check the current entry for the buyer's country.
2. Sign the purchase contract (conditional on the consent is wise; set a long-stop date and what
   happens to any kapara if consent is refused).
3. **Application** to MPUDT, in writing, in person or by post, with (mpudt.gov.hr):
   - the contract (original or certified copy);
   - proof of the seller's ownership (ZK izvadak);
   - certificate from the city's planning office on the property's status (inside the building
     zone, not agricultural land);
   - proof of citizenship (certified passport copy) or company register extract;
   - power of attorney if represented; a buyer living abroad without a representative must name
     an agent for service in Croatia.
4. Administrative procedure under the Zakon o općem upravnom postupku; **no statutory processing
   time is published** (practice: several months — **VERIFY** current times with the ministry or
   a local lawyer). Administrative fees (upravne pristojbe) apply — **VERIFY** amount.
5. With the consent, file the uknjižba. **RETT liability arises on the day consent is obtained**
   (ZPPN čl. 16(5)); foreign and domestic buyers pay RETT equally (ZPPN čl. 3).
6. Areas excluded by special laws (agricultural land; protected areas as defined by special law)
   cannot be acquired. **VERIFY** for any property in a nature park, national park or
   military/security zone.

Inheritance by third-country nationals: possible under reciprocity; some regimes require the
property to be sold within a period (e.g. Swiss companies inheriting: 2 years per the ministry
page). **VERIFY** case by case.

## Buying through a Croatian company

- A d.o.o. or j.d.o.o. registered in Croatia is a domestic legal person; it buys without ministry
  consent and pays RETT like any buyer. Running costs: accounting, annual financial statements,
  corporate income tax, VAT rules on later sale (new builds) → `croatia:hr-business-admin`.
- Using a company purely to avoid the consent regime can be challenged; the ministry treats
  "indirect acquisition" specially for some foreign companies (see Swiss-company rules on
  mpudt.gov.hr). Get legal advice. **VERIFY**.
- Contributing property into a company's capital is exempt from RETT unless sham (ZPPN čl. 14).

## Other practical points for foreign buyers

- **Language**: contracts for the ZK must be in Croatian (bilingual is common); a sworn court
  interpreter (sudski tumač) is used when a party does not understand Croatian.
- **Residence**: owning property is not in itself a basis for a residence permit for third-country
  nationals (**VERIFY** current Zakon o strancima rules). Schengen 90/180 rules apply.
- **Tax residence**: renting the property out creates Croatian tax obligations regardless of
  residence; selling within 2 years triggers the 24 % tax (`taxes-costs-2026.md`).
- **Property tax** (porez na nekretnine) applies to holiday homes of non-residents: they are not
  used for permanent living (ZLP čl. 27). Budget 0.60–8 €/m² a year.
- **Banking**: Croatian banks lend to non-residents restrictively; EU buyers have better access.
  HNB DSTI/LTV limits apply to consumers (`renting-long-term.md`).
- **First-home refund** is for Croatian citizens only.
