# Apartment buildings: co-ownership, manager, reserve

Status: researched 2026-10-25. Not legal advice.

Law: **Zakon o upravljanju i održavanju zgrada (ZUOZ)**, NN 152/24, 47/26, in force
**1 Jan 2025** (čl. 69). It took building management out of the Zakon o vlasništvu i drugim
stvarnim pravima and the Uredba o održavanju zgrada (procedures started earlier finish under the
old rules, čl. 68). Applies to multi-dwelling, residential-commercial and commercial buildings
and block garages (čl. 1). Supervision: ministry (mpgi.gov.hr) and communal wardens (čl. 55–56).

## Key players

| Who | Role | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Zajednica suvlasnika** (owners' association) | all owners of separate parts; a **legal person** from registration in the Registar zajednica suvlasnika; owns the reserve account; can sue and be sued; name "Zgrada" + address | ZUOZ čl. 7–8 |
| Registar zajednica suvlasnika | national electronic register kept by DGU, with OIBs of associations, managers, buildings and units | čl. 9–10 |
| **Predstavnik suvlasnika** (owners' representative) + deputy, optional Vijeće suvlasnika | elected by majority; convenes meetings, signs contracts for the owners, receives notices | čl. 42–46 |
| **Upravitelj zgrade** (building manager) | **mandatory**: owners must entrust management to a registered manager (čl. 47(1)); investor of a new building within 30 days of the final use permit (čl. 47(2)); not required for single-owner buildings or buildings owned only by spouses/partners/relatives (čl. 47(3)) | čl. 47–54 |
| **Prinudni upravitelj** (compulsory manager) | appointed by the mayor within 6 months of learning that owners have not chosen one | čl. 50 |
| Međuvlasnički ugovor (co-owners' agreement) | concluded when signed by owners holding more than 50 %; binds all | čl. 36 |

## Majorities (čl. 4)

| Majority | Threshold (by value area or registered co-ownership shares) |
|---|---|
| natpolovična (simple) | > 50 % |
| dvotrećinska (two-thirds) | > 2/3 |
| kvalificirana (qualified) | > 80 % |

Decisions are taken at owners' meetings or by collecting signatures (čl. 37–40). Decisions on
management (čl. 39(3)) include: choosing the representative and manager, annual and multi-year
maintenance programmes, insurer, **amount of the reserve**, energy renovation, EV charging,
façade works, lifts, accessibility, green infrastructure.

## Zajednička pričuva (reserve fund)

- Purpose: maintenance of common parts and management; may also fund investment maintenance and
  loan repayments per the multi-year programme (čl. 28).
- Paid **monthly** into the special reserve account (čl. 29(1)–(2)).
- **Statutory minimum**: per m² of value area of the flat per year = **0.54 % of the etalonska
  cijena građenja** published by the ministry (čl. 29(3)); the monthly amount cannot be below
  1/12 of that (čl. 29(4)).
- With the published etalonska cijena of **796.34 €/m²** (NN 106/25, "za primjenu u drugim
  propisima"): 4.30 €/m² per year ≈ **0.36 €/m² per month**. A 60 m² flat → at least ~21.50 € a
  month. **VERIFY** whether a newer etalonska cijena has been published in 2026.
- If no annual/multi-year programme is adopted, owners must pay **five times** the last adopted
  reserve (never less than the statutory minimum) (čl. 29(5)).
- The co-owners' agreement may set **double reserve** for business premises and units used for
  short-term rental or rental to many persons (čl. 28(4)).
- Ministry interpretation of the reserve rules: mpgi.gov.hr, *Zajednička pričuva – tumačenje*
  (13 Jul 2026).

## Short-term rental and business use in flats

| Use | Consent needed | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet registered activities (e.g. office work) | none | ZUOZ čl. 33(1) |
| Other registered activities (after change of use) | **2/3 majority** + **all owners whose walls, floors or ceilings adjoin** the flat; for ≥ 5 years; revocable by simple majority after ≥ 3 breaches of house rules in 2 years | čl. 33(2)–(5) |
| **Short-term rental** (under 30 days) | prior **written** consent of a **2/3 majority** + **all adjoining owners**; owners already doing short-term rental count as consenting; given for ≥ 5 years; revocable by simple majority after ≥ 3 breaches in 2 years; binds future owners | **čl. 34** |
| Rental to more than 4 unrelated adults | same consent rule | čl. 35 |
| Transitional | flats already used for short-term rental on 1 Jan 2025 must collect consent within **5 years** (by 1 Jan 2030); business activity also 5 years; multi-person rental within 1 year | čl. 63 |

The owner remains liable for misdemeanours even if a third party runs the rental (čl. 34(3)).
Buyers planning tourist rental must check whether consent exists and transfers (it binds future
owners, čl. 34(7)) → `croatia:hr-tourism-rentals`.

## Owners' duties (čl. 41)

- Follow the law, the co-owners' agreement, house rules and decisions.
- Maintain your own unit; allow the manager access when needed for common parts or to stop damage
  (refusal: fines 1,000–5,500 € for natural persons, čl. 58 as amended NN 47/26).
- Notify the representative of: change of ownership (**30 days**), change in number of occupants
  if costs depend on it, change of tenant, change of use (**15 days** each).

## Energy renovation and co-financing

- Energy renovation, renewable systems, façades, lifts and taking a loan for maintenance are
  management decisions taken by **simple majority** (čl. 39(1), (3)); investment maintenance under
  čl. 27 and converting common parts need the **qualified (80 %)** majority (čl. 39(4)).
- The association must insure common parts against basic risks through the manager (čl. 30).
- State co-finances **one third** of lift/accessibility installation in existing buildings (čl. 21)
  and façade renovation in historic city centres, with the local unit paying another third (čl. 22).
- EU/national energy-renovation calls → `croatia:hr-eu-funding`.

## Buying a flat: what to ask the manager

- Certificate of no arrears (pričuva, utilities) for the unit.
- Current reserve amount, account balance, planned works, loans taken by the building.
- Co-owners' agreement and house rules; any short-term-rental consents or disputes.
- Insurance of common parts; recent owners' decisions (special levies).
- Whether the building is registered in the Registar zajednica suvlasnika.
