# Taxes and costs 2026

Status: researched 2026-10-25. Not tax advice. Calculations produced from this file must
start with the PREDLOŽAK / TEMPLATE line and be labelled "estimate only".

## 1. Buying: RETT or VAT, never both

| Situation | Tax | Who pays | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resale between individuals, or any sale not subject to VAT | **Porez na promet nekretnina 3 %** of market value | buyer (stjecatelj) | ZPPN čl. 6, 12 |
| First supply of a new building (before first occupancy, or within 2 years of first occupancy) by a VAT payer | **PDV 25 %** in the price; no RETT | seller charges, buyer bears | ZPPN čl. 5(2); Zakon o PDV-u čl. 40(1)(j) |
| Building land from a VAT payer | PDV 25 % | – | Zakon o PDV-u čl. 40(1)(j) (land other than building land is exempt) |
| Older building between VAT payers | exempt, with option to tax (reverse charge) | – | Zakon o PDV-u čl. 40(4) — **VERIFY** paragraph |

- **No general first-home exemption from RETT** has existed since 1 Jan 2017 (the 2016 law,
  ZPPN NN 115/16, dropped it; old exemptions survive only as transitional cases, čl. 33, 35).
  Instead there is a **refund** (below).
- **Exemptions** that still exist (ZPPN čl. 13–15), most relevant:
  - spouses, descendants and ancestors (direct line), adoptive parents/children receiving by
    inheritance or gift (čl. 15(1));
  - ex-spouses settling property (čl. 15(3));
  - partition of co-ownership into separate parts (čl. 13(8));
  - contribution of real estate into a company's capital, mergers and splits (čl. 14),
    unless sham (čl. 14(3));
  - lifetime maintenance contracts: 5 % reduction per year of maintenance (čl. 8(2)).
- **Base** is market value (čl. 9). Porezna accepts the contract price if close to market,
  otherwise estimates from comparable sales (čl. 9(4)–(5)) or an expert (čl. 10).
- **Procedure**: the notary sends the contract within 30 days (čl. 18); without a notary the buyer
  files a Prijava prometa nekretnina within 30 days (čl. 19(2)); pay within 15 days of the
  rješenje (čl. 26). Liability arises at signing (čl. 16(1)); for foreigners needing consent,
  at consent (čl. 16(5)).
- RETT revenue goes to the municipality where the property is (čl. 2).

### First-home refund ("povrat poreza za prvu nekretninu")

Zakon o priuštivom stanovanju (NN 45/26, in force 7 May 2026) čl. 62–66, replacing the Zakon
o društveno poticanoj stanogradnji (čl. 100); the rulebook Pravilnik o potporama za stjecanje
prve stambene nekretnine (NN 86/25) stays in force until a new one is adopted (čl. 98(3)).
Administered by APN (apn.hr/povrat-poreza).

| Condition | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who | adult **Croatian citizen**, **not older than 45** (čl. 65(1)) |
| Contract | signed after 1 Jan 2025 (čl. 65(1)) |
| First home | no other adequate home; an inadequate one must be sold within 24 months (apn.hr FAQ) |
| Residence | applicant and close family registered **and living** at the address (čl. 65(1)) |
| Tax paid | RETT or VAT paid in full first (čl. 65(1)) |
| Area | adequate home: 35 m² for one person + 10 m² per extra household member, +50 % for a house (čl. 12(3)); tolerance 5 m² (apn.hr FAQ) |
| Price | excluded if the price per m² is more than 50 % above the average price the ministry publishes for the area (čl. 65(3), 66(1)) |
| Amount | RETT: refund up to the tax, max 3 % of the base; VAT: 50 % of the VAT, max 12.5 % of the base (čl. 66(5)–(6)); calculated on the eligible area × average price (apn.hr) |
| Deadline | within **24 months** of acquiring ownership (čl. 64(1)) |
| Clawback | repay with default interest if within 5 years of payment the property is sold, rented out, or the family stops living there (čl. 66(7)), with exceptions such as death, serious illness, divorce, birth of a child, job move (čl. 66(8)) |

Foreigners and Croatians over 45 are not eligible.

## 2. Selling: income tax on the gain

Source: porezna-uprava.gov.hr, *Dohodak od otuđenja nekretnina i imovinskih prava*;
Zakon o porezu na dohodak (ZPD).

- **Taxable** if sold within **2 years** of acquisition (date of purchase contract; for self-built
  property, when it became usable).
- **Also taxable**: selling more than 3 properties of the same type within 5 years (each flat
  counts; plots up to 250 m² each and 1,000 m² total are excluded).
- **Not taxable**: sold after 2 years; the property was the seller's or dependent family's home
  (address registration alone does not prove it); transfers between spouses/close relatives,
  divorce settlements, inheritance-related transfers, expropriation.
- **Gift then sale**: if a gifted property is sold within 2 years of the **donor's** acquisition,
  the seller is taxed using the donor's date and value.
- **Rate: 24 %** of the gain. Gain = sale price at market value − acquisition value indexed by
  industrial producer prices − documented investment and selling costs.
- Paid by rješenje within 15 days of delivery. Losses can offset only same-year gains.
- Selling as a business (obrt/d.o.o.) is taxed differently → `croatia:hr-business-admin`.

## 3. Owning

### Porez na nekretnine (property tax, from 2025)

Zakon o lokalnim porezima (ZLP), amended NN 152/24 (in force 1 Jan 2025).

- **Mandatory** in every city/municipality (čl. 20(1)). Replaced the holiday-home tax (porez na
  kuće za odmor); old holiday-home decisions were deemed property-tax decisions where not
  updated (NN 152/24 prijelazna čl. 12(2)).
- **Object**: residential buildings, flats and other spaces intended for living (čl. 2(1)(12)).
  Business premises with a commercial coefficient in the komunalna naknada decision are not
  "nekretnina" for this tax.
- **Taxpayer**: owner on **31 March** (čl. 25(1)); new builds from the use permit (čl. 25(3)).
- **Rate**: **0.60–8.00 €/m²** of useful area per year, set by the local council; 0.60 if no
  decision (čl. 26). May vary by zone and building age. Example: Zagreb announced an increase
  from 5 to 8 €/m² from 1 Jan 2027 (proposal in public consultation, Sept–Oct 2026, **VERIFY**
  the adopted decision); several coastal towns are at or near 8 €/m².
- **Exempt** (čl. 27(1)): used for **permanent living**; let under a **long-term residential
  lease** (at least 10 months in the year, čl. 27(5)); public/institutional use; company stock for
  sale or repossessed (under 6 months); uninhabitable after a disaster or because residential use
  is impossible (no infrastructure, čl. 27(6)–(7)); a tourist host's own permanent home.
  Councils may exempt socially vulnerable citizens (čl. 27(2)).
- **Proof**: permanent living must be proven on request; registered address alone is **not**
  enough (čl. 27(3)); the tax office may use utility consumption data (čl. 27(4)).
- **Admin**: rješenje each year based on the status on 31 March (čl. 49); pay within 15 days;
  owners must report changes by 31 March (čl. 49a), fine 1,000–6,630 € for not reporting (čl. 56).
- **Revenue**: 80 % municipality, 20 % county (čl. 28).
- Tourist-rented flats (rješenje for household catering) are generally **taxed** unless they are
  the host's permanent home → `croatia:hr-tourism-rentals`.

### Komunalna naknada (utility fee)

- Annual fee to the city for communal infrastructure, paid by the owner (or the user if agreed
  and reported), based on m², zone and use coefficient; set by each city's decision under the
  Zakon o komunalnom gospodarstvu. Applies whether or not property tax applies.
  **VERIFY** current article numbers (ZKG NN 68/18 as amended) and the city's decision.

### Other recurring costs (apartments)

- Pričuva (reserve) to the building: minimum ~0.36 €/m² per month (ZUOZ čl. 29), often more.
- Manager's fee, building insurance, utilities, waste fee, water communal charges.

## 4. Renting out (long term) — tax summary

Details in `renting-long-term.md`. Source: porezna-uprava.gov.hr, *Dohodak od najma*.

- Rate **12 %** on rent minus **30 %** flat expenses (effective ≈ 8.4 % of gross rent).
- Paid monthly per rješenje (by the last day of the month). Register the contract within **8 days**.
- Example 2026: rent 700 €/month → 8,400 €/year; base 70 % = 5,880 €; tax 12 % = **705.60 €/year**
  (58.80 €/month). Estimate only.
- Short-term tourist rental is taxed by a flat amount per bed (100–300 € in category I areas)
  → `croatia:hr-tourism-rentals`.

### Worked example (estimate only)

Resale flat, price 200,000 €, cash, agency paid by buyer at 3 % + PDV:
RETT 6,000 € + agency 7,500 € (6,000 + 25 % VAT) + notary ~50–400 € + court fees ~40 € +
lawyer (optional) ~1,000–2,000 € ⇒ roughly **13,600–16,000 €** on top of the price.
Same flat bought new from an investor: no RETT (VAT is in the price); a Croatian buyer under 45
buying a first home may get back 50 % of the VAT within the caps.
