# Buying property in Croatia, step by step

Status: researched 2026-10-25. Not legal advice; see the disclaimer rules in SKILL.md.

Abbreviations: ZK = zemljišna knjiga (land register); ZZK = Zakon o zemljišnim knjigama
(NN 63/19, 128/22, 155/23, 127/24); ZOO = Zakon o obveznim odnosima; ZPPN = Zakon o porezu na
promet nekretnina (NN 115/16, 106/18); RETT = real estate transfer tax (porez na promet nekretnina).

## Overview

| Step | What | Who | Typical time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pin facts: buyer status, financing, first home, new build vs resale | buyer | – |
| 2 | Get OIB (foreigners), pre-approval from bank | buyer, bank | days–weeks |
| 3 | Desk due diligence: ZK izvadak, katastar, permits, energy certificate, debts | buyer / lawyer / agent | 1–2 weeks |
| 4 | Offer, negotiation, **predugovor** with **kapara** (optional) | both, lawyer | – |
| 5 | Valuation (procjena) for the bank, loan approval | bank, sudski vještak/procjenitelj | 1–4 weeks |
| 6 | **Kupoprodajni ugovor**, notarised signatures (ovjera potpisa) or solemnizacija | both, notary | 1 day |
| 7 | Payment against **tabularna isprava** / via notary deposit or bank | both, bank, notary | same day–weeks |
| 8 | **Uknjižba** (registration) filed at the ZK court, usually e-filed by the notary | notary/lawyer | days–months |
| 9 | RETT rješenje from Porezna, pay within 15 days | buyer | weeks–months |
| 10 | Handover (primopredaja), utilities, building manager, property-tax report | both | – |

## Step 1–2: preparation

- **OIB**: every party must have one; the notary reports it to Porezna (ZPPN čl. 18).
  Foreigners get it from Porezna or a consulate → `croatia:hr-identifiers`.
- **Third-country buyers** need ministry consent before registration; build that time into the
  contract → `foreigners.md`.
- **Mortgage**: HNB limits DSTI 45 % and LTV 90 % (see `renting-long-term.md` → *Mortgages*).
  The bank will require a valuation by a court expert and a lien (hipoteka/založno pravo) on the
  property, usually established by a notarised (solemnised) loan contract.

## Step 3: desk due diligence (before paying anything)

Full list in `checklists.md`. The minimum:

1. **ZK izvadak** (free on oss.uredjenazemlja.hr): seller registered as owner in list B, list C
   clear or with encumbrances to be deleted at closing, no *plomba* (pending filing), no
   zabilježba of a lawsuit, enforcement or pre-emption right. See `land-registry.md`.
2. **Katastar**: plot number, area and buildings drawn match the ZK and reality.
3. **Building legality**: uporabna dozvola, rješenje o izvedenom stanju (legalisation) or proof the
   building predates 15 Feb 1968. See `legalisation-energy.md`.
4. **Energy certificate**: the seller must have it and show it before the contract (Zakon o
   energetskoj učinkovitosti u zgradarstvu čl. 14).
5. **Flat in a building**: etažiranje done (flat is a separate unit in ZK or KPU), manager's
   certificate of no pričuva/utility arrears, building decisions on big works and loans.
6. **Land**: urbanistic status (inside the building zone? information from the city's planning
   office or ISPU), access road, utilities. Agricultural land → state pre-emption rules.
7. **Seller capacity**: all co-owners sign; spouses (bračna stečevina may exist even if only one
   spouse is registered); heirs only after the inheritance decision is registered; powers of
   attorney notarised and recent.

## Step 4: predugovor and kapara

- **Predugovor** (ZOO čl. 268) is a contract obliging the parties to conclude the main contract
  later. It must be in the form required for the main contract (written, for real estate)
  and contain its essential elements (property, price, deadline), otherwise it may be
  unenforceable. **VERIFY** the exact form/content rule in ZOO čl. 268 st. 2–3 before relying on it.
- Include: exact ZK data (k.o., čestica, ZK uložak / poduložak, etaža), price and payment
  schedule, deadline for the main contract, what happens to the kapara, seller's duty to deliver
  a clear title and energy certificate, condition precedent for loan approval or ministry
  consent (foreigners), who pays which cost.
- **Kapara** (ZOO čl. 303–305):
  - On fulfilment it is returned or credited to the price (čl. 303(2)).
  - Unless agreed otherwise, the payer cannot simply walk away by leaving the kapara (čl. 303(3)).
  - If the **payer** is at fault for non-performance, the recipient may demand performance and
    damages, or keep the kapara (čl. 304(1)).
  - If the **recipient** is at fault, the payer may demand performance, or return of **double**
    the kapara (čl. 304(2)).
  - On partial performance the kapara cannot be kept; the creditor can demand the rest (čl. 305).
  - Market practice: 10 % of the price. Not a legal figure.
- Alternative: **predujam** (advance payment) without kapara effects, or **odustatnina** (an
  agreed walk-away fee). Name the payment explicitly.
- A **predbilježba** (provisional entry) or a zabilježba of the predugovor is not generally
  available for a predugovor; protect the buyer with a short deadline and the seller's
  warranty instead. **VERIFY** with a notary whether a zabilježba is possible in the specific case.

## Step 6: the purchase contract (kupoprodajni ugovor)

- Must be written; signatures notarised (ovjera potpisa) for registration (ZZK čl. 57).
  Solemnizacija (notarial confirmation) or a javnobilježnički akt adds enforceability and a
  notary's check of the content; banks usually require solemnisation of the loan contract.
- Essential content: parties with OIB, exact ZK designation, price in euro and payment terms,
  **tabularna isprava / clausula intabulandi** (seller's explicit consent to registration of the
  buyer's ownership, ZZK čl. 59(1)(b)), warranty against legal defects (pravni nedostaci) and the
  encumbrances to be deleted, handover date and condition, energy certificate statement,
  who pays RETT (by law the buyer, ZPPN čl. 6; if the seller takes it over contractually he is
  jointly liable, čl. 27).
- **Keep the clausula separate** when the price is paid in instalments or by loan: the seller
  signs a separate notarised izjava (tabularna isprava) that is handed to the buyer, the bank or
  the notary only when the price is paid. This is the main protection for the seller.

## Step 7: paying safely (escrow-like options)

Croatia has no statutory escrow for private sales. Common options:

| Option | How it works | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Polog kod javnog bilježnika** (notary deposit) | buyer pays the price (or the clausula is deposited) with the notary; notary releases to the seller when conditions are met (e.g. ZK entry with priority, deletion of mortgage) | notary fee by tariff (Pravilnik o privremenoj javnobilježničkoj tarifi, Tar. 32–33, value scale of Tar. 12); **VERIFY** current euro amounts |
| Bank loan paid directly to seller | bank pays after registration of its lien, or after the plomba with priority | usual for mortgages |
| Clausula held by notary | seller deposits the tabularna isprava with the notary; released on payment | cheap, common |
| Simultaneous signing and transfer | buyer pays at signing and immediately e-files the uknjižba via the notary | priority is set by the time of filing (ZZK čl. 50) |

Never pay the full price before you have (a) a current clean ZK izvadak and (b) either a
clausula intabulandi in hand or a filing that secures your priority.

## Step 8: registration (uknjižba)

- The notary or lawyer e-files the application; a *plomba* is entered immediately with date and
  time (ZZK čl. 108) and secures priority (čl. 50).
- Court fees: 6.64 € filing + 26.54 € for the decision per right, 50 % off the filing fee if
  e-filed; 5× if filed more than 60 days after the conditions are met (gov.hr; sudovi.hr).
- Then check that the plomba disappears and the buyer appears in list B (`land-registry.md`).

## Step 9: RETT

- The notary sends the contract to Porezna within 30 days (ZPPN čl. 18). If no notary was
  involved, the buyer files the Prijava prometa nekretnina within 30 days (čl. 19(2)).
- Porezna issues a rješenje; pay within 15 days (čl. 26). It may reassess the price to market
  value (čl. 9(4)).
- Tax liability arises when the contract is signed (čl. 16(1)); for foreigners needing consent,
  when the consent is obtained (čl. 16(5)).
- First-home refund (Croatian citizens under 45) → `taxes-costs-2026.md`.

## Step 10: after closing

- Handover record (zapisnik o primopredaji) with meter readings; transfer utility contracts.
- Tell the building's predstavnik suvlasnika about the ownership change within 30 days
  (ZUOZ čl. 41(1)(4)).
- Property tax: report changes to the tax authority by 31 March (ZLP čl. 49a); prove permanent
  living if exempt.
- Komunalna naknada: the new owner registers with the city.

## Selling: the seller's side in brief

- Get the energy certificate before advertising; state the class in the ad.
- Clear the title: delete paid-off mortgages (brisovno očitovanje from the bank), regulate
  katastar/ZK mismatch, have the legalisation decision registered.
- Keep the clausula separate until fully paid.
- Income tax on the gain: see `taxes-costs-2026.md` (2-year rule, own-home exemption).
- Agency: written contract, fixed term (12 months if none agreed), no advance fee
  (Zakon o posredovanju u prometu nekretnina NN 69/26 čl. 17, 29).
