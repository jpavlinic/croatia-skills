# Checklists and red flags

Status: researched 2026-10-25. Any checklist given to a user starts with the PREDLOŽAK /
TEMPLATE line from SKILL.md. A fillable version is in `assets/buyer-due-diligence-checklist.md`.

## Buyer due diligence

### Title and registers
- [ ] Current **ZK izvadak** (oss.uredjenazemlja.hr): seller(s) in list B with shares totalling 1/1.
- [ ] List C: every mortgage, easement, usufruct, pre-emption, lease, enforcement noted; plan for
      deletion at closing (bank's brisovno očitovanje).
- [ ] No **plomba** (pending filing) and no zabilježba of a lawsuit or enforcement.
- [ ] Flat is a separate unit (etažiranje); if only KPU, get legal advice.
- [ ] **Cadastre** matches ZK: plot, area, buildings drawn; no survey (izmjera) dispute.
- [ ] Chain of title explained (inheritance decision registered, old contracts registered).
- [ ] Seller's identity and OIB match; spouse/partner consent where marital property may exist;
      powers of attorney notarised and recent (≤ 1 year if general, ZZK čl. 58).

### Building and permits
- [ ] Uporabna dozvola, rješenje o izvedenom stanju, or pre-15 Feb 1968 status for the **whole**
      building as it is now (`legalisation-energy.md`).
- [ ] No illegal extensions (closed balconies, extra floor, attic conversion) beyond the documents.
- [ ] **Energy certificate** shown before signing; class consistent with the ad.
- [ ] For land: planning status (inside the building zone? zoning information from the city), access
      road, utilities; agricultural land → state pre-emption offer (ZPZ čl. 71).

### Building (flats)
- [ ] Manager's certificate: no arrears for pričuva and utilities.
- [ ] Reserve amount, planned works, building loans, special levies, insurance.
- [ ] Co-owners' agreement, house rules; short-term-rental consent if you plan tourist rental
      (ZUOZ čl. 34).

### Money and contract
- [ ] Financing: bank pre-approval within HNB limits (DSTI 45 %, LTV 90 %); costs budgeted
      (RETT 3 %, agency, notary, lawyer, fees).
- [ ] Predugovor: exact ZK data, price, deadline, kapara vs advance, conditions (loan, consent).
- [ ] Purchase contract: clausula intabulandi (or separate tabularna isprava held in escrow),
      warranties against legal defects, handover date, who pays RETT.
- [ ] Payment only against a clean ZK check on the day **and** a secured registration route
      (clausula in hand, notary deposit, or immediate e-filing with priority).
- [ ] Uknjižba filed within 60 days (fees ×5 after that); check the result.
- [ ] RETT rješenje paid within 15 days; first-home refund applied for within 24 months
      (Croatian citizens ≤ 45).
- [ ] After handover: utilities, manager notified (30 days), property-tax status reported by 31 March.

### Foreign buyers (extra)
- [ ] OIB; EU/EEA vs third-country status; reciprocity checked; ministry consent timeline in the
      contract (`foreigners.md`).
- [ ] Interpreter for signing if needed.

## Seller checklist

- [ ] Energy certificate before advertising; class in every ad.
- [ ] Clean title: registered as owner, old mortgages deleted or deletion arranged, legalisation
      decision registered, cadastre and ZK aligned.
- [ ] Manager's no-arrears certificate ready.
- [ ] Agency contract in writing, fixed term, exclusivity understood, commission and who pays
      (no advance fee; Zakon o posredovanju čl. 17–18, 29–30).
- [ ] Kapara terms clear; keep the **clausula intabulandi separate** until fully paid.
- [ ] Income tax check: sold within 2 years of acquisition and not own home → 24 % on gain; more
      than 3 same-type properties in 5 years → taxable.
- [ ] After sale: cancel utilities, report change to property-tax authority by 31 March.

## Landlord checklist (long-term)

- [ ] Energy certificate; class in the ad.
- [ ] Written contract with ZNS čl. 5 elements; deposit terms; handover record with photos.
- [ ] Consider notarial solemnisation (direct enforceability).
- [ ] File the contract with Porezna within **8 days** and with the city's housing department
      (ZNS čl. 26).
- [ ] Pay 12 % tax monthly per rješenje; keep records.
- [ ] Tell the building representative about the tenant within 15 days (ZUOZ čl. 41).
- [ ] Fixed term: diary the 30-day non-renewal notice (ZNS čl. 25).
- [ ] Property tax: flat let ≥ 10 months/year is exempt (ZLP čl. 27(5)).

## Red flags

| Red flag | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Seller is not in list B ("I'll register it later", "it's my late father's") | you cannot acquire title from a non-registered person without the chain being fixed first |
| Plomba or zabilježba spora on the folio | pending transfer, lawsuit or enforcement may take priority over you |
| "Legalizacija u tijeku" (legalisation pending) | not legal yet; may be rejected, and a final rejection can no longer be re-filed |
| Building or extension not in the cadastre/ZK | cannot be registered or mortgaged; possible demolition order |
| Request to pay most of the price in cash or before the contract, or to a third party's account | fraud and money-laundering risk; price below market also triggers RETT reassessment |
| Kapara far above ~10 % with no clausula and no deadline | you bear the risk if the seller cannot deliver title |
| Agent asking for an advance fee or a fee for a viewing | prohibited (Zakon o posredovanju NN 69/26 čl. 23, 29) |
| Agent not in the HGK register / no written contract | unlicensed brokerage; no insurance cover |
| No energy certificate ("not needed for small flats") | wrong for flats; misdemeanour for owner and agent |
| Flat used for short-term rental, no co-owner consent | consent must be collected by 1 Jan 2030; neighbours may block |
| Large unpaid pričuva or building loan not disclosed | you inherit the building's financial position |
| Agricultural land sold to a third-country buyer, or large plot without state offer | contract void (ZPZ čl. 71(7)); foreigners cannot acquire |
| Power of attorney from abroad without apostille/certification | ZK will reject; delays |
