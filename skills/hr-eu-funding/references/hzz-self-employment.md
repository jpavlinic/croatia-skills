# HZZ self-employment subsidy 2026 (potpora za samozapošljavanje)

Source: HZZ, `https://mjere.hzz.hr/mjere/potpore-za-samozaposljavanje-2026/` (read
2026-10-25) and "Uvjeti i načini korištenja sredstava za provođenje mjera u 2026. godini"
(mjere.hzz.hr, Jan 2026). It is **de minimis aid** (potpora male vrijednosti, Reg.
2023/2831), co-financed by the EU (ESF+). Conditions change every January: for 2027,
re-check everything below.

**Status on 2026-10-25**: requests were accepted **until 30 Sep 2026 or until funds ran
out**. The 2026 window is therefore closed. New conditions are normally published in
January (VERIFY for 2027). Use the 2026 rules only to prepare, and say so.

## 1. Who can apply

- An **unemployed person registered with HZZ** (nezaposlena osoba u evidenciji HZZ-a) who
  will register a new business: obrt, trgovačko društvo (d.o.o./j.d.o.o.), samostalna
  djelatnost (e.g. free profession) or ustanova. Business registration
  (`croatia:hr-business-admin`) happens **after** approval; payment requires the
  registration decision.
- Applies online at `https://mjere.hzz.hr` with **NIAS (e-Građani)** login.
- If others co-found the company, the applicant must hold the **majority share** and be
  authorised to represent it; in a joint obrt the applicant must be the holder (nositelj).
- Third-country nationals: only with long-term residence or permanent stay approved by
  the Ministry of the Interior (Zakon o tržištu rada čl. 15).

### Excluded applicants (2026)
- Blocked private accounts or unexecuted payment orders (FINA register), or a simple
  consumer bankruptcy procedure.
- Anyone who in the **24 months** before applying was: holder/member of an OPG or
  self-supply farm, owner of an obrt, owner/founder of a company, holder/director/
  responsible person in another self-employed activity, cooperative member, or did home
  crafts or a secondary occupation.
- People dismissed for personal reasons (osobno uvjetovani otkaz) in the same or similar
  activity, unless since then long-term unemployed or inactive > 12 months.
- Anyone who has **ever** used the self-employment subsidy.
- Taking over an existing business.
- If the applicant had a business more than 24 months ago, all public debts from it must
  be paid.

## 2. Amounts 2026 (maximum per applicant)

| Business form / NKD 2025 area | Maximum |
|---|---|
| **Obrt with flat-rate taxation (paušalni obrt)**, any eligible activity | **€7,000** |
| Group 1: C Manufacturing (divisions 25–33); F Construction (except group 43.6) | **€15,000** |
| Group 2: C Manufacturing (divisions 10–24); K Telecommunications, computer programming, consultancy, computing infrastructure and other information service activities | **€10,000** |
| Group 3: D (35), E (36–39), J publishing/broadcasting/content, N professional/scientific/technical (except 69.1), O administrative and support (except 77, 78, 79), Q education, R health and social work, S arts/entertainment/recreation (except 93.19), T other services (except 94, 95.4, 96.4) | **€7,000** |

Group 1–3 amounts apply to obrt taxed on income (porez na dohodak, not flat-rate),
companies, self-employed activity and institutions. The amounts are ceilings; the
final amount depends on the justified investment in the business plan and cost list.

NKD 2025 letters differ from the old NKD 2007 (e.g. K is now telecoms/IT, N
professional activities, O administrative services, Q education, R health). Always map
the planned activity with the official NKD 2025 tables (`croatia:hr-identifiers` for
codes and registers).

Several commercial sites quote "up to €20,000" for 2026. The official 2026 page shows
€15,000 as the highest group ceiling for one applicant; treat €20,000 as **unconfirmed**
(VERIFY whether it refers to combinations or a different year).

### Composition
- **Fixed part: €5,000** for registration and start-up costs, the applicant's own
  full-time employment and usual running costs in the first months (bookkeeping, rent,
  membership fees, admin, delivery, small inventory, materials, work clothes, website,
  advertising). No proof of spending required.
- **Variable part** (the rest up to the ceiling), only for:
  - new equipment needed for the activity with a useful life over one year (machines,
    tools, IT equipment) and services needed to use it (installation, assembly);
  - purchase or lease of licensed IT software;
  - purchase of a franchise.
- **VAT on invoices is not eligible** in the variable part. No changes to the cost list
  after the contract is signed. Prices must be realistic market prices; HZZ may ask for
  market research.

### Excluded activities (NKD 2025)
A agriculture, forestry, fishing; B mining; F group 43.6; G wholesale and retail trade;
H transport and storage; I accommodation and food service; L financial and insurance;
M real estate; N group 69.1 legal activities; O divisions 77, 78, 79; P public
administration; S class 93.19; T division 94, groups 95.4, 96.4; U; V.

## 3. Scoring (max 100, threshold 65)

| Criterion | Max |
|---|---|
| Work experience in the planned activity (documented) | 15 |
| Education or additional training related to the activity | 15 |
| First entrepreneurial experience | 5 |
| HZZ preparatory workshop attended in 2025 or 2026 (`radionice.hzz.hr`) | 5 |
| Completeness of business plan with offers/pro-forma invoices and letters of intent | 15 |
| Development index of the local unit (JLS groups I–IV get 10, V–VIII get 0; Odluka NN 3/2024) | 10 |
| Realistic two-year revenue and cost estimate | 15 |
| Sustainability and competitiveness (adviser's overall assessment) | 10 |
| Innovation (new product/process) | 5 |
| Investment in activities missing on the local market | 5 |

Practical levers: attend the free HZZ workshop (+5), attach offers for every equipment
item and letters of intent from future clients (+15 band), choose a seat in a JLS of
groups I–IV only if it is genuinely where the business will operate (+10), and build a
realistic 24-month projection.

## 4. Documents with the application

- Business plan with cost list (troškovnik) and the planned legal form.
- FINA certificate on blocked accounts / payment-order register in the applicant's name,
  **not older than 7 days** (free on request).
- If previously self-employed through an obrt or free profession: Tax Administration
  certificate of no outstanding debts. If through a company: court register extract.
- HZZ obtains some documents by data exchange.

## 5. Payment and security

- **Lump sum** paid to the business account within 30 days of signing the grant
  contract, after delivery of a **notarised blank promissory note** (bjanko zadužnica)
  within 8 days of signing and the registration decision.
- For a company, members and authorised representatives also sign the promissory note
  as co-debtors (jamci-platci); for other forms another natural person signs as guarantor.
  Guarantors must be Croatian citizens resident in Croatia.

## 6. Obligations (2026 contract)

1. **Keep the business and the applicant's full-time employment for 24 months.**
2. Spend the variable part within the **first 12-month period** as per the cost list;
   prove it within 30 days after that period (invoice in the business name + bank
   statement; photos of equipment over €1,500 with the sticker).
3. Achieve total revenue in the 24 months from pension-insurance registration of at least
   **50 % of the subsidy amount**, shown in official statements (PO-SD for flat-rate obrt;
   P-PPI, DI, DOH for income-tax obrt; GFI-POD for profit-tax payers).
4. Label co-financed equipment worth **€1,500 or more (VAT included)** within 30 days of
   purchase with a plate/sticker (white background, HZZ logo, EU emblem, "Sufinancira
   Europska unija"), kept at least to the end of the co-financed period.
5. All costs paid by bank transfer from the business account; cash is not eligible;
   invoices and delivery before the end of the first 12 months.
6. Being found on the undeclared-work "black list" terminates the contract.

### Repayment
- Unproven or late variable spending: repay that part (+ default interest if not repaid
  within 15 days).
- Business closed, applicant de-registered from employment insurance, or switched to
  part-time **in months 1–12**: repay the **whole** subsidy + statutory default interest.
- Same **in months 13–24**: repay the **variable part + 50 % of the fixed part** + interest.

In short, "12/24 months" means: 12 months to spend the variable part, 24 months to stay
active.

## 7. Interactions to explain

- It is **de minimis**: it reduces the headroom for later de minimis grants (city/county
  grants, FZOEU business calls, HAMAG-BICRO) of the new business and linked enterprises.
- The applicant becomes employed in their own business (compulsory pension and health
  insurance as obrtnik/director). Tax form choice (paušal vs income vs profit tax) changes
  the ceiling and the reporting: coordinate with an accountant (`croatia:hr-business-admin`).
- Unemployment benefit (novčana naknada) normally ends when the person starts insured
  self-employment (VERIFY with HZZ for the individual case).

## 8. Related HZZ measures for employers (2026 snapshot)

Source: `https://mjere.hzz.hr/mjere/potpore-za-zaposljavanje-2026/` (read 2026-10-25).
Details in `croatia:hr-payroll-employment`.

**Potpore za zapošljavanje** (hiring subsidies): monthly flat amount regardless of real
cost, for 12 months (target group 1, disadvantaged) or 12/24 months (groups 2 and 3).

| Monthly flat amount | Primary school or less | Secondary | Higher education |
|---|---|---|---|
| With tax relief, groups 1 and 2 | €420 | €470 | €590 |
| With tax relief, group 3 (persons with disabilities), full time | €680 | €770 | €970 |
| Without tax relief, groups 1 and 2 | €480 | €550 | €690 |
| Without tax relief, group 3, full time | €790 | €900 | €1,130 |
| Minimum gross 1 salary, full time | €1,050 | €1,195.80 | €1,494.75 |

Key conditions: employer founded ≥ 12 months earlier; at least one full-time permanent
employee in the last 3 months (exception: obrt/free profession/OPG without employees can
subsidise one person if the owner has been insured full-time for 6 months); net increase
in headcount; maximum subsidised persons = 50 % of average headcount; no business-reason
dismissals in the previous 6 months; no tax debts (or upravni ugovor in good standing);
not "u poteškoćama"; blank promissory note; keep the average headcount during the
subsidised period or repay everything with interest.

Other 2026 measures listed on mjere.hzz.hr: osposobljavanje na radnom mjestu (on-the-job
training), Posao+ hiring subsidy, measures for young people and first jobs, public works.
Check the catalogue (`https://mjere.hzz.hr/katalog-mjera/`) for current amounts.
