# Writing a strong application

Researched 2026-10-25. Applies to cohesion calls (PKK, ITP, ULJP), national calls and,
with adjustments, EU direct programmes. The call's form (Prijavni obrazac, in eKohezija
or eFZOEU) and its evaluation grid always set the structure: follow it, not this file,
where they differ.

## 1. Read the Upute za prijavitelje (UzP) quickly

UzP documents are long (40–120 pages). Read in this order:

1. **Cover / Sažetak poziva**: reference code, grantor (PT1/PT2), allocation, min/max
   grant, intensity, deadline type.
2. **Prihvatljivost prijavitelja i partnera** (eligibility of applicant/partners) and
   **isključenja** (exclusions).
3. **Prihvatljive aktivnosti** and **prihvatljivi / neprihvatljivi troškovi**, cost limits,
   eligibility period (razdoblje prihvatljivosti), VAT rule, simplified cost options.
4. **Postupak dodjele** (award procedure): open call with fixed deadline, trajni poziv with
   batches (rokovi za podnošenje), restricted call; **kriteriji odabira** and the
   **bodovni prag** (score threshold).
5. **Obvezna dokumentacija** (mandatory documents) and form requirements (signatures,
   formats, language, maximum length).
6. **Pokazatelji** (indicators) the project must contribute to.
7. **Obveze korisnika** (beneficiary duties): procurement annex, visibility, reporting,
   durability. These cost money: price them in.
8. **Prilozi**: draft grant contract (Ugovor o dodjeli bespovratnih sredstava) and Opći
   uvjeti (general conditions), Pravila o financijskim korekcijama, aid scheme.
9. **Izmjene** and **Pitanja i odgovori**: amendments and official Q&A override the text.

Produce a one-page call summary for the user (template in `assets/projektni-prijedlog-predlozak.md`, section 0).

## 2. Structure of the proposal (intervention logic)

Write the project as one chain; evaluators check that each link supports the next.

1. **Problem and need** (problem i potreba): evidence with numbers (market data, own
   financials, capacity limits, energy use, unemployment in the area). Cite sources.
2. **Objective** (cilj): one general objective aligned with the call's specific objective
   (specifični cilj), and 1–3 specific project objectives.
3. **Target groups** (ciljane skupine) and end users (krajnji korisnici).
4. **Activities** (aktivnosti) and **elements** (elementi projekta): each with tasks,
   responsible person, duration, deliverables. Include mandatory activities (project
   management, visibility).
5. **Results and indicators** (rezultati i pokazatelji): output indicators (pokazatelji
   ostvarenja) and result indicators (pokazatelji rezultata) **from the call's list**,
   with baseline (polazna vrijednost), target (ciljna vrijednost), unit, verification
   source, and date. Do not over-promise: unmet indicators lead to financial corrections.
6. **Budget** (proračun): every cost linked to an activity, priced from offers, within the
   call's limits; financing plan (bespovratna sredstva + vlastita sredstva + other).
7. **Implementation capacity** (provedbeni kapaciteti): team, experience, previous EU
   projects, financial capacity (own funds, cash flow).
8. **Risks** (rizici) and mitigation.
9. **Sustainability** (održivost): financial (who pays after the grant), institutional,
   environmental; durability period obligations.
10. **Horizontal principles** (horizontalna načela): equality between women and men,
    non-discrimination and accessibility (incl. UN CRPD), sustainable development; plus
    **DNSH** and climate proofing where required.

## 4. Budget rules

- **Eligible cost basics** (CPR 2021/1060 Art. 63–64 and the call): necessary for the
  project, incurred and paid within the eligibility period, by the beneficiary, at market
  prices, documented (invoice + proof of payment from the business account), recorded in
  the books, compliant with procurement rules. Cash payments are usually ineligible.
- **Market price evidence**: offers (ponude) or market research (istraživanje tržišta);
  many calls require 2–3 offers above a value or a procurement procedure after award.
- **VAT (PDV)**: CPR Art. 64(1)(c) allows VAT as eligible for operations below €5 m total
  cost, and above €5 m only if non-recoverable. Croatian calls usually narrow this: VAT
  is eligible **only if the beneficiary cannot recover it** (not in the VAT system, or
  input VAT not deductible). Read the UzP and the national rule (Pravilnik o
  prihvatljivosti izdataka, VERIFY current version). HZZ self-employment: VAT on invoices
  is not eligible in the variable part.
- **Simplified cost options** (CPR Art. 53–56): e.g. flat rate up to 7 % of eligible
  direct costs for indirect costs (Art. 54(a)), up to 15 % of direct staff costs for
  indirect costs (Art. 54(b)), up to 40 % of direct staff costs for remaining costs
  (Art. 56), hourly staff rate = latest annual gross employment costs / 1,720 hours
  (Art. 55). Use only the options the call offers.
- **Typical ineligible items**: interest, fines, exchange losses, recoverable VAT, used
  equipment (often), land above 10 % of eligible costs, in-kind contributions unless
  allowed, costs before the eligibility date, routine operating costs, passenger cars
  (often), leasing without ownership transfer (often), costs of preparing the application
  unless explicitly eligible.
- **Intensity**: grant = eligible costs × intensity, capped by max grant and by state-aid
  rules (de minimis headroom, GBER regional map). Ineligible costs and the unfunded share
  are paid by the beneficiary.
- **Cash flow**: show how the beneficiary pre-finances (own funds, bank or HBOR/HAMAG loan)
  until reimbursement; evaluators score financial capacity.

## 5. Common rejection reasons

1. Applicant or project ineligible (size with linked enterprises, NKD, location, company age).
2. Missing or invalid mandatory document; unsigned forms; old tax certificate.
3. Works started / binding order before submission (incentive effect).
4. Costs outside eligible categories, unrealistic prices, no offers.
5. Weak link between problem, activities and indicators; indicators not from the call.
6. Insufficient financial capacity (own share not credible).
7. De minimis ceiling exceeded once linked companies are counted.
8. Below the score threshold, or funds exhausted before the proposal was reached (trajni
   poziv: timing matters).
9. Late submission or technical failure on the deadline day (submit at least 2 days early;
   the NPOO portal itself advised this).
10. Ignoring Q&A and amendments.

## 6. Writing style

- Use the call's exact terms (specifični cilj, pokazatelj names) and number the sections
  to match the evaluation grid so evaluators can find each criterion.
- Concrete, measurable, short sentences; no marketing language. Every claim with a number
  should have a source.
- Croatian text: standard Croatian, formal register, no anglicisms where a Croatian term
  exists; apply `croatia:croatian-language`. Address the grantor neutrally; address one
  person with capital **Vi**.
- Keep a document list with file names that match the call's numbering (Prilog 1, 2, …).

## 7. Konzultanti (consultants)

Neutral guidance; never recommend a specific firm.

- **What they do**: scan calls, check eligibility, write the proposal and business plan,
  prepare the budget and annexes, submit through the system under a power of attorney,
  manage procurement, payment claims and reports after the award.
- **Fees** (market practice, no official tariff, VERIFY by asking several offers): a fixed
  preparation fee and/or a **success fee** as a percentage of the approved grant
  (commonly quoted in single-digit to low double-digit percent), plus separate fees for
  implementation management. Application preparation costs are usually **not eligible**
  under the grant unless the call says so; implementation management sometimes is.
- **Red flags**: guaranteed approval or "connections"; advice to inflate prices or split
  purchases; asking you to start works or sign binding orders before the call allows;
  asking you to sign blank or backdated documents; success fee due before the grant is
  paid with no clause on recovery or corrections; no written contract; refusing to give
  you access to your own eKohezija/eFZOEU account and documents; a consultant who is also
  the supplier of the funded equipment (conflict of interest under NOJN rules).
- **Contract points**: scope, deliverables, deadlines, fee trigger (approval vs payment),
  who bears the cost of corrections caused by the consultant's errors, confidentiality,
  GDPR (`croatia:hr-legal-compliance`), document handover.
- The beneficiary stays legally responsible for every statement signed, even if written
  by a consultant.
