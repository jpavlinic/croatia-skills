# After the award: staying compliant

Researched 2026-10-25. The grant agreement (Ugovor o dodjeli bespovratnih sredstava),
its Opći uvjeti and the call's annexes are binding and override this summary. For cohesion
funds the legal base is the Common Provisions Regulation (CPR, Reg. (EU) 2021/1060).

## 1. Procurement for beneficiaries that are not contracting authorities (NOJN)

Private companies, crafts, OPGs and most NGOs are **NOJN** (neobveznici Zakona o javnoj
nabavi). Public bodies, cities and bodies governed by public law follow the Zakon o javnoj
nabavi (ZJN) instead.

NOJN rules come from the call itself: an annex usually titled **"Postupci nabave za osobe
koje nisu obveznici Zakona o javnoj nabavi"**. Each ministry's annex can differ. Read the
one attached to *your* call. A typical 2021–2027 annex (example: Ministry of Demography and
Migration call, Prilog 7) contains:

| Procedure | Goods and services | Works |
|---|---|---|
| Direktna pogodba (direct award) | below €26,540 | below €66,360 |
| Javno nadmetanje (open tender) | €26,540 or more | €66,360 or more |

Values excluding VAT, at project level (for partner projects, the joint value counts).

- These thresholds mirror the old ZJN simple-procurement thresholds. **ZJN amendment
  NN 48/2026** raises the ZJN simple-procurement thresholds to **€50,000** (goods/services)
  and **€100,000** (works) from **1 Sep 2026**. Whether a given call's NOJN annex follows
  the new values depends on that annex and any amendment: **VERIFY per call**; never assume.
- **No artificial splitting** of a procurement to stay under a threshold; breaches
  normally lead to a financial correction.
- **Conflict of interest**: persons authorised to represent the NOJN sign a declaration;
  offers from economic operators in a conflict of interest must be rejected. Typical
  conflicts: supplier owned by the beneficiary's owners, directors or their relatives.
- **Open tender** (javno nadmetanje): publish a tender notice (Obavijest o nadmetanju) and
  tender documents (Dokumentacija za nadmetanje); minimum 10 days for offers in the example
  annex; clarifications published, deadlines extended after significant changes; a
  procurement committee opens and evaluates offers, writes minutes, issues a selection
  decision (Odluka o odabiru), publishes it, and signs the contract. Publication channel
  (beneficiary website, eKohezija portal, EOJN RH) depends on the annex: VERIFY.
- SP ZPP (APPRRR) projects have their own procurement rules for private beneficiaries
  (Pravilnik on procurement for SP ZPP beneficiaries, VERIFY); a 2026 APPRRR notice says
  EOJN RH must still be used regardless of the new ZJN thresholds, except listed cases.
- Keep the full file: market research, invitations, all offers (including rejected),
  minutes, decisions, contract, delivery notes, acceptance records.

## 2. Reporting and payment claims

- **Zahtjev za nadoknadom sredstava (ZNS)** (payment claim) with invoices, proofs of
  payment, procurement file and progress report, at the frequency in the contract
  (e.g. quarterly or by milestones). In eKohezija the claim is filed online.
- **Advance payment** (predujam) may require a bank guarantee or bill of exchange
  (zadužnica); it is cleared through later claims.
- **Final report** (završno izvješće) and **post-implementation reports** (izvješća nakon
  provedbe) during the durability period, including indicator achievement.
- On-the-spot checks (provjere na licu mjesta) by the intermediate body; audits by the
  Audit Authority (ARPA), the European Commission and the European Court of Auditors; OLAF
  for fraud; EPPO for criminal offences against the EU budget.
- National schemes have their own forms: HZZ requires proof of spending the variable part
  within 30 days after the first 12-month period; FZOEU requires a payment request with
  invoices and proof of bank payment by a fixed date (EnU-6/26: by 30 Jun 2028).

## 3. Visibility (vidljivost)

CPR Art. 47 and 50, and the call's visibility manual:
- Use the **EU emblem with "Sufinancira Europska unija"** (Co-funded by the European
  Union) on all communication, documents and equipment where required.
- Short description of the project, with objectives and results and the EU support, on
  the beneficiary's **website and social media**, if they exist (Art. 50(1)(a)).
- **Plaques or billboards** visible to the public for ERDF/CF operations involving physical
  investment or equipment purchase with total cost above **€500,000**, as soon as works
  start or equipment is installed (Art. 50(1)(c)); other operations: at least one
  printed or electronic display, **minimum A3**, with information on the project
  (Art. 50(1)(d)).
- Operations of strategic importance or above €10 m: a communication event.
- Non-compliance can reduce the grant by **up to 3 %** (Art. 50(3)).
- National examples: HZZ self-employment requires a sticker or plate with the HZZ and EU
  logos and "Sufinancira Europska unija" on co-financed equipment worth €1,500 or more (VAT
  included) within 30 days of purchase; Modernisation Fund calls have their own manual.

## 4. Keeping documents

- Cohesion funds: keep all documents for **5 years from 31 December of the year in which
  the managing authority makes the last payment to the beneficiary** (CPR Art. 82(1)),
  unless the contract sets a longer period; the period is interrupted by legal proceedings
  or a Commission request.
- State aid: grantors keep records 10 years (GBER Art. 12; Reg. 2023/2831 for de minimis),
  so keep your own evidence at least that long if the aid is GBER/de minimis.
- Accounting law retention periods (Zakon o računovodstvu) apply in parallel; take the
  longest.
- Keep originals or certified electronic copies, organised per cost item and procurement.

## 5. Durability (trajnost)

- **CPR Art. 65**: for operations with investment in infrastructure or productive
  investment, repay if within **5 years of the final payment** (or the period in state-aid
  rules) the activity ceases or relocates outside the programme area, ownership of
  infrastructure changes giving undue advantage, or there is a substantial change
  affecting nature, objectives or conditions. Member States may reduce the period to
  **3 years** for investments or jobs created by SMEs. The call states which applies.
- National schemes set their own durability, e.g. HZZ self-employment: keep the business
  and own full-time employment for **24 months**; LAG calls: keep seat/residence in the LAG
  area 5 years from final payment; FZOEU vehicle calls: keep the vehicle for a set period
  (VERIFY per call).
- Durability usually covers: keeping the assets in use for the project purpose, not
  selling or moving them, keeping the jobs/indicators achieved, maintaining insurance.

## 6. Irregularities and repayment risks

- **Nepravilnost** (irregularity): any breach of EU or national law that could harm the
  EU budget (CPR Art. 2(31)). Result: **financial correction** (a percentage or the whole
  amount of the affected cost) or recovery with interest. Calls attach "Pravila o
  financijskim korekcijama" with the rates.
- Most frequent causes for private beneficiaries:
  1. Procurement errors: splitting, missing procedure, conflict of interest, discriminatory
     specifications, changing the contract after award.
  2. Costs outside the eligibility period or not paid from the business account.
  3. Indicators not achieved; durability breached (business closed, asset sold).
  4. Double financing.
  5. Missing visibility.
  6. False declarations (also criminal: subsidy fraud, Kazneni zakon; EU fraud reporting).
- HZZ example (self-employment 2026): closing the business or de-registering from
  pension insurance in months 1–12 means repaying the whole subsidy; in months 13–24 the
  variable part plus 50 % of the fixed part, with statutory default interest.
- Grantors secure repayment with **bjanko zadužnica** (notarised blank promissory note),
  sometimes signed by owners as co-debtors. Explain this to users: personal liability.
- If a problem arises, **inform the grantor early in writing**; voluntary reporting and a
  justified change request are treated better than a finding in an audit.

## 7. Changing the project (izmjene)

- Changes go through the procedure in the contract: usually a written **request for
  amendment** (zahtjev za izmjenom ugovora) before the change is made.
- Minor changes (e.g. small budget shifts between lines within a percentage, timetable
  shifts within the end date, team members) may be notified; substantial changes
  (objectives, indicators, location, ownership, total budget, end date) need an addendum.
- Changes cannot improve the score retroactively in a way that would have changed the
  selection, cannot increase the grant above the contract, and cannot extend beyond the
  programme's final eligibility date.
- HZZ self-employment: no changes to the cost breakdown (troškovnik) after signing.

## 8. Practical compliance calendar (fill per project)

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| Before any purchase | Check the NOJN annex, run procurement, conflict-of-interest declarations |
| Within the visibility deadline | Website text, A3 poster or plaque, stickers on equipment |
| Every claim period | ZNS with invoices, proofs of payment, procurement file, progress report |
| End of implementation | Final claim and report, indicator evidence |
| Each year of durability | Post-implementation report, keep assets and jobs, insurance |
| Until retention end | Keep the full archive; respond to audits |
