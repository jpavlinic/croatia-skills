# EU direct programmes for Croatian applicants

Researched 2026-10-25. These programmes are managed by the European Commission or its
agencies; calls, budgets and rules are set in yearly or two-yearly **work programmes**.
Figures marked VERIFY come from general programme rules and must be checked in the
specific call document. All 2021–2027 programmes end with the current EU budget; the
2028–2034 architecture is still being negotiated (VERIFY before describing it).

## 1. How to apply (common to most programmes)

1. Find the call on the **EU Funding & Tenders Portal**
   (`https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal`). Erasmus+ decentralised
   actions and Interreg have their own systems (see below).
2. Create an **EU Login** and register the organisation to get a **PIC** (Participant
   Identification Code); appoint a **LEAR** (legal entity appointed representative).
3. Read the call conditions, the **topic text** and the **standard application form**
   (Part A administrative, Part B technical narrative). Evaluation is usually Excellence,
   Impact, Quality and efficiency of implementation, each scored with thresholds.
4. Many actions require a **consortium** (e.g. Horizon Europe collaborative actions:
   at least three independent legal entities from three different EU Member States or
   associated countries, at least one from a Member State; VERIFY per topic).
5. Grants are often **lump sums** now; indirect costs typically 25 % flat rate in Horizon
   Europe (VERIFY per action).
6. National help: **National Contact Points (NCP)** for Horizon Europe are coordinated in
   Croatia by the Agency for Mobility and EU Programmes (AMPEU, `https://www.ampeu.hr`)
   (VERIFY current NCP list on the Portal); Enterprise Europe Network partners help SMEs
   find partners.

### Interreg programmes covering Croatia (2021–2027)
Cross-border: Italy–Croatia, Slovenia–Croatia, Hungary–Croatia, IPA Croatia–Serbia,
IPA Croatia–Bosnia and Herzegovina–Montenegro. Transnational: Central Europe, Danube
Region, Euro-MED, IPA ADRION. Interregional: Interreg Europe, URBACT, ESPON, INTERACT.
Most use the **Jems** electronic monitoring system for applications (VERIFY per programme).
Calls are periodic; partnerships are built months in advance.

## 3. Practical notes for Croatian applicants

- **Seal of Excellence**: EIC Accelerator proposals scoring at least 13/15 that are not
  funded get a Seal of Excellence; some national/cohesion calls can fund such projects
  (check eufondovi.gov.hr and HAMAG-BICRO for Seal-based schemes, VERIFY).
- **Co-financing the non-EU share**: some ministries or cities co-finance the own
  contribution of NGOs in EU projects (e.g. the Government Office for NGOs scheme for
  co-financing EU projects of associations, VERIFY current call).
- **Language**: EU direct applications are normally written in English; Erasmus+
  decentralised actions can be submitted in Croatian (VERIFY per action).
- **State aid**: direct EU grants are not state aid and do not count towards de minimis,
  but cohesion and national co-financing of them can be.
- **Success rates** are low for competitive programmes (EIC Accelerator well under 10 %
  overall, VERIFY); advise users to treat them as one option among national schemes.

## 4. EIC Accelerator process (2026)

From eic.ec.europa.eu (read 2026-10-25):
1. **Short proposal** (Step 1): continuous submission, remote evaluation (go/no-go).
2. **Full proposal** (Step 2) at cut-off dates (2026 schedule reported as six cut-offs,
   including 2 Sep and 4 Nov 2026; VERIFY on the EIC site for 2027 dates).
3. **Interview** with a jury (Step 3).
4. Grant agreement and investment due diligence by the EIC Fund (Step 4).
Applicants from third countries may relocate before Step 2 to become eligible. The 2026
work programme also has a separate defence/dual-use strand and STEP Scale-up (VERIFY).
