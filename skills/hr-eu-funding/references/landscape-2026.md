# Funding landscape in Croatia: dated snapshot

Snapshot date: **2026-10-25**. Every row is **VERIFY-current**: open the live page before
telling a user that anything is open, how much it pays, or when it closes. Use this file to
know *where to look* and *what kind of support exists*, not as a list of open calls.

## 1. How Croatian public funding is organised

| Layer | Money source | Typical forms | Who runs calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU shared management (cohesion) 2021–2027 | ERDF, Cohesion Fund, ESF+, JTF | Grants (bespovratna sredstva), financial instruments (loans, guarantees) | Ministries as intermediate bodies (posrednička tijela, PT1), agencies such as SAFU, HAMAG-BICRO, HZZ (PT2) |
| EU agricultural policy | EAGF / EAFRD via SP ZPP 2023–2027 | Investment grants, area payments, LAG (LEADER) calls | Ministry of Agriculture, APPRRR, LAGs |
| EU maritime and fisheries | EMFAF, Programme for Fisheries and Aquaculture 2021–2027 | Grants | Ministry of Agriculture, APPRRR |
| Recovery and Resilience Facility | NPOO 2021–2026 | Grants, HBOR/HAMAG loans | Ministries, HBOR, FZOEU (ending, see §4) |
| Modernisation Fund / ETS auction revenue | EU ETS | Grants | MZOZT with FZOEU |
| National budget and agencies | State budget, own revenue | Grants, subsidies, loans, guarantees, interest subsidies | HZZ, FZOEU, HAMAG-BICRO, HBOR, ministries |
| Local and regional | County, city and municipal budgets | Small de minimis grants, interest subsidies, co-financing EU projects | Counties, cities, development agencies (RRA), LAGs |
| EU direct management | Horizon Europe, Digital Europe, LIFE, Erasmus+, Creative Europe, Single Market Programme, CERV | Competitive grants, often consortia | European Commission and its agencies |
| European Territorial Cooperation | Interreg | Grants to cross-border partnerships | Joint secretariats of each programme |

## 4. Status notes that change advice (2026)

- **NPOO is over for new projects.** All NPOO measures had to be completed by 31 Aug 2026;
  the combined final payment requests (9th and 10th, ≈ €2.7 bn) were due by end of
  September 2026, with EU payments expected by end-2026 (vlada.gov.hr, 31 Aug 2026).
  Beneficiaries still have reporting, durability and audit obligations.
- **Cohesion 2021–2027 is in its main contracting phase.** The Dec 2025 PKK presentation
  reported 119 calls (€3.89 bn) published and 53 more (€1.64 bn) planned to end-2026.
  Spending under 2021–2027 must be completed by end-2029 (n+3 rule, VERIFY per call).
- **GBER (Reg. 651/2014) expires 31 Dec 2026**; a new GBER is expected to apply from
  1 Jan 2027 with a transition period for schemes (VERIFY). Calls under GBER Articles may
  be amended or closed around year end.
- **HZZ and FZOEU** publish new household/individual conditions each year; between
  October and January there is often no open window.
- **MFF 2028–2034**: the Commission's proposal (2025) reshapes programmes (national and
  regional partnership plans, a European Competitiveness Fund). Still in negotiation;
  do not describe it as adopted.

## 5. Matching applicant to likely source

| Applicant | First places to look |
|---|---|
| Unemployed person starting a business | HZZ self-employment subsidy; city start-up grants; HAMAG-BICRO micro loan; HBOR start-up loans |
| Existing micro/small business, equipment or digital | Ministry of Economy PKK calls; ITP if in a target area; county/city de minimis grants; HAMAG-BICRO loans/guarantees; HBOR |
| Manufacturing SME with an innovation | PKK S3/innovation calls; innovation vouchers; HAMAG-BICRO innovation programmes; EIC Accelerator |
| Startup, deep tech | HAMAG-BICRO (AKCELERATOR, B LIGHT, EUREKA/Eurostars); EIC Accelerator; EIC Pathfinder via research partners |
| Employer hiring | HZZ hiring subsidies, on-the-job training, Posao+ (`croatia:hr-payroll-employment`) |
| Farmer / OPG / food processor | APPRRR SP ZPP interventions; LAG calls; HAMAG-BICRO agricultural loans; HBOR |
| Tourism / accommodation | Many SME calls **exclude** NKD accommodation and food service; check ITP/LAG and Ministry of Tourism calls (`croatia:hr-tourism-rentals`) |
| NGO / association | ULJP (ESF+), ministries' and Office for NGOs calls, cities, Erasmus+, CERV, Creative Europe, Interreg as partner |
| Household | FZOEU (heat pump, PV, battery; energy-poverty renovation); city co-financing; `croatia:hr-real-estate` for renovation context |
| Municipality / public body | PKK/ITP infrastructure calls, FZOEU, MZOZT, Interreg (public procurement under ZJN applies) |
