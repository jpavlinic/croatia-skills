# Eligibility basics and state-aid rules

Researched 2026-10-25. The call's Upute za prijavitelje (UzP) and its annexes always
decide. Use this file to understand the terms and to build the checklist.

## 1. SME definition (MSP, mikro, mali i srednji poduzetnik)

Source: Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC, Annex (also Annex I to GBER 651/2014).
Croatian calls also cite the Zakon o poticanju razvoja malog gospodarstva and the Zakon o
računovodstvu for other purposes; for grants the **EU definition** applies.

| Category | Staff (GJR, annual work units) | and | Turnover | or | Balance sheet total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Micro (mikro) | < 10 | | ≤ €2 m | | ≤ €2 m |
| Small (mali) | < 50 | | ≤ €10 m | | ≤ €10 m |
| Medium (srednji) | < 250 | | ≤ €50 m | | ≤ €43 m |

- Headcount is a hard ceiling; for money the enterprise may choose turnover **or** balance.
- Data from the last approved accounts; status changes only if a ceiling is crossed in
  **two consecutive** years (Annex Art. 4(2)).
- **Autonomous** (samostalno): holds < 25 % in others and nobody holds ≥ 25 % in it.
- **Partner** (partnersko): 25 %–50 % links. Add the partner's data proportionally to the
  share, upstream and downstream (one level each way).
- **Linked** (povezano): > 50 % of votes, right to appoint the majority of management,
  dominant influence by contract, or control by agreement. Add **100 %** of the linked
  enterprise's data. Natural persons acting jointly who own enterprises in the same or
  adjacent markets also create links.
- Public bodies holding ≥ 25 % normally make an enterprise **not** an SME (exceptions for
  universities, research centres, regional authorities under €10 m budget and < 5,000
  inhabitants, business angels, venture capital: Annex Art. 3(2)).
- Croatian calls use a "Izjava o veličini poduzetnika" (SME declaration) form, often with
  a table of linked and partner enterprises. Get the ownership chain right; it is the
  most common SME-status error.
- **Review**: the Commission has worked on updating SME rules and introducing "small
  mid-caps" (EIC already uses up to 499 employees for equity). VERIFY whether the
  Recommendation was changed before relying on the thresholds above.

## 2. De minimis aid (potpore male vrijednosti)

Source: Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/2831 (in force 1 Jan 2024, applies until
31 Dec 2030); summary by the Ministry of Finance (mfin.gov.hr).

- **Ceiling**: €300,000 per **single undertaking** (jedan poduzetnik) over **any rolling
  period of three years** (not calendar or fiscal years). Before 2024 the ceiling was
  €200,000; older texts still show it.
- At each new grant, add all de minimis granted in the three years before that date,
  regardless of grantor (state, county, city, HZZ, FZOEU, HAMAG-BICRO), purpose,
  instrument, and including EU-funded de minimis.
- **Moment of grant** = when the beneficiary acquires the legal right (decision/contract),
  not the payment date.
- **Single undertaking**: enterprises linked by majority votes, right to appoint/remove
  the majority of the board, dominant influence by contract or statute, or control of
  majority votes by agreement, also through other enterprises (Art. 2(2)). Enterprises
  linked only through the same public body are not one undertaking.
- **If a new grant would exceed the ceiling, it cannot be granted at all** (not even the
  part below the ceiling). An unlawful excess must be repaid with interest.
- Excluded sectors: primary fisheries and aquaculture; primary agricultural production;
  processing/marketing of agricultural products when aid is fixed by price/quantity or
  passed on to primary producers; export-related aid; aid conditional on domestic goods.
  Mixed businesses can receive aid if they separate activities or costs.
- Other de minimis regimes and ceilings: agriculture (Reg. 1408/2013 as amended by
  Reg. 2024/3118; €50,000 over 3 years, VERIFY), fisheries (Reg. 717/2014, as amended;
  VERIFY current ceiling), services of general economic interest (Reg. 2023/2832,
  €750,000). They can be cumulated up to the relevant ceilings.
- Loans and guarantees count by their **gross grant equivalent** (bruto novčana
  protuvrijednost). HBOR interest subsidies and HAMAG-BICRO guarantees are typically
  de minimis or GBER aid: ask the lender for the GGE figure.
- **Register**: Croatia keeps a register of state aid and de minimis aid (Registar
  državnih potpora i potpora male vrijednosti) at the Ministry of Finance since
  1 Jan 2017; grantors must enter each grant (Pravilnik NN 125/17). The register is not a
  self-service lookup for applicants (VERIFY), so the applicant keeps their own list and
  signs an **Izjava o korištenim potporama male vrijednosti** with each application.
- Grantors must keep de minimis records for 10 years.
- De minimis can be granted to undertakings in difficulty, except loans and guarantees
  under conditions (Reg. 2023/2831 Art. 4).

## 3. GBER and other state-aid bases

- Larger calls use the **General Block Exemption Regulation** (GBER, Reg. (EU) 651/2014,
  prolonged to **31 Dec 2026**; a new GBER is expected from 1 Jan 2027, VERIFY). Each
  activity is tied to a GBER article with its own intensity, e.g. Art. 14 regional
  investment aid (intensity by NUTS 2 region per the regional aid map), Art. 17 SME
  investment, Art. 18 SME consultancy, Art. 25 R&D, Art. 28 innovation aid for SMEs,
  Art. 29 process and organisational innovation, Art. 38+ energy and environment.
- **Incentive effect** (GBER Art. 6): work on the project must not start before the
  written application. "Start of works" means the first legally binding commitment to
  order equipment or start construction, whichever is first (buying land and preparatory
  studies are not start of works, GBER Art. 2(23)). Signing a binding order the day
  before submission can make the whole project ineligible.
- Cumulation: GBER aid and de minimis for the **same eligible costs** cannot exceed the
  highest GBER intensity (Reg. 2023/2831 Art. 5).
- State-aid records for GBER aid: 10 years (GBER Art. 12).
- Calls attach a **Program dodjele potpora** (aid scheme) naming the legal basis. Read it:
  it tells you whether the aid is de minimis or GBER, which changes the checks.

## 4. Undertaking in difficulty (poduzetnik u teškoćama)

GBER Art. 2(18), summarised:
- Limited company (d.o.o., d.d.), older than 3 years: more than half of subscribed share
  capital lost due to accumulated losses.
- Partnership with unlimited liability: more than half of capital in the accounts lost.
- Subject to collective insolvency proceedings, or meeting the criteria for them on
  creditors' request (in Croatia: stečaj, predstečajna nagodba).
- Received rescue aid and not repaid / restructuring aid and still under plan.
- Non-SMEs only: for the last two years, book debt-to-equity > 7.5 and EBITDA interest
  coverage < 1.0.
- SMEs younger than 3 years are not "in difficulty" under the capital-loss test.

Most GBER calls exclude undertakings in difficulty; HZZ excludes "poslodavci u
poteškoćama" as defined in its Pojmovnik. Many calls ask for a calculation from the GFI-POD
(annual financial statements). Check before applying: d.o.o. with negative equity after
years of losses are often excluded.

## 5. Tax and payment record

- **Potvrda Porezne uprave o stanju duga** (certificate of no outstanding tax and
  contribution debt): order via e-Porezna (ePorezna → zahtjev za potvrdu). Calls usually
  require it not older than 30 days at submission (VERIFY per call) and again before
  contract and payments. Some grantors obtain it themselves by data exchange (e.g. HZZ).
- Debts covered by an **upravni ugovor** (instalment agreement) with the Tax
  Administration are accepted by some calls if the instalments are being paid (HZZ 2026
  hiring subsidy explicitly accepts this).
- Blocked accounts: FINA "Potvrda o blokadi računa / Očevidnik redoslijeda osnova za
  plaćanje" (HZZ requires one not older than 7 days for self-employment).
- Other common exclusions: final convictions of the applicant or responsible persons
  (uvjerenje o nekažnjavanju), being on the "black list" of undeclared work (Zakon o
  suzbijanju neprijavljenog rada), unpaid salaries, previous unrecovered irregularities,
  recovery order outstanding after a Commission decision (Deggendorf principle).

## 6. No double financing (zabrana dvostrukog financiranja)

- The same expenditure item cannot be reimbursed twice from EU or other public sources
  (CPR 2021/1060 Art. 63(9)). Different costs of one project can be co-financed by
  different sources only if the call allows it and the total stays under the intensity.
- Calls require an **Izjava o nepostojanju dvostrukog financiranja** under material and
  criminal liability. FZOEU household calls also cap total public funding at < 100 % of
  eligible costs (EnU-6/26).
- Keep invoices marked or coded per project ("separate accounting code"); auditors cross
  check with other grantors.

## 7. NKD exclusions and sector rules

- Calls list eligible or excluded **NKD 2025** codes (Nacionalna klasifikacija
  djelatnosti 2025). Eligibility is tested on the **registered** activity and on the
  activity the **project** concerns, sometimes also on the main activity (by revenue).
- Typical exclusions in SME calls: agriculture/fisheries primary production (has its own
  funds), trade, real estate, financial services, gambling, tobacco, weapons, accommodation
  and food service (often), transport of goods by road (equipment), some consulting.
- HZZ self-employment 2026 excludes, among others, NKD areas A, B, G, H, I, L, M and
  specific groups (full list in `hzz-self-employment.md`).
- If the registered activity is missing, it can often be added before contracting, but
  some calls require it registered **at the application date**. Read the UzP.
