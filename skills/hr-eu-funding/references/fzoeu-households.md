# FZOEU calls for households (2026)

Sources: Fond za zaštitu okoliša i energetsku učinkovitost (FZOEU), `https://www.fzoeu.hr`
→ Nacionalni javni pozivi i natječaji; call text "Javni poziv za poticanje korištenja
obnovljivih izvora energije i sustava pohrane električne energije u obiteljskim kućama
EnU-6/26" (PDF on fzoeu.hr); MZOZT/MPGI press release 7 Apr 2026. Read 2026-10-25.
For the renovation and building side (energy certificates, legality of the house) see
`croatia:hr-real-estate`.

## 1. EnU-6/26: heat pumps, PV and batteries for family houses

**Status on 2026-10-25**: applications opened **2 Sep 2026 at 9:00** and the call was
**temporarily closed on 3 Sep 2026 at 12:00** (allocation reached in about one day). The
call formally runs until the end of the calendar year or until funds are used; check
fzoeu.hr for a reopening. Total allocation **€38,000,000** (planned split per press
release: €10 m heat pumps, €20 m PV, €8 m batteries).

### Who can apply
- **Only natural persons** who own or co-own a family house and have their **registered
  residence (prebivalište) at that address for at least 30 days** before applying
  (residence condition applies to the applicant, not to all co-owners).
- Family house: more than 50 % of gross floor area residential (see call definitions).
  Persons using 50 % or more of the floor area for business are not eligible.
- The house must be legal and meet the technical conditions (Prilog 1. Tehnički uvjeti).
  For heat pumps the press release states the house needs at least energy class **C**
  (continental) or **B** (coastal) (VERIFY in Tehnički uvjeti).
- Higher rate for households **at risk of energy poverty**: average monthly equivalised
  income per household member in 2025 **up to €1,341.42** (DZS 2025 average equivalised
  disposable income €16,097 / 12, OECD equivalence scale), and the measure must be on the
  household's only habitable property. Proven with Tax Administration certificates on
  2025 income for every household member and property certificates.

### Measures and amounts (2026)

| Measure | Max eligible cost | Standard (50 %) | Energy-poor (70 %) |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 Heat pump for domestic hot water and space heating (or heating + cooling) | €12,500 | up to **€6,250** | up to **€8,750** |
| M2 PV plant for self-consumption, grid-connected | €12,000 | up to **€6,000** or **€600/kW** | up to **€8,400** or **€840/kW** |
| M3 Battery storage (**only together with M2**) | €11,200 | up to **€5,600** or **€350/kWh** | up to **€7,840** or **€490/kWh** |

The lower of the percentage and the per-kW/kWh cap applies (read the call's section V).
An energy-poor application that fails the 70 % conditions can still be funded at 50 %.

### Eligible costs and timing
- Installation costs incurred **from the call's publication date**; advance payments
  incurred and paid from **1 Jan 2026**; HEP ODS metering point equipment paid from
  **1 Jan 2026**.
- Systems **fully paid before the call was published are not eligible**.
- Eligibility period of costs: **1 Jan 2026 to 30 Jun 2028**; payment request with full
  documentation by **30 Jun 2028**.
- Payment proof must be a bank document; a contractor's confirmation of payment or
  customer card is **not** accepted.
- No double financing: total public funding must stay below 100 % of eligible costs.

### How to apply
- Online only via **eFZOEU**, accessed through `https://fondovi.gov.hr`, with **NIAS
  credentials of significant assurance level** (e.g. e-Građani with a suitable
  credential). A representative needs a notarised special power of attorney (template in
  the call's annexes).
- Annexes include: Tehnički uvjeti; income calculation example; application form;
  designer's statement (Izjava projektanta); applicant's statement; household members
  statement; property statement.
- Typical documents: land registry extract proving ownership (posjedovni list only in
  limited cases), proof of legality of the house, offers/cost estimates per measure,
  designer's documentation for PV, income and property certificates for the 70 % rate.
- Warranty statement from the contractor (at least 2 years on works); final supervising
  engineer's report for systems ≥ 30 kW.
- Objections to FZOEU decisions within 30 days.

### Practical advice
- These calls close within hours or a day: prepare all documents, NIAS access and
  contractor offers **before** the opening date; submit at opening time.
- Do not pay the full price before publication of the call; an advance from 1 Jan of the
  call year is allowed under EnU-6/26.
- PV for self-consumption also involves HEP ODS connection procedures (prosumer status,
  "kupac s vlastitom proizvodnjom"); check with the installer.

## 2. Previous PV calls (for context)

- **EnU-1/25** and **EnU-6/25**: retroactive PV support at up to 50 % of eligible costs,
  **€600 per kW** (max eligible cost per the call), for plants built in 2025 and in 2024
  respectively; both closed.
- Do not assume a retroactive call will repeat; EnU-6/26 is not retroactive beyond
  1 Jan 2026 advances.

## 3. Energy renovation of family houses (facade, roof, windows)

- The broad "energetska obnova obiteljskih kuća" programme for all citizens (measures
  A1 integral renovation, A2 envelope, A3 heating, A4 PV; 60 % / 80 % for
  earthquake-damaged houses in 2024) has **not** been repeated in 2026 as of the snapshot
  (VERIFY on fzoeu.hr).
- In 2025 FZOEU ran an **expression of interest** for vulnerable households at risk of
  energy poverty; a financing call for these households was in the 2026 indicative plan
  (VERIFY status). Earlier energy-poverty schemes required the household to be a recipient
  of the guaranteed minimum benefit (zajamčena minimalna naknada) (VERIFY for the new call).
- Commercial sites forecast a general renovation call in 2027: unconfirmed.

## 4. Electric vehicles

- **EnU-4/26** (opened 18 Feb 2026, closed): only **crafts (obrtnici) and legal persons**,
  not citizens and not associations; main activity in taxi/ride-hailing, road freight or
  car leasing excluded. Categories L1–L7 electric, M1–M3 and N1–N3 electric or hydrogen.
  Funded under NPOO C1.4.R5-I3 (REPowerEU) and de minimis. Total €3.2 m. Secondary sources
  report up to 40 % of net cost and up to €90,000 per application, vehicle to be kept
  3 years (VERIFY in the call PDF).
- Applicants funded under EnU-4/25 from 3 Oct 2025 were not eligible for EnU-4/26.
- No 2026 EV subsidy for private citizens was found on fzoeu.hr as of 2026-10-25 (VERIFY).
- Modernisation Fund call for taxi, delivery and car-sharing EVs (open since
  15 Jan 2026) is for companies and crafts.

## 5. Where to look next year

- MZOZT + FZOEU **indicative annual plan** of calls (2026 plan published 29 Jan 2026,
  €1.58 bn total, of which FZOEU's own calls €98.5 m): mzozt.gov.hr and fzoeu.hr.
- FZOEU call page and Q&A; phone and e-mail contacts are in each call.
- Cities and counties sometimes co-finance PV, heat pumps or energy certificates on top of
  FZOEU: check local calls and whether combining is allowed (double financing rule).
