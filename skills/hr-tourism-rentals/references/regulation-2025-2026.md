# Regulatory changes 2025–2027

Status: researched 2026-10-25. This area is moving. The new Hospitality Act was in its second
reading in the Croatian Parliament when this was written. Always check narodne-novine.nn.hr and
mint.gov.hr for the adopted text.

## 1. ZUD amendments NN 152/2024 (in force 25 Dec 2024; key articles from 1 Jan 2025)

Source: Zakon o izmjenama i dopunama Zakona o ugostiteljskoj djelatnosti, NN 152/2024.

| Change | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Co-owner consent** for short-term rental becomes a condition for the rješenje | ZUD čl. 24(1) (business premises) and čl. 34(2) point 4 (domaćinstvo), via NN 152/24 čl. 2 and 6 | New rješenja for units in multi-apartment and mixed buildings need the prior written consent of co-owners under the building-management act (below) |
| **Transitional period for existing renters** | NN 152/24 čl. 18 | Holders of rješenja in multi-apartment or mixed buildings have **5 years** to obtain consent. Without it, the right lapses and the rješenje is revoked ex officio |
| **Local capacity decisions** | new ZUD čl. 21a and 35a | Rješenja must follow the city or municipality council's decision on the number, type, category and capacity of facilities. Requests are handled in order of receipt |
| **"Domaćin" category** | new ZUD čl. 30a | A renter resident in the same county (or on Pag), not in a multi-apartment building. May serve breakfast. Existing renters who meet the criteria are domaćini automatically (čl. 19(2)) |
| **Temporary rješenja** abolished for the future; existing ones valid **until 31 Dec 2026** | NN 152/24 čl. 3, 17 | See the 2026 proposal below for an extension |
| Signage shows type, category and special standard; without category, type only | ZUD čl. 32(1)(1) | |
| **Fines in euro** | ZUD čl. 46–48 | €660–3,980; €330–2,650; €330–1,320 |

**Zoning**: NN 152/24 gives councils powers over **capacity, number, type and category** in the
destination (čl. 21a/35a). It does not create residential vs tourism zones as such. Spatial plans
(prostorni planovi) may separately limit tourist use. Check the local plan and any council decision.

## 2. Co-owner consent: Zakon o upravljanju i održavanju zgrada (ZUOZ, NN 152/2024)

- **Short-term rental** = renting a flat or part of a flat for temporary accommodation for **less than
  30 days** (ZUOZ čl. 4(1) point 5).
- **Consent** (čl. 34(1)): prior written consent of a **two-thirds majority of co-owners, by
  ownership share**, **and** of **all co-owners whose units adjoin** the flat (shared walls, floor or
  ceiling).
- Units already used for short-term rental: their co-owners are presumed to agree (čl. 34(2)).
- Consent is given for **at least 5 years** (čl. 34(4)). It can be revoked early by a **simple majority**
  if the activity breaches house rules or co-owners' rights **3 or more times within 2 years** (čl. 34(5)).
- **Existing renters** must collect the consents **within 5 years** of the act's entry into force
  (čl. 63(2)), i.e. by the end of 2029 (commentators cite 31 Dec 2029 / 1 Jan 2030). **VERIFY** the
  exact end date with the office issuing rješenja.
- The Ministry of Physical Planning (MPGI) published a recommended consent template ("Odluka o
  suglasnosti … kratkoročni najam") on mpgi.gov.hr.
- Houses (not multi-apartment buildings) are not affected.

## 3. The new Zakon o ugostiteljskoj djelatnosti (2026–2027)

Timeline:
- Spring 2026: draft presented and public consultation.
- **26 June 2026**: the Government sends the Prijedlog zakona (P.Z.E. br. 315) to Parliament. The
  Tourism Committee supports it at first reading on 30 June 2026.
- **21 Oct 2026**: the Government submits the **Final Proposal** (Konačni prijedlog) for second
  reading. It is on the Parliament agenda from 22 Oct 2026 (Tragento, 23 Oct 2026). **Not yet adopted
  at the time of research.**

Main content (Vlada press release, Sabor committee report, Tragento summary of the Final Proposal):
1. **Single registration system** for renters and a **mandatory registration number** for rooms,
   apartments, studio apartments and holiday houses **advertised on online platforms**. Hotels and
   camps are excluded. The owner, not the agency, obtains the number. It is **free, digital, and
   issued on request by the Ministry of Tourism and Sport**.
   - **Start date**: the Ministry said "early 2027", to be confirmed after the act is adopted (mint.gov.hr,
     22 May 2026). The Final Proposal sets **1 May 2027** for the registration-number and data-exchange
     provisions. **VERIFY** in the adopted act.
   - **Until then there is no obligation** to obtain or display a registration number (mint.gov.hr,
     22 May 2026).
2. **Platform duties and fines**: platforms that do not deliver data or do not check hosts face fines
   of **€13,000–130,000** or **1–6% of revenue** (Vlada, 2026).
3. **Supervision** extended beyond the State Inspectorate to the **Customs Administration, communal
   wardens** and nature wardens.
4. **No new rješenja for apartments and rooms in residential premises (stambena namjena) for legal
   persons and obrtnici**. The aim is to protect housing. Domaćinstvo is regulated "more precisely".
   **VERIFY** the final wording and whether it affects domaćinstvo.
5. **Mandatory re-categorisation**: renters (domaćinstvo) **every 10 years** after alignment with a new
   rulebook; rooms and apartments in "other accommodation" every 10 years; hostels and similar every
   5 years; hotels and camps every 5 years (previously 4).
6. **All administrative procedures electronic through eTurizam**.
7. **Temporary rješenja** expiring 31 Dec 2026 extended to **31 Dec 2030** (Final Proposal).
8. Clarification on hosting non-family members in holiday flats and houses (vikendaši); a ban on
   serving energy drinks to under-18s.

**Earlier draft vs final**: some spring 2026 coverage mentioned a registration number "from
20 May 2026". The Ministry called this misinformation (mint.gov.hr, 22 May 2026). Do not repeat it.

## 4. Regulation (EU) 2024/1028 (short-term rental data)

Regulation (EU) 2024/1028 of 11 April 2024 on data collection and sharing relating to short-term
accommodation rental services, amending Regulation (EU) 2018/1724. **Applies from 20 May 2026.**

Key mechanics (EUR-Lex text and summary):
- **Registration procedures are optional for member states**. Where a member state requires
  registration, it must be online, simple, and give a **unique registration number** per unit.
- Hosts subject to registration must **display the number** on every listing. Platforms must provide
  the field, display it, and make **random checks**. They must remove listings flagged by the
  competent authority as lacking a valid number.
- Platforms transmit **activity data** (number of nights rented and guests per unit, URL, address,
  registration number) **monthly** to a national **single digital entry point** (micro and small
  platforms: quarterly). Authorities use it for statistics and policy, with limits on use.
- Croatian implementation: through the new Hospitality Act (registration system, Ministry as the
  issuing authority, eTurizam, data exchange with platforms) from **2027** (see §3).
- Practical consequence for hosts: a listing without a valid Croatian registration number becomes
  removable once Croatian registration is in force.

## 5. Tax changes that interact (see `taxes-vat-platforms.md`)

- 1 Jan 2025: flat-rate tax per bed by **tourism development index** (ranges €20–300). The VAT
  threshold rises to **€60,000**. **Property tax** replaces the holiday-house tax.
- 1 Jan 2026: **Fiskalizacija 2.0** (eRačun for VAT payers; B2C fiscalization extended to bank
  transfers). Non-VAT issuers from 1 Jan 2027.
- 13 Oct 2026: Airbnb's host-only fee for EEA hosts (larger reverse-charge VAT base).
- Proposed for 2027 (Sept 2026): higher minimum flat-rate tax in index categories I and II.
- 1 Jul 2030: ViDA deemed-supplier rule for platforms (Directive (EU) 2025/516). **VERIFY**.

## 6. Local restrictions

- City or municipality decisions under ZUD čl. 35a (capacity caps, moratoria on new rješenja in
  certain settlements). Ask the issuing office and the local TZ, and search the city's official
  gazette (službeni glasnik).
- Spatial plans can restrict tourist use in residential zones. Check with the city planning department.
- Building rules: the house rules of the building (kućni red zgrade) and the ZUOZ consent regime.
