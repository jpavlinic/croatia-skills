# Registration and categorisation

Status: researched 2026-10-25. Main acts:
- Zakon o ugostiteljskoj djelatnosti (ZUD; NN 85/15, 121/16, 99/18, 25/19, 98/19, 32/20, 42/20, 126/21, 152/24)
- Pravilnik o razvrstavanju i kategorizaciji objekata u kojima se pružaju ugostiteljske usluge u
  domaćinstvu (NN 9/16, 54/16, 61/16, 69/17, 120/19)

A new ZUD was in Parliament in October 2026 (see `regulation-2025-2026.md`). Check whether it has
been adopted before relying on the article numbers below.

## 1. Which form fits

| Form | Who | Capacity / scope | Tax regime |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Domaćinstvo** (obiteljski smještaj, private accommodation in a household) | A physical person (iznajmljivač) who is a Croatian, EEA or Swiss national (ZUD čl. 30(1)–(2)) and **owns** the property. A spouse, partner or close relative may own it if they consent in writing (čl. 34(3)) | Rooms, apartments, studio apartments, holiday houses: **max 10 rooms or 20 beds**, extra beds not counted. Camp / kamp odmorište in domaćinstvo: **max 10 units or 30 guests** at once, children under 12 not counted. Robinson-style: same 10/30. Breakfast for own guests only (ZUD čl. 30(3)–(4)) | Flat-rate tax per bed (paušal) if ≤ 20 beds and not a VAT payer; otherwise income from self-employment |
| **Domaćin** (category added by NN 152/24, ZUD čl. 30a) | A renter whose registered residence is in the same county (or on the island of Pag) as the property. **Not** for flats in multi-apartment or mixed buildings | Same as domaćinstvo | Same. Domaćin status matters for local capacity rules and the property-tax exemption of the domaćin's own home (ZLP čl. 27(1)(9)) |
| **OPG / seljačko domaćinstvo** (family farm, "agroturizam") | A farm in the Upisnik poljoprivrednika (ZUD čl. 39(1)) | Rooms/apartments/rural houses: **max 10 rooms or 20 guests**. Camp or robinson: max 20 units / 60 guests. Total accommodation max 80 guests. Guests must get meals mostly from own production (čl. 39(2)) | OPG rules. Ask an accountant **VERIFY** |
| **Obrt** (sole trader) | A physical person with a registered trade | No domaćinstvo limits. Ugostiteljski objekt, categorised under the "ostali ugostiteljski objekti za smještaj" or "hoteli" rulebooks | Self-employment income (real or paušalni obrt), VAT rules, fiscalization → `croatia:hr-business-admin` |
| **d.o.o. / j.d.o.o.** | A company | As above | Profit tax, VAT, fiscalization |

**When must you move to an obrt or company?**
- More than 10 rooms or 20 beds (or 10 camp units / 30 guests).
- Property you do not own (subletting or managing other people's flats is not domaćinstvo; an
  agency or company is needed).
- Meals beyond breakfast. The exception is the "15 km rule": lunch and dinner are allowed in
  domaćinstvo only if no registered food business is within 15 km by road (Pravilnik čl. 9; ZUD).
  **VERIFY** the current wording.
- Non-EEA/Swiss nationals cannot register domaćinstvo (ZUD čl. 30(2)).

**Planned change (Final Proposal, Oct 2026):** obrti and companies could no longer get **new**
rješenja for apartments and rooms in residential premises (stambena namjena). See
`regulation-2025-2026.md`.

## 2. Getting the rješenje (approval + category)

Authority: the county administrative department for the economy (upravni odjel županije nadležan
za gospodarstvo), or the City of Zagreb. Many offices still use the older name "Ured državne
uprave". **From the new ZUD, procedures are to run electronically through the eTurizam system.**

**Typical documents** (a county office checklist, 2026; check your own office):
1. A request form ("Zahtjev za pružanje ugostiteljskih usluga u domaćinstvu").
2. A copy of the ID card or passport.
3. A land-registry extract (izvadak iz zemljišne knjige) proving ownership, usually not older than
   6 months. For co-ownership: the co-owners' consent. In multi-apartment buildings: the written
   consent for short-term rental under ZUOZ čl. 34 (two-thirds by share plus all adjoining
   co-owners), see `regulation-2025-2026.md`.
4. **Proof of usability** of the building (dokaz o uporabljivosti, uporabna dozvola or equivalent
   under the Zakon o gradnji). Illegal or unlegalised buildings cannot be categorised.
5. The self-assessment form of category conditions (Prilog I for rooms and studio apartments,
   Prilog II for apartments and holiday houses) from the Pravilnik (mint.gov.hr).
6. An administrative fee (upravna pristojba). One county office listed **€9.29** in 2026. **VERIFY**
   locally.

**Procedure** (Pravilnik čl. 27–29): document review plus an on-site inspection by a commission of at
least two members, or a shortened procedure without inspection. The rješenje states the owner,
OIB, address, type, capacity (beds, extra beds) and category (ZUD čl. 37). It is entered in the
**Središnji registar** (central register) (ZUD čl. 34(4)).

**Local capacity decisions** (ZUD čl. 35a, from 1 Jan 2025): if the city or municipality council has
adopted a decision on the number, type, category or capacity of accommodation in the destination,
new rješenja must comply. Requests are processed in the order received. Ask the local office or
tourist board whether such a decision exists.

**Changes, inheritance, closure:**
- Changing beds or units, or deregistering, needs a request to the same office (ZUD čl. 38).
- Heirs or donees may continue under the previous rješenje if they file a statement that nothing has
  materially changed (ZUD čl. 35).
- Notify the tax office within **8 days** of any change (Porezna uprava).
- Spouses or family members in one household may each hold a rješenje, but the combined capacity is
  capped at the čl. 30(3) limits (ZUD čl. 36).

**Temporary rješenja** (privremena rješenja, issued without full building documentation): valid
until 31 Dec 2026 under NN 152/24 čl. 17. The new ZUD Final Proposal would extend them to
**31 Dec 2030** (Tragento, 23 Oct 2026). About 15,000 renters held one at the end of 2024.
**VERIFY** the adopted text.

## 3. Types and stars

Pravilnik čl. 16–25:

| Type (vrsta) | Categories |
|---|---|
| Soba (room) | 2, 3, 4 stars |
| Studio apartman | 2, 3, 4 stars |
| Apartman | 2, 3, 4, 5 stars |
| Kuća za odmor (holiday house) | 2, 3, 4, 5 stars |
| Kamp (camp in domaćinstvo) | 2, 3, 4, 5 stars (Pravilnik čl. 24–25; **VERIFY** for kamp odmorište) |
| Kamp odmorište, robinzonski smještaj | type only / own conditions (Pravilnik čl. 23a, Prilog IV) |

Each unit has one category (čl. 24(3)). **Special standards** (posebni standard, e.g. themed labels)
are granted on request under ZUD čl. 34a.

**Rekategorizacija (re-categorisation)**: under the current rules there is no fixed expiry date.
The new ZUD Final Proposal introduces mandatory re-categorisation **every 10 years** for renters,
after alignment with a new rulebook. **VERIFY** when adopted.

## 4. Minimum conditions

From the Pravilnik:
- **Safety and order** (čl. 5): a tidy site and exterior, working equipment, a **first-aid kit**,
  and regular waste removal.
- **Utilities** (čl. 8): hot and cold running water, electricity and lighting, **heating to at least
  20 °C** in guest areas (not required for summer-only objects open 1 May–31 Oct, čl. 3(4)),
  ventilation, and a phone connection.
- **Layout and modules** (čl. 6–7, 10–11): minimum floor areas per room type and bed count. The
  numbers are in the Prilozi. Open the Pravilnik for the exact m² per category.
- Summer-only objects may change linen and towels on the guest's request (čl. 3(4)).
- Robinson-style: the fire inspector may require extra fire measures (čl. 9a).

**Practical checklist** that tourist boards publish (2026). Several items are local or good
practice, not Pravilnik items:
- the price list posted in each unit (ZUD čl. 32(1)(2)), including the note that the tourist tax is included;
- the plaque at the entrance (ZUD čl. 32(1)(1));
- a complaint form available, with a written reply within 15 days; keep complaints 1 year (ZUD čl. 32(1)(6));
- an evacuation plan inside the entrance door of each unit;
- a first-aid kit in each unit;
- house rules (kućni red) displayed. ZUD čl. 32 does not list them for domaćinstvo, but tourist
  boards recommend them. Templates are in `guest-communication-templates.md`;
- a fire extinguisher, smoke detector and CO detector (for gas or solid-fuel appliances): fire rules
  for domaćinstvo are not spelled out in the Pravilnik. **VERIFY** with the local fire inspector
  (Pravilnik o zaštiti od požara u ugostiteljskim objektima) and the tourist board;
- a notice that alcohol is not served to under-18s, if alcohol is served (ZUD čl. 32(3)).

## 5. Plaque (ploča)

Pravilnik čl. 14–15, Prilog V; ZUD čl. 32(1)(1):
- At or next to the main entrance. It shows the type, the category (stars) and any special standard.
  One plaque may cover several units of the same kind in one building (čl. 14(5)).
- Spec: 25 × 25 cm, 1 mm stainless steel, two-colour UV-resistant print. It must come from a maker
  approved by the Ministry (list on mint.gov.hr). The owner pays.
- An owner who has lost the right to a category shows the type only, without stars (NN 152/24).
- Fines for missing or wrong signage: ZUD čl. 48 (euro amounts since NN 152/24).

## 6. After the rješenje: the order of steps

1. **Tax office** (Porezna uprava, ispostava where the property is): within **8 days**, bring a copy
   of the rješenje and file the **RPO** form. Porezna issues the flat-rate tax decision.
2. **eVisitor account**: from the local tourist board (turistička zajednica, TZ). Enter units and
   beds and get credentials. Guests can only be checked in once the facility is in eVisitor.
3. **Tourist board**: the flat-rate tourist tax and the membership fee are charged in eVisitor and via TZ-2.
4. **PDV-ID**, if you will list on Booking.com, Airbnb or any EU platform: P-PDV form at least 15
   days before the first commission (`taxes-vat-platforms.md`).
5. **Plaque, price list, receipt book, EP book**.
6. **List on platforms**: show the category exactly as in the rješenje (ZUD čl. 32(2)). When
   advertising to EU businesses (the platforms), show your OIB or PDV-ID. From 2027, the
   **registration number** too (`regulation-2025-2026.md`).

## 7. Insurance

No compulsory liability insurance for domaćinstvo was found in ZUD or the Pravilnik. **VERIFY.**
Property and third-party liability insurance (osiguranje od odgovornosti prema gostima) is
strongly advisable. Check that a home policy covers commercial short-term letting, because many
exclude it. Booking.com and Airbnb host protection programmes are not a substitute; read their terms.

## 8. Fines (ZUD, euro amounts from NN 152/24)

- The most serious offences (e.g. operating without a rješenje): **€660–3,980** (čl. 46).
- Main breaches: **€330–2,650** (čl. 47).
- Domaćinstvo-specific (services contrary to the rješenje, etc.): **€330–1,320** (čl. 48).
- The new ZUD Final Proposal adds supervision by the Customs Administration and communal wardens,
  and platform fines of **€13,000–130,000** or 1–6% of revenue (Vlada, 2026).
