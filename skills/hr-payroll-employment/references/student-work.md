# Student work 2026 (studentski ugovor) – Croatia, researched 2026-10-25

Legal basis: Zakon o obavljanju studentskih poslova (NN 96/18, 16/20 – **VERIFY** later
amendments); ZPD čl. 9 st. 1 t. 6 and Pravilnik o porezu na dohodak čl. 6 (tax-free amount);
Zakon o doprinosima (contributions on student pay).

## 1. Who can work on a student contract

- **Full-time (redoviti) students** enrolled at a Croatian higher-education institution (and
  certain foreign students as provided by the Act), with student status, through an
  **authorised intermediary (posrednik)** – a student centre/student service (e.g. Studentski
  centar u Zagrebu) holding the ministry's authorisation.
- Part-time students (izvanredni studenti) may do student jobs only if they are not employed,
  self-employed or registered as insured on another basis (rules clarified in 2020) – **VERIFY**
  in the Act before advising.
- Pupils (učenici) aged 15+ work via pupil contracts under the Zakon o tržištu rada / similar
  rules; the 2026 minimum hourly rate is also EUR 6.56 (as reported by student services).
- Student work must not replace an employment relationship for a regular, permanent job; the
  contract is tripartite (student – intermediary – client/naručitelj) and signed **before** work
  starts.

## 2. 2026 figures

| Item | 2026 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum hourly pay (studentska satnica)** | **EUR 6.56 net** | Odluka of the Ministry of Science, Education and Youth; SC Zagreb "Studentska satnica za 2026." |
| Increase for night, Sunday, holiday and overtime hours | at least **+50 %** (EUR 9.84 at the minimum) | Zakon o obavljanju studentskih poslova; student services |
| Break | at least 30 min for 6+ hours a day | the Act |
| **Tax-free earnings via intermediaries** | **EUR 12,000/year** (was EUR 10,800 in 2025): special amount 8.0 × 600 = EUR 4,800 + annual basic deduction 12 × 600 = EUR 7,200 | SC Zagreb "Studentske zarade – limiti"; Pravilnik o porezu na dohodak čl. 6 (coefficient raised to 8.0 from 1 Jan 2026) |
| Above EUR 12,000 | tax withheld by the intermediary on the excess (often quoted as 20 %; depends on local rates and the annual assessment) | **VERIFY** with the intermediary/Porezna uprava |
| **Dependant threshold** (student stays on parent's tax card) | annual receipts up to **EUR 3,600** (unchanged in 2026) | ZPD čl. 17 st. 1; SC Zagreb, SC Karlovac |
| Payment by client | to the intermediary within **15 days** after the job ends; then the intermediary pays the student | the Act |

The two thresholds are different: earning between EUR 3,600 and 12,000 costs the parent the
dependant deduction but the student still pays no tax.

## 3. Client's cost on top of the student's net pay (2026)

Per SC Zagreb ("Zarada studenta – neto iznos", "Student servis"):

| Item | Rate on the student's pay |
|---|---|
| Pension contribution (MIO) | **5 %** |
| Health insurance for injury at work and occupational disease | **0.5 %** |
| Intermediary fee (incl. student-council share) | **12 %** of the net amount |

Roughly **17.5 %** on top of the net pay (before any tax on earnings above EUR 12,000).
Exact bases and rounding differ by intermediary – **VERIFY** on its price list.

## 4. Employer checklist

- Use only authorised intermediaries; get the contract (ugovor/uputnica) issued before work.
- Keep records of hours, especially night/Sunday/holiday hours (+50 %).
- Provide safe working conditions and protect dignity like for employees; minors have extra
  protections (no night work, limited hours) – see `working-time-leave.md`.
- Don't use student contracts for the same person in a permanent full-time job pattern – risk of
  reclassification as employment (ZOR čl. 10 st. 2).
- Students' personal data: GDPR rules → `croatia:hr-legal-compliance`.
