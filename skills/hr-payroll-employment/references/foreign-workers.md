# Foreign workers – Croatia, researched 2026-10-25

Legal basis: Zakon o strancima (ZoS), NN 133/20 as amended incl. **NN 40/2025** (in force
15 March 2025) and **NN 55/2026** (published 27 May 2026, in force **4 June 2026**); Pravilnik o
boravku državljana trećih zemalja (NN 43/2026); HZZ and MUP guidance (hzz.hr, mup.gov.hr,
gov.hr "Rad stranaca").

Secondary summaries used for the 2026 amendment details: humble-hunters.com "Zakon o strancima
2026.: što se mijenja" (article-by-article), Poslovni.hr/Hina (23 Apr 2026, final bill). Where
they differ, the item is marked **VERIFY** – check the NN 55/2026 text on narodne-novine.nn.hr.

## 1. EU/EEA/Swiss citizens

Free access to the labour market: no work permit, same employment rules as Croatians. They
register residence with the police if staying over 3 months (Zakon o strancima part on EEA
nationals – **VERIFY** article). Employer simply registers them with HZMO/HZZO before work
starts like any worker; they need an OIB.

## 2. Third-country nationals – single permit (dozvola za boravak i rad)

Typical route (ZoS čl. 97–99 as amended):

1. **Labour market test (test tržišta rada)** at HZZ, unless an exemption applies (e.g.
   occupations on the shortage list, extension for the same employer/occupation/worker, other
   exemptions in the Act). HZZ reports the result within 15 days; the permit application must
   follow within 90 days (ZoS čl. 98 st. 5–6).
2. **Positive HZZ opinion (mišljenje)** – from 4 June 2026 the employer must, among others
   (ZoS čl. 99 as replaced by NN 55/2026):
   - actively carry on a registered activity linked to the occupation;
   - have no tax/contribution debts and have filed all tax reports;
   - have employed continuously for the last year at least one Croatian/EEA/Swiss worker full
     time on an indefinite contract;
   - have inflows to its account of at least **EUR 100,000** (legal person) or **EUR 40,000**
     (natural person) in the last **12 months**;
   - not have had its account blocked for more than 30 consecutive days in the last 6 months;
   - not be on the list of employers with undeclared work, nor convicted of labour offences;
   - employ domestic (HR/EEA/CH) full-time workers equal to at least **20 %** of the number of
     third-country workers (**10 %** for shortage occupations) – Poslovni.hr reported 10 %;
     **VERIFY** in ZoS čl. 99 st. 11–12;
   - offer pay not lower than that of Croatian workers in the same or similar occupation and a
     non-discriminatory contract.
3. **MUP decision** within at most **90 days** of a complete application, including the labour
   market test (extension by 30 days possible, not for seasonal work) (ZoS čl. 91 st. 8–9). Until
   3 June 2026 the deadline was 15 days.
4. The worker may start work on the confirmation delivered to the employer, with HZMO/HZZO
   registration (ZoS čl. 91 st. 6). Visa, if needed, is applied for at a consulate.

Key worker rights and limits after NN 55/2026 (**VERIFY** details):
- **Change of employer** within the permit: after **6 months** with the first employer (was
  1 year), via notification: new employer notifies the police within 3 days, HZZ opinion in
  5 days, MUP decides within 45 days (ZoS čl. 103.d).
- **Allowed unemployment** during the permit: up to 3 months (permit < 2 years) or 6 months
  (> 2 years) (ZoS čl. 103.a); the worker notifies HZZ within 5 days of the job ending.
- Territorial limit: a shortage-occupation permit is valid only in police administrations where
  the occupation is in shortage; a labour-market-test permit only where the test was done
  (ZoS čl. 88).
- The **employer** (not the worker) notifies the police electronically of the end of the
  employment contract (within 5 working days per the summary; ZoS čl. 95).
- Seasonal permits: up to 90 days or 9 months a year, and a multi-year seasonal permit of up to
  3 years for the same worker and employer (ZoS čl. 104).
- **Croatian language**: proof of an A1.1 exam for renewal/new čl. 97 permits applies from
  **4 June 2027**; the employer pays for the exam (ZoS čl. 92.a).
- **Accommodation**: the employer must ensure adequate accommodation where it provides it and
  report changes in adequacy within 15 days (ZoS čl. 103.f; standards in Pravilnik NN 43/2026) –
  **VERIFY** obligations for your case.
- Residence registration within 15 days (ZoS čl. 178).
- Fines for employers for missed notifications range e.g. EUR 500–1,500 and EUR 1,500–3,000
  (ZoS čl. 251) – **VERIFY**.

Employment law, pay and tax apply exactly as for Croatian workers (same ZOR, minimum wage,
payroll). Tax residence: a foreign worker becomes a Croatian tax resident if they have residence
or habitual abode in Croatia (ZPD) – non-residents' payroll differs (personal deduction only for
months with Croatian income, ZPD article after čl. 14 – **VERIFY** number) – the calculator assumes residents.

## 3. Posted workers

Workers posted to Croatia by foreign employers: Zakon o upućivanju radnika u Republiku Hrvatsku
(posting notification, Croatian minimum conditions) – **VERIFY** before advising. Croatian
employers posting abroad: ZOR čl. 18.

## 4. Digital nomads

- Temporary stay for digital nomads (privremeni boravak u svrhu boravka digitalnih nomada): up
  to **18 months**; a new application only after 6 months from expiry (mup.gov.hr).
- The nomad works for a foreign employer or own foreign company **and may not work for Croatian
  employers** or provide services to Croatian clients under this status.
- Means of subsistence 2026: about **EUR 3,622.50 per month** (EUR 65,205 for 18 months), per
  the MUP method of calculation as reported in 2026 – **VERIFY** the current amount on
  mup.gov.hr.
- Income from that foreign work is exempt from Croatian income tax under the ZPD exemption for
  digital nomads (ZPD čl. 9 – **VERIFY**), but staying over 183 days may create tax residence
  questions – advise a tax adviser.
- A digital nomad is not "employed in Croatia": no Croatian payroll, no HZMO registration.

## 5. Practical checklist for hiring a third-country national

1. Check the shortage list / run the HZZ labour market test.
2. Check the employer conditions (inflows, domestic worker ratio, no blocked account, no debts).
3. Prepare the employment contract (Croatian, non-discriminatory, pay at least comparable) and
   proof of accommodation if provided.
4. Submit the permit application (electronically / via the police administration); track the
   e-Građani inbox for requests.
5. Register with HZMO before day one; payroll as for any worker.
6. Diarise: change-of-employer notifications, end-of-contract notification, permit expiry,
   language exam from June 2027.
