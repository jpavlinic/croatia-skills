# Employment contracts (ugovor o radu) – Croatia, researched 2026-10-25

Primary source: Zakon o radu (ZOR), NN 93/14, 127/17, 98/19, 151/22, 46/23, 64/23
(consolidated text on zakon.hr, in force from 14 June 2023). An amendment transposing the
Pay Transparency Directive (EU) 2023/970 and the Platform Work Directive (EU) 2024/2831 was
in preparation in autumn 2026 (expected "by the end of 2026") – **VERIFY** whether it has been
published in Narodne novine before relying on the article numbers or rules below.

## 1. Form and timing

- Written form (ZOR čl. 14 st. 1). A contract not in writing is still valid (st. 2), but the
  employer must give the worker a written confirmation (potvrda o sklopljenom ugovoru) **before
  work starts** (st. 3). If neither exists before work starts, the contract is deemed to be
  **for an indefinite period** (st. 4).
- Before work starts the employer gives the worker a copy of the written contract; a copy of the
  registration for mandatory pension and health insurance follows within 8 days after the
  registration deadline (ZOR čl. 14 st. 5).
- Registration with HZMO (prijava na obvezno mirovinsko osiguranje, which also covers HZZO
  health insurance): **at the earliest 8 days before and at the latest before the worker starts**
  (Zakon o mirovinskom osiguranju čl. 109; mirovinsko.hr "Prijave i odjave na osiguranje").
  De-registration (odjava): within 24 hours of the end of employment per HZMO "Rokovi za
  podnošenje prijava" – **VERIFY** the exact odjava rule for your case on mirovinsko.hr.
- Employers keep a register of workers (evidencija o radnicima, ZOR čl. 5) under the
  Pravilnik o sadržaju i načinu vođenja evidencije o radnicima (NN 73/17 and amendments –
  **VERIFY** current version), including daily working-time records.

## 2. Mandatory content (ZOR čl. 15 st. 1)

The written contract or confirmation must contain:

1. the parties, their OIB and residence/seat;
2. place of work (or the different places, if no fixed place); the parties may agree that the
   worker freely chooses the place of work (st. 2);
3. job title or nature/type of work, or a short list/description of duties;
4. date of conclusion and date work starts;
5. indefinite or fixed term, and the end date or expected duration if fixed-term;
6. paid annual leave entitlement (or how it is determined);
7. termination procedure and notice periods (or how they are determined);
8. **gross pay**: gross basic/agreed pay, allowances (dodaci) and other receipts for work, and
   payment periods;
9. length of the working day or week in hours;
10. full-time or part-time;
11. right to education/training under čl. 54, if any;
12. duration and conditions of probation, if agreed.

Points 6–9, 11 and 12 may be replaced by a reference to the law, collective agreement or work
rules (pravilnik o radu) (st. 3). Pay must be agreed, set or prescribed in a gross amount (ZOR, article "Način određivanja plaće", st. 1; follows čl. 90.a).

Extra content: remote work (čl. 17.a), posting abroad for more than four consecutive weeks
(čl. 18), permanent seasonal work (čl. 16 st. 7).

## 3. Fixed-term contracts (ugovor na određeno vrijeme, ZOR čl. 12)

- Only exceptionally, for an **objective reason** that must be stated in the contract
  (replacing an absent worker, work limited by its nature to a term or event) (st. 1, 3).
- One contract: maximum **3 years** (st. 2).
- With the same worker: at most **3 successive fixed-term contracts**, total duration (including
  the first) not over 3 years (st. 4) – this "max three contracts" rule came with NN 151/22.
- "Successive" = no break or a break of up to 3 months, also across related employers
  (povezani poslodavci) (st. 5, 6). Any amendment that extends the term counts as a new
  successive contract (st. 8).
- Longer than 3 years only to replace an absent worker, to finish an EU-funded project, or when
  a special law or collective agreement allows it (st. 7).
- After the 3-year limit (or the last successive contract) a new fixed-term contract with the
  same worker requires a gap of at least **6 months** (st. 9).
- Breach, or continuing to work after expiry → deemed indefinite (st. 10).
- Seasonal contracts (up to 9 months per year, čl. 16 st. 2) are outside st. 2, 4 and 9 (st. 11).
- Equal treatment with indefinite-term workers (čl. 13); after 6 months (and completed
  probation) the worker may ask for an indefinite contract; the employer answers in writing
  within 30 days (60 days if fewer than 20 workers) (čl. 13 st. 5–8).

## 4. Probation (probni rad, ZOR čl. 53)

- Maximum **6 months** (st. 3). May be extended in proportion to absences (sick leave,
  maternity/parental rights, paid leave), but the total before and after the interruption may
  not exceed 6 months (st. 4, 5).
- Not allowed again for the same job with the same worker after a contract with probation ends
  (st. 7), nor for a changed-conditions contract under čl. 123 (st. 2).
- Notice period on probation: at least **one week** (st. 10). Failing probation is a ground for
  regular dismissal (čl. 115 st. 1 t. 4).

## 5. Part-time, additional work, on-call

- Part-time (nepuno radno vrijeme, ZOR čl. 62): total hours with all employers may not exceed
  40/week except dodatni rad (st. 2); pay and rights (jubilarna, regres, božićnica) pro rata
  unless agreed otherwise (st. 5). After 6 months the worker may request full time; reasoned
  written reply within 30 days (60 if fewer than 20 workers) (ZOR čl. 63 st. 5–8).
- **Dodatni rad** (additional work for another employer, ZOR čl. 18.a–18.c, NN 151/22): a worker
  who already works 40 h/week (one full-time job, or several part-time jobs totalling 40 h)
  may conclude a contract on additional work:
  - must notify each main employer in writing **before** starting;
  - **max 8 hours per week**; with an uneven schedule up to 16 h/week but on average no more
    than 8 h/week over 4 months (6 months by collective agreement), and only with the worker's
    written voluntary consent;
  - the main employer may require the worker to stop for objective reasons (competition ban,
    overlap with working time);
  - no overtime may be ordered by the additional-work employer except force majeure (ZOR čl. 65
    st. 8).
  The old "180 hours per year" rule from before 2023 no longer applies. Pay from dodatni rad is
  salary (taxed as dohodak od nesamostalnog rada), not a tax-free amount.
- **On-call / stand-by (pripravnost)**: time when the worker is available to respond but is not
  at the workplace is **not working time**; its duration and compensation are set by the
  contract or collective agreement; time actually spent working on call is working time
  (ZOR čl. 60 st. 2–4).

## 6. Work from home vs remote work (since 1 Jan 2023, NN 151/22)

| | Rad na izdvojenom mjestu rada | Rad na daljinu |
|---|---|---|
| Definition (ZOR čl. 17) | work from home or another agreed place that is not the employer's premises (st. 1) | always via ICT, and the worker **chooses where** to work, possibly changing (st. 2) |
| Occupational safety | the place counts as a place of work under OSH rules | not considered a place of work under OSH rules |
| Cost reimbursement | employer **must** reimburse costs if the arrangement is permanent or exceeds 7 working days in a calendar month (čl. 17.a st. 1 t. 4; čl. 17.b st. 3) | only if agreed (čl. 17.a st. 2 t. 3) |
| Extra contract content | organisation of work, time recording, equipment, costs, participation rights, duration (čl. 17.a st. 1) | ZOR čl. 15 data + right to choose place + items of st. 1 except equipment/costs (čl. 17.a st. 2) |

- Tax-free lump sum for work at an izdvojeno mjesto rada: up to **EUR 4 per day, max EUR 70 per
  month** (Pravilnik o porezu na dohodak čl. 7, as listed by RRiF for 2025, unchanged in 2026) –
  see `non-taxable-payments-2026.md`.
- Workers may request temporary work from home for health, pregnancy, children up to 8, or care
  duties; written reasoned reply within 15 days (čl. 17.c).
- Emergencies (epidemic, earthquake...) allow ordering work from home without amending the
  contract; beyond 30 days a proper contract must be offered (čl. 17 st. 5, 6).
- "Right to disconnect": during rest and leave the parties respect work–life balance and
  non-availability for professional communication, except urgent need (ZOR, article
  "Raspored radnog vremena", st. 8; NN 151/22).

## 7. Platform work

ZOR (NN 151/22, applying from 1 January 2024) has a separate chapter "Uređenje rada putem
digitalne radne platforme": definitions of platform, aggregator, presumption of employment
criteria and transparency duties. The EU Platform Work Directive 2024/2831 (transposition due
2 December 2026) is planned in the 2026 ZOR amendment – **VERIFY**.

## 8. Ugovor o djelu / autorski ugovor vs employment (disguised employment)

- If the work has the features of employment (personal work, under instructions, in the
  employer's organisation and working time, for pay), it must be an employment contract
  regardless of the label: a contract with the features of employment is deemed an employment
  contract unless the employer proves otherwise (ZOR čl. 10 st. 2). Labour inspectors reclassify and fine;
  the Zakon o suzbijanju neprijavljenoga rada (announced 2026) – **VERIFY** status.
- Tax treatment of ugovor o djelu / author contracts (drugi dohodak) is covered by
  `croatia:hr-business-admin`.

## 9. Directors (direktor / član uprave)

- A management-board member may be employed under a ZOR contract or act under a management
  agreement (ugovor o poslovodstvu / menadžerski ugovor). ZOR čl. 4 st. 4 excludes the ZOR rules on fixed-term
  contracts, termination, notice periods and severance for such persons.
- 2026 minimum contribution base for an employed board member working full time:
  **EUR 1,295.45/month** (Naredba NN 150/2025, coefficient 0.65 × average wage EUR 1,993.00).
  `hr_salary.py` does not enforce this – compute contributions on at least that base.

## 10. Collective agreements and work rules

- Employers with **20+ workers** must adopt work rules (pravilnik o radu) on pay, organisation,
  dignity and anti-discrimination, unless a collective agreement covers them (ZOR čl. 26).
- Extended collective agreements (proširena primjena) bind the whole sector (e.g. construction,
  hospitality/tourism, retail – **VERIFY** which are currently extended on mrosp.gov.hr). The
  MROSP note on the 2026 minimum wage mentions sector minimums in construction from
  EUR 1,000 to 2,200.
- 20+ workers: appoint a person trusted by workers to oversee personal-data processing
  (ZOR čl. 29 st. 6). Employee data/GDPR → `croatia:hr-legal-compliance`.

## 11. Whistleblowing, OSH, equality

- **Zakon o zaštiti prijavitelja nepravilnosti** (NN 46/22): employers with **at least 50
  workers** must set up an internal reporting system and appoint a confidential person
  (povjerljiva osoba) and deputy on the proposal of at least 20 % of workers; acknowledge a
  report within 7 days (narodne-novine.nn.hr 2022_04_46_572; mpudt.gov.hr reminder).
- **Zaštita na radu** (Zakon o zaštiti na radu): risk assessment (procjena rizika), trained
  OSH officer or the employer itself (depending on size), worker training before starting,
  first aid – **VERIFY** thresholds in the Act and Pravilnik before advising a specific employer.
- Equal pay for women and men for equal work or work of equal value; pay criteria cannot be a
  business secret; on request the employer gives criteria for comparable workers (ZOR, articles
  "Način određivanja plaće" st. 5–6 and "Jednakost plaća žena i muškaraca"). Pay transparency (Directive 2023/970): transposition deadline was
  **7 June 2026**; as of October 2026 the Croatian amendment was still a draft (planned:
  pay levels and worker categories, mandatory criteria skills/effort/responsibility/conditions,
  reporting via a state system "NSTP" that generates the gender pay gap report). **VERIFY**.
