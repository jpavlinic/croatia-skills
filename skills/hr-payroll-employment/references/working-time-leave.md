# Working time, rest and leave – Croatia, researched 2026-10-25

Primary source: Zakon o radu (ZOR), consolidated NN 93/14 … 64/23 (zakon.hr). Holidays and
working-day counts → `croatia:hr-calendar`. A ZOR amendment planned for late 2026 would extend
carry-over of old annual leave after long sick leave or parental leave to 15 months after the
end of the year (unija.com, October 2026) – **VERIFY** whether it has been adopted.

## 1. Working time

| Rule | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Full time | max **40 h/week**; 40 h if nothing else is set | ZOR čl. 61 |
| Part-time with several employers | total max 40 h/week, except dodatni rad | ZOR čl. 62 st. 2 |
| Additional work (dodatni rad) | max 8 h/week (uneven: up to 16 h, average 8 h over 4 months) | ZOR čl. 18.b |
| Overtime – weekly cap | total work max **50 h/week** | ZOR čl. 65 st. 3 |
| Overtime – yearly cap | **180 h/year**, or **250 h/year** if agreed by collective agreement | ZOR čl. 65 st. 4 |
| Overtime order | written request; if oral, confirm in writing within 7 days | ZOR čl. 65 st. 1–2 |
| Overtime bans/consent | banned for minors; pregnant workers, parents of a child under 8 and part-timers with several employers only with written voluntary consent (except force majeure) | ZOR čl. 65 st. 5–6 |
| Uneven schedule (nejednaki raspored) | 1 month – 1 year; max 50 h/week (60 h by collective agreement); average max 48 h/week over 4 months (6 by CA) | ZOR čl. 66 |
| Redistributed time (preraspodjela) | max 48 h/week; 56 h (60 h seasonal) only by CA + written consent; max 4 months (6 by CA) | ZOR čl. 67 |
| Schedule notice | at least **one week** in advance; urgent changes before work starts | ZOR, "Raspored radnog vremena" st. 5–6 |
| Night work | 22:00–06:00 (agriculture 22:00–05:00); minors outside industry 20:00–06:00; night worker average max 8 h per 24 h over 4 months | ZOR čl. 69 |
| Minors at night | banned, narrow exceptions (never 00:00–04:00) | ZOR čl. 70 |
| Shortened time for hazardous work | equals full time for pay and rights | ZOR čl. 64 |

On-call (pripravnost) outside the workplace is not working time; work done on call is
(ZOR čl. 60 st. 2–4).

## 2. Breaks and rest

- **Break (stanka)**: at least **30 minutes** for 6+ hours/day (minors: 30 min for 4.5+ h);
  counts as working time (ZOR čl. 73).
- **Daily rest**: at least **12 hours** uninterrupted in each 24 h (ZOR čl. 74). In special
  cases (e.g. seasonal work) shorter rest is allowed – not under 10 h daily / 20 h weekly, or
  8 h daily by collective agreement – with compensatory rest right after (ZOR, special-cases
  article before chapter 11, čl. 89 – **VERIFY** numbering).
- **Weekly rest**: at least **24 hours** uninterrupted plus the daily rest, normally on Sunday
  and the day before or after (ZOR čl. 75).

## 3. Records

Employers keep daily working-time records (start/end, overtime, night, Sunday and holiday
hours, leave, sick leave) as part of evidencija o radnicima (ZOR čl. 5; Pravilnik o sadržaju i
načinu vođenja evidencije o radnicima, NN 73/17 and amendments – **VERIFY** current text).
Remote workers: the method of recording time must be in the contract (ZOR čl. 17.a).

## 4. Increased pay (povećana plaća, ZOR čl. 94)

- Workers have a right to increased pay for difficult conditions, **overtime, night work, Sunday
  work, work on public holidays and statutory non-working days**, in an amount set by the
  collective agreement, work rules or contract.
- **Sunday: at least +50 % per hour** (statutory minimum since NN 151/22, 1 Jan 2023).
- Other percentages are not fixed by ZOR; without a contractual basis the worker gets an
  "appropriate" increase (what is usually paid; otherwise set by the court) (st. 4–5). Common
  collective-agreement values (e.g. overtime +50 %, night +30–40 %, holiday +50–150 %) are
  examples only – check the applicable agreement.
- Increases must be calculated on at least the minimum-wage amount (ZOR čl. 90 st. 5); the
  Zakon o minimalnoj plaći excludes such increases from the minimum wage itself.

## 5. Annual leave (godišnji odmor)

| Rule | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum | **4 weeks** per calendar year (5-day week = **20 working days**) | ZOR čl. 77 st. 1 |
| Minors and workers in jobs where harm cannot be prevented | at least **5 weeks** | ZOR čl. 77 st. 1 |
| Longer leave | by CA, works-council agreement, work rules or contract (typical extra days for seniority, children, disability) | ZOR čl. 77 st. 2 |
| Waiting period | full right after **6 months** of continuous employment (first job or a break > 8 days) | ZOR čl. 77 st. 3 |
| Pro rata | 1/12 per month; on leaving, pro rata for that year; half a day or half a month rounds up | ZOR čl. 78, čl. 79 st. 4 |
| Counted in | working days per the worker's weekly schedule; holidays, sick leave and paid leave days do not count | ZOR čl. 79 |
| Pay during leave | at least the average pay of the previous **3 months** (all cash and in-kind pay for work) | ZOR čl. 81 |
| Cash instead of leave | void, except compensation when employment ends | ZOR čl. 80, čl. 82 |
| Splitting | at least **2 continuous weeks** in the year, unless agreed otherwise | ZOR čl. 83 |
| Carry-over | rest may be used by **30 June** next year; leave missed due to illness/parental leave by 30 June after return (parental leave: until end of the year of return) | ZOR čl. 84 |
| Schedule | employer sets the plan by **30 June**; notifies the worker at least **15 days** before; the worker may pick **1 day** at will with 3 days' notice | ZOR čl. 85 |

"Regres" (holiday pay) is not a statutory right; it comes from CAs, work rules or contracts and
is tax-free together with the Christmas bonus up to EUR 700/year (see
`non-taxable-payments-2026.md`).

## 6. Paid leave (plaćeni dopust, ZOR čl. 86)

- Important personal needs (marriage, birth of a child, serious illness or death of a close
  family member, etc.): **7 working days per year** in total, unless the CA, work rules or
  contract set more.
- Blood donation: **1 paid day** per donation, on top of the 7 days (st. 5–8).
- Close family includes spouse, relatives in direct line and their spouses, siblings,
  step-children, adopted and foster children, step-parents, persons the worker must support by
  law, and extramarital or (informal) life partners (st. 3).
- Paid leave counts as time at work (st. 9). Unpaid leave (neplaćeni dopust) is by agreement
  (ZOR čl. 87).

## 7. Sick leave (bolovanje, privremena nesposobnost za rad – PNR)

Source: Zakon o obveznom zdravstvenom osiguranju (ZOZO) and Pravilnik NN 37/26; hzzo.hr page
"Naknada plaće za vrijeme privremene nesposobnosti za rad" (updated 16 April 2026).

- Doctor (izabrani doktor) certifies the start and end.
- **Employer pays the first 42 days** of sick leave for illness or injury at its own cost
  (7 days for an invalid rada); from **day 43** the employer still pays but on HZZO's account
  and claims a refund (HZZO repays within 45 days).
- Benefit base: average pay of the **last 6 months** before the month of the sick leave.
- HZZO-funded rates: **70 %** of the base for the first 6 months, **80 %** afterwards, capped at
  **EUR 995.45/month** (225.5 % of the budget base). 100 % (still capped at 995.45) for
  pregnancy complications, care of a sick child under 7, organ donation, carriers.
- **100 % without cap**: work injury and occupational disease, maternity leave, leave after the
  death of a child.
- Minimum: 70 % of the base, but not less than **EUR 353.15/month** (80 % of the budget base;
  implies a budget base of EUR 441.44) for full time.
- Care for a sick child aged 7+: max 40 days per illness, 70 %, cap EUR 995.45, paid by employer
  on HZZO's account from day 1. Care of a spouse/child over 18: max 20 days.
- Employer-funded first 42 days: ZOZO sets at least 70 % of the base; collective agreements
  often pay more (e.g. 85–100 %) – check the CA.
- Sick leave is not a valid reason for dismissal (ZOR čl. 117 st. 1) and notice does not run
  during sick leave (ZOR čl. 121 st. 5) – see `termination.md`.

## 8. Maternity and parental leave (Zakon o rodiljnim i roditeljskim potporama, NN 152/22, 34/25)

Source: hzzo.hr "Rodiljne i roditeljske potpore" (2026).

| Right | Duration | Benefit 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Rodiljni dopust (maternity) | from 28 days before birth (at the earliest 45) until the child is **6 months**; mandatory to day 70 after birth | **100 %** of the base, **no cap** (with 6 months continuous / 9 months in 2 years prior insurance; otherwise EUR 701.89 = 159 % of budget base) |
| Očinski dopust (paternity) | **20 working days** (one child), **30 working days** (twins or more) – since the NN 34/25 amendment (previously 10/15) | **100 %**, no cap, from the state budget |
| Roditeljski dopust (parental) | **8 months** for the 1st/2nd child (4 + 4; 6 months if one parent uses it); **30 months** for twins / 3rd+ child; 2 non-transferable months per parent; usable until the child is 8 | first 6 months (one parent) / 8 months (both): 100 % of base, max **EUR 3,001.79**, min EUR 701.89; rest of the twins/3rd+ period: EUR 803.42 (min EUR 503.24) |

Protection: dismissal is banned during pregnancy and these leaves (ZOR čl. 34) – see
`termination.md`.
