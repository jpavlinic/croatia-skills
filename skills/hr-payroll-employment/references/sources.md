   published, and its effective date and new article numbers.
2. Whether payroll may apply the youth relief monthly (official guidance: annual assessment).
3. ZoS NN 55/2026 domestic-worker ratio (20 %/10 % vs reported 10 %), accommodation and
   notification details.
4. Exact JOPPD submission deadlines by payment type.
5. Employer health-contribution exemptions (young workers etc.) in the Zakon o doprinosima.
6. Foreign per-diem country amounts; commuting by private car rules.
7. Student contract details for part-time students and the tax rate above EUR 12,000.
8. Article numbers marked VERIFY in the references (works-council consultation/consent,
   special-case rest, non-residents' deduction).
9. Digital-nomad means-of-subsistence amount for 2026.
