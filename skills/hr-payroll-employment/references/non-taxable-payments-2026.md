# Non-taxable payments 2026 (neoporezivi primici) – Croatia, researched 2026-10-25

Legal basis: Zakon o porezu na dohodak (ZPD) čl. 8–9 and **Pravilnik o porezu na dohodak**
(NN 10/17 and amendments; these amounts last changed from 1 Jan 2025 with the higher EUR 600
deduction – amendment published in NN 16/2025, **VERIFY**; a later amendment raised only the
student coefficient from 1 Jan 2026), **čl. 7 st. 2** (table
of amounts for employees; items 1–18 are coefficients × the EUR 600 basic personal deduction,
čl. 7 st. 3).

Sources: porezna-uprava.gov.hr "Neoporezive naknade, potpore, darovi, nagrade, dnevnice, dodaci,
otpremnine…" (2026); porezna-uprava.gov.hr "Neoporezivi primici od 1. siječnja 2025."
(coefficients); RRiF "Neoporezivi primitci od 1. siječnja 2025." (full table); RRiF
"Neoporezivi primitci u 2026. godini" (30 Jan 2026: **amounts unchanged in 2026**).

Rules of use:
- These amounts are **free of income tax and contributions** only up to the limit and only when
  the conditions are met; the excess is salary (taxed and grossed up).
- They are **annual per worker per employer** unless stated (monthly/daily/per event); several
  employers in a year → each limit applies across the year as the Pravilnik states (**VERIFY**
  for multiple employers).
- Pay cashless where required; report on JOPPD with the correct non-taxable code.
- Whether the worker is **entitled** to them depends on the collective agreement, work rules or
  contract (ZOR čl. 90.a); the tax rules only cap the tax-free part.
- Some old summaries still quote 2024 values (e.g. severance EUR 896, support EUR 560): those
  were based on the old EUR 560 deduction and are **out of date**.

## 7. Not employees

Students and pupils via authorised intermediaries: special tax-free amount EUR 4,800 in 2026
(coefficient 8.0 × 600) plus the annual basic deduction, i.e. **EUR 12,000/year** – see
`student-work.md`. Apprentice/dual-education pay up to EUR 300/month; scholarships up to
EUR 600/month (excellence EUR 900) (Pravilnik čl. 6).

## 8. Practical checks

- Christmas bonus + regres + Easter bonus together > EUR 700 → only the excess is taxed.
- Performance bonus (EUR 1,200) is separate from occasional bonuses (EUR 700).
- Part-time workers: the tax limits are not pro-rated by law; entitlement may be pro rata under
  ZOR čl. 62 st. 5.
- Never present 2026 limits as valid for 2027 – check the Pravilnik amendments each December.
