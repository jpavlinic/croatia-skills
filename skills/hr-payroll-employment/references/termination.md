# Termination of employment – Croatia, researched 2026-10-25

Primary source: Zakon o radu (ZOR), consolidated NN 93/14 … 64/23 (zakon.hr). Tax treatment of
severance: Pravilnik o porezu na dohodak čl. 7 (see `non-taxable-payments-2026.md`).

## 1. Ways employment ends (ZOR čl. 112)

Death of the worker; death of an employer who is a natural person or craftsman (unless the craft
is transferred); craft ending by law; **expiry of a fixed-term contract**; the worker reaching
**65 years and 15 years of pension service** (unless agreed otherwise); **mutual agreement**;
notice of a final decision on invalidity pension for total loss of work capacity; **dismissal
(otkaz)**; court decision. A company deleted from the register ends all contracts.

## 2. Mutual agreement (sporazumni prestanak)

Must be in writing (ZOR čl. 113). No notice period, no statutory severance (parties may agree
one). Agreements proposed by the employer count toward collective redundancy (čl. 127 st. 2).
Unemployment-benefit consequences at HZZ differ by reason – **VERIFY** on hzz.hr.

## 3. Regular dismissal (redoviti otkaz, ZOR čl. 115)

With notice and a justified reason:

1. **poslovno uvjetovani** – no further need for the job for economic, technological or
   organisational reasons;
2. **osobno uvjetovani** – the worker cannot properly perform due to lasting traits or abilities;
3. **uvjetovan skrivljenim ponašanjem** – breach of employment obligations;
4. **failed probation**.

- Business-reason dismissal: employer must consider length of service, age and maintenance
  obligations – not required if fewer than **20 workers** (st. 2–3). No new hire for the same job
  for **6 months**; if the need arises, offer it first to the dismissed worker (st. 5–6).
- Worker may resign with notice without giving a reason (st. 4).
- Before misconduct dismissal: written warning about the breach and the possibility of dismissal
  (unless unreasonable), and a chance to defend (ZOR čl. 119).
- Business- and personal-reason dismissal is justified only if the employer cannot employ the
  worker on other work or retrain them (ZOR čl. 115 – see full text; **VERIFY** paragraph).
- Form: **written, reasoned, delivered** to the worker (ZOR čl. 120).

## 4. Extraordinary dismissal (izvanredni otkaz, ZOR čl. 116)

Without notice, for a particularly serious breach or other particularly important fact making
continuation impossible; must be given within **15 days** of learning of the fact. Available to
both sides (e.g. the worker for unpaid salary, ZOR čl. 92 st. 6 – article on payment).

## 5. Invalid reasons and protected persons

- **Temporary absence due to illness or injury is not a valid reason** (ZOR čl. 117 st. 1); nor
  complaints, lawsuits or reports to authorities, nor good-faith corruption reports (st. 2–3).
- **Ban on dismissal (ZOR čl. 34)** during pregnancy, maternity, parental, adoption and paternity
  leave, half-time work for child care, leave of a pregnant/breastfeeding worker, and care of a
  child with severe disabilities. A dismissal is **void** if the employer knew, or if the worker
  informs the employer within **15 days** of delivery with a doctor's certificate. Exceptions:
  death of an employer natural person, end of a craft by law, deletion of a sole trader, and
  business-reason dismissal during liquidation.
- Workers with work injury/occupational disease, works-council members, union representatives
  and some others: dismissal needs **works council or union consent** in specific cases
  (ZOR provisions on works council consent and union representatives – **VERIFY** exact
  articles, commonly cited čl. 151 and čl. 188).

## 6. Works council consultation

Before decisions important for workers, including dismissal, the employer must consult the works
council (radničko vijeće) where one exists, providing the relevant information (ZOR, article on
consultation with the works council, "Prije donošenja odluke važne za položaj radnika", commonly
cited as čl. 150). A dismissal without the required consultation is null – **VERIFY** in
čl. 150 st. 3 text. If there is no works council, the union representative has the council's
rights (**VERIFY**).

## 7. Notice periods (ZOR čl. 122)

Minimum notice for regular dismissal by the employer, by continuous service with the same
employer:

| Service | Minimum notice |
|---|---|
| under 1 year | **2 weeks** |
| 1 year | **1 month** |
| 2 years | **1 month and 2 weeks** |
| 5 years | **2 months** |
| 10 years | **2 months and 2 weeks** |
| 20 years | **3 months** |
| 20+ years and age 50+ | +2 weeks (= 3 months 2 weeks) |
| 20+ years and age 55+ | +1 month (= 4 months) |

- Misconduct dismissal: **half** the above periods (st. 3).
- Worker released from work during notice: full pay compensation and all rights (st. 4).
- Job-search time off: at least **4 hours per week** paid during notice (st. 5).
- When the worker resigns: a shorter period may be agreed; with a particularly important reason
  the worker's notice cannot exceed **1 month** (st. 6–7).
- A worker aged 65 with 15 years of pension service has no right to notice (st. 8).
- Probation dismissal: at least **1 week** (ZOR čl. 53 st. 10).

Running of notice (ZOR čl. 121): starts on delivery; **does not run** during pregnancy,
maternity/parental/paternity leave, sick leave (including work injury), military duty; it does
run during annual leave and paid leave. If the worker was released from work before the sick
leave began, notice runs during sick leave unless the CA/contract says otherwise. With
interrupted notice due to illness, employment ends at the latest **6 months** after notice
started (st. 8).

## 8. Severance (otpremnina, ZOR čl. 126)

- Due when the **employer** dismisses after **2 years of continuous work**, **except** for
  misconduct dismissal and workers who have 65 years and 15 years of pension service.
- Minimum: **1/3 of the average monthly pay of the last 3 months for each completed year** with
  that employer.
- Cap: **6 average monthly pays**, unless law, CA, work rules or contract say otherwise.
- Work injury/occupational disease where no suitable job can be offered: at least **double**
  severance (ZOR čl. 42).
- Tax 2026: tax-free up to **EUR 960 per completed year** with the employer for business- or
  personal-reason dismissal; EUR 1,200 per year for injury/occupational disease; retirement
  severance EUR 1,500 (Pravilnik o porezu na dohodak čl. 7; porezna-uprava.gov.hr). The excess is
  taxed as salary.

## 9. Changed-contract dismissal (otkaz s ponudom izmijenjenog ugovora, ZOR čl. 123)

Dismissal with an offer of a new contract on changed terms; the worker has at least **8 days**
to answer; accepting the offer still allows a court challenge.

## 10. Collective redundancy (kolektivni višak, ZOR čl. 127–128)

- Triggered when within **90 days** at least **20 workers** may lose their jobs, of whom at least
  **5** by business-reason dismissal (employer-proposed agreements count).
- Consult the works council in good time with written data (reasons, numbers, criteria,
  severance, measures); notify HZZ with the results.
- Contracts may not end within **30 days** of notifying HZZ; HZZ may postpone by another 30 days.

## 11. Disputes and deadlines (ZOR čl. 133)

- The worker first files a written **request to the employer within 15 days** of delivery of the
  decision (or learning of the breach); if the employer does not satisfy it within 15 days, the
  worker may sue within a **further 15 days**. Not needed for damages or money claims.
- Court may order reinstatement (čl. 124) or, if continuation is unacceptable, end the contract
  and award **3–8 monthly salaries** of compensation (čl. 125).
- Unlawful-dismissal risk assessment always needs a lawyer.

## 12. End-of-employment admin

- Return documents and give the HZMO de-registration copy and a certificate of work within
  **15 days** of the end (ZOR čl. 130).
- Pay compensation for unused annual leave (ZOR čl. 82) and severance with a written
  calculation (obračun) within 15 days of payment (ZOR čl. 93).
- De-register from mandatory insurance with HZMO (see `employment-contracts.md`).
- Foreign workers: the employer notifies the police of the end of employment (Zakon o strancima
  as amended by NN 55/2026) – see `foreign-workers.md`.
