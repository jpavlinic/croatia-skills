Common confusions

- "mojaPorezna" does not exist: the portal is **ePorezna**, the app **mPorezna**.
- Porezna uprava content appears under both porezna-uprava.gov.hr and
  porezna.gov.hr (Fiskalizacija pages); link the page you actually checked.
- Write "e-Građani" with the hyphen and capital G; "eGrađani" also appears on
  official pages, but gov.hr uses "e-Građani".
