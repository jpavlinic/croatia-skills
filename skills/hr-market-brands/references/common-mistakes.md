## Wording traps

- **dm** is always lower case, also at the start of a sentence in body text
  where possible ("Drogerija dm …").
- "Hrvatski Telekom" (capital T, the company) vs "telekom" (generic operator).
- Do not call Zagrebačka banka "UniCredit Bank Croatia" or PBZ "Intesa Bank":
  the Croatian names are the brands.
- "Erste&Steiermärkische Bank d.d." is the legal name; "Erste banka" in copy.
- "Plavi oglasnik" is the brand; the site is oglasnik.hr.
- "paketomat" is the Croatian word; avoid "parcel locker" calques like "paketni ormarić" in UI copy unless the brand uses it.
