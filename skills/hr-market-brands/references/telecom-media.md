# Telecom and media

All entries checked 2026-10.

## Telecom operators

Three mobile network operators: **Hrvatski Telekom (HT)**, **A1 Hrvatska**,
**Telemach Hrvatska**. HAKOM market data 2025: total electronic-communications
service revenue 1.87 bn € (+3.6 %); about 471,000 fibre connections, almost
40 % of 1.18 million fixed connections; pay-TV connections over 939,600
(source: https://lidermedia.hr/tvrtke-i-trzista/hakom-trziste-elektronickih-komunikacija-lani-poraslo-za-3-6-posto/ ).
Subscriber market shares per operator: no reliable dated figure was confirmed in
this research. Take them from HAKOM's quarterly reports (hakom.hr) and cite the
quarter.

## Press and portals

Daily press, AZTN 2025: Styria (24sata, Večernji list) 50–60 % of copies sold;
Hanza Media (Jutarnji list, Slobodna Dalmacija) 30–40 %; regional group (Novi
list, Glas Istre, Zadarski list) 10–20 %. 24sata is the best-selling daily;
Jutarnji overtook Večernji in 2025. Source: financije.hr article above.
