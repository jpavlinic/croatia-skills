Instant Payments Regulation: receiving since January 2025, sending since

**Plaćanje pouzećem (cash on delivery)** remains unusually popular: a January
2026 industry summary quotes Adyen data that about 50 % of Croatian online buyers
prefer it (secondary source, VERIFY). Croatian web-shop mock checkouts should
offer pouzeće alongside cards.
