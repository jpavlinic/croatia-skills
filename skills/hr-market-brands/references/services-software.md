# Business software and services popular with Croatian SMEs

All entries checked 2026-10. Since 1 January 2026 every VAT-registered business
in Croatia must send and receive structured eRačuni (e-invoices) through an
**informacijski posrednik** (information intermediary) on the Porezna uprava
list (Fiskalizacija 2.0). Businesses outside VAT can receive with the free
**MikroeRačun** app during 2026.
Source: https://porezna.gov.hr/fiskalizacija/bezgotovinski-racuni/eracun
For the rules themselves see `croatia:hr-fiscalization-dev`.

Whether a product below is currently on the official intermediary list must be
checked on the Porezna uprava list itself; the list changes. Do not claim
"certified" from this file.

Other recurring business services Croatians name: **Fina** (registrations,
e-signature certificates, payment orders), **e-Obrt** (online craft registration,
VERIFY), **HZZO** and **HZMO/REGOS** (social insurance), banks' business
internet banking. Accountants (računovodstveni servisi) usually decide which
software a small business uses.
