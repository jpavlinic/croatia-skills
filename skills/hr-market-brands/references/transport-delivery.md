## Parcels and paketomati (parcel lockers)

HAKOM, 2025 share of parcel volume (other postal services): **Hrvatska pošta
(Paket24) 30.6 %**, **GLS 19.4 %**, **Overseas 15.3 %**, **BOX NOW 15.1 %**
(6.7 % in 2024), **DPD 14.5 %**. Share of parcel revenue: GLS 25.8 %, HP 23.8 %,
Overseas 16 %, DPD 11.6 %, Intereuropa 6.2 %, BOX NOW 5.8 %. HAKOM counted
**2,892 paketomati** at the end of 2025; in HAKOM's 2025 survey 84 % of adults had
used a paketomat.
Source: https://financije.hr/u-godinu-dana-box-now-povecao-trzisni-udio-za-125-posto-pala-tri-najveca-privatna-konkurenta/ (3 September 2026, citing HAKOM)
