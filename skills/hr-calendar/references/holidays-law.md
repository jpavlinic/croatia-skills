# Holidays, working days, deadlines arithmetic, time zone

Status: researched 2026-10-25. Primary source for holidays: **Zakon o blagdanima,
spomendanima i neradnim danima u Republici Hrvatskoj** (Public Holidays, Memorial
Days and Non-Working Days Act), NN 110/19, 72/25, consolidated text on zakon.hr
(`https://www.zakon.hr/z/372/`), current version in force since 1 May 2025.
Below it is called "Zakon o blagdanima".

## 1. Blagdani i neradni dani (public holidays), Zakon o blagdanima čl. 1 st. 1

14 days. Exact statutory names:

| Date | Name (HR, statutory) | English |
|---|---|---|
| 1. siječnja | Nova godina | New Year's Day |
| 6. siječnja | Bogojavljenje ili Sveta tri kralja | Epiphany |
| movable | Uskrs i Uskrsni ponedjeljak | Easter Sunday and Easter Monday |
| movable | Tijelovo | Corpus Christi (Easter + 60 days, a Thursday) |
| 1. svibnja | Praznik rada | Labour Day |
| 30. svibnja | Dan državnosti | Statehood Day |
| 22. lipnja | Dan antifašističke borbe | Anti-Fascist Struggle Day |
| 5. kolovoza | Dan pobjede i domovinske zahvalnosti i Dan hrvatskih branitelja | Victory and Homeland Thanksgiving Day and Day of Croatian Defenders |
| 15. kolovoza | Velika Gospa | Assumption of Mary |
| 1. studenoga | Svi sveti | All Saints' Day |
| 18. studenoga | Dan sjećanja na žrtve Domovinskog rata i Dan sjećanja na žrtvu Vukovara i Škabrnje | Remembrance Day for the Victims of the Homeland War and for Vukovar and Škabrnja |
| 25. prosinca | Božić | Christmas Day |
| 26. prosinca | prvi dan po Božiću, Sveti Stjepan | St Stephen's Day |

Key rules:
- **Sunday holidays are not moved** (čl. 4: "ne prenosi se u idući dan"). A
  holiday on a Saturday is also simply lost; there is no substitute Monday.
- Employees are entitled to wage compensation (naknada plaće) on these days
  (čl. 5). Work on a holiday is paid at an increased rate under Zakon o radu
  čl. 94 st. 1; the amount is set by collective agreement, work rules or the
  employment contract (for Sunday work the increase is at least 50 % per hour under
  the current text NN 93/14 … 64/23; for holiday work no statutory minimum, but čl. 94 st. 4 gives
  a right to an "appropriate" increase if nothing is agreed). Details: `croatia:hr-payroll-employment`.
- The Government may decide which bodies and persons must work on holidays
  (čl. 6). Shops are closed on holidays (Zakon o trgovini čl. 57 st. 3), see
  `sunday-trading.md`.
- **Veliki petak (Good Friday) is not a public holiday**, but it is a TARGET /
  EuroNKS closing day for interbank payments (section 8).
- **Badnjak (24 Dec) and Stara godina (31 Dec) are ordinary working days.**
- Easter Sunday always falls on a Sunday, so it never reduces the number of
  working days, but it still counts as a holiday (shops closed, holiday pay).

Common mistakes:
- 25 June (Dan neovisnosti) and 8 October (Dan Hrvatskoga sabora) are **not**
  days off since 2020; they are memorial days.
- 30 May replaced 25 June as Statehood Day from 2020.
- 18 November became a public holiday from 2020.

## 3. Religious days off for believers (čl. 3)

Members of these faiths have the right to a day off (neradni dan), with wage
compensation (čl. 5); for everyone else it is a normal working day:

| Faith | Day |
|---|---|
| Christians celebrating Christmas on 7 January | 7. siječnja |
| Christians celebrating Easter by the Julian calendar | Uskrsni ponedjeljak (Julian) |
| Islamic faith | Ramazanski bajram and Kurban-bajram: one day of their choice for each |
| Jewish faith | Jom kipur and Roš hašana: one day of their choice for each |

MROSP clarifies the right is one day per listed feast, regardless of how long
the feast lasts. The script computes only 7 January and Orthodox Easter Monday
(`--religious`). Bajram and Jewish feast dates follow lunar/lunisolar calendars:
**VERIFY** each year with the Islamska zajednica u Hrvatskoj and the
Koordinacija židovskih općina u RH (or a reputable calendar); do not compute them.

## 5. Before 2020

The previous law (NN 33/96 … 130/11) had a different list (e.g. 25 June Dan
državnosti, 8 October Dan neovisnosti as days off; no 18 November). The new law
took effect on 1 January 2020 (čl. 8). The script refuses years before 2020; for
older dates read the historical text on narodne-novine.nn.hr.

## 6. Working days (radni dani)

- No single statute defines "radni dan" for all purposes. Common usage and the
  script: **Monday–Friday that is not a public holiday** under čl. 1.
- **Saturday**: not a public holiday, but public authorities, courts and most
  offices do not work on Saturdays, so for procedural deadlines it is treated
  as a day "kad tijelo/sud ne radi" (section 7). Shops may open on Saturdays
  (Zakon o trgovini čl. 57 st. 1: Monday–Saturday, up to 90 h/week). Employers
  with a 6-day week can count Saturdays (`--saturday-working`).
- Weekly rest: the employee has at least 24 h uninterrupted weekly rest, as a
  rule on Sunday plus the day before or after (Zakon o radu čl. 75 st. 1 and 3). Payroll details: `croatia:hr-payroll-employment`.
- Counting: `working-days A B` counts both ends inclusive. `add-working-days D N`
  does not count D itself.
- **Fond sati** (monthly/annual hours fund used in payroll) = (working days +
  holidays on weekdays) × 8 h. 2026: 252 + 9 = 261 days = 2,088 h.

## 7. Procedural deadlines (rokovi) – how they roll over

| Area | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Administrative procedure (ZUP), also tax procedure | Day of service/event not counted; deadline in days runs from the next day. Sundays, holidays and non-working days **do not affect start or running** of the deadline. If the **last day** falls on a Sunday, holiday or another day the authority does not work, it ends on the **next working day**. Months/years: same date number; if missing, last day of the month. | Zakon o općem upravnom postupku (NN 47/09, 110/21) čl. 80, 81; Opći porezni zakon (NN 115/16 … 151/25) čl. 4 applies ZUP to tax bodies unless OPZ says otherwise |
| Civil litigation (ZPP) | Same counting; "Ako posljednji dan roka pada na državni blagdan, ili u nedjelju ili u koji drugi dan kad sud ne radi, rok istječe protekom prvoga idućeg radnog dana." Filing by registered post: day of posting counts as day of filing (čl. 113). | Zakon o parničnom postupku čl. 112 st. 2–4, čl. 113 |
| Contracts / obligations (ZOO) | Days: from the day after the event to the end of the last day. If the last day is a day "kad je zakonom određeno da se ne radi", the next working day counts as the last day. "Početak mjeseca" = 1st, "sredina" = 15th, "kraj" = last day. | Zakon o obveznim odnosima (NN 35/05 … 69/26) čl. 300 |
| Criminal procedure (ZKP) | Similar roll-over rule. | **VERIFY** article (secondary source cites ZKP čl. 90 st. 6) |

Notes:
- Saturday: ZUP and ZPP roll over any day the body or court does not work, so a
  Saturday last day rolls to Monday in practice (script default for `zup`,
  `zpp`; `--saturday-open` disables it). ZOO only rolls over days that a law
  declares non-working; the script rolls Sundays and holidays only. Whether
  Sunday counts under ZOO čl. 300 st. 3 is the prevailing reading, not
  confirmed by case law here (**VERIFY** for disputes).
- Substantive (material-law) deadlines may not roll over at all; some laws set
  their own rule. Always check the specific statute.
- Statutory tax filing dates ("do 15. u mjesecu", "do 30. travnja"): when the
  date falls on a weekend or holiday, Porezna uprava in practice accepts the
  next working day under the ZUP rule via OPZ čl. 4. **VERIFY** for the specific
  form, especially for payments (see `statutory-deadlines.md`).

Script: `deadline 2026-12-10 15 --rule zup` → raw 25.12. (Christmas) → rolls
over 26.12. (Sat, holiday) and 27.12. (Sun) → ends Monday 28.12.2026.

## 8. Payment (bank) working days

- **EuroNKS** (national euro clearing, operated by Fina) works on TARGET working
  days. Closed: Saturdays, Sundays, **1 Jan, Good Friday, Easter Monday, 1 May,
  25 Dec, 26 Dec**. Other Croatian holidays (6 Jan, 30 May, Tijelovo, 22 Jun,
  5 Aug, 15 Aug, 1 Nov, 18 Nov) **are EuroNKS working days** (Fina EuroNKS
  operating rules, version 06-2024; **VERIFY** the current version on fina.hr).
- Porezna uprava notices for 2026 confirm 3 April (Good Friday) and 1 May as
  non-working days for regular interbank orders because "platni sustavi EuroNKS
  i Target ne rade"; **instant payments** run every calendar day regardless of
  holidays (porezna-uprava.gov.hr notice 8485, 2026).
- Bank branches may be closed on Croatian holidays even when EuroNKS works.
- Script: `is-payment-day 2026-04-03` → not a settlement day.

## 9. Time zone and daylight saving time

- Zone: **Europe/Zagreb** (IANA). Standard time = UTC+1 (CET, "srednjoeuropsko
  zonsko vrijeme"), Zakon o računanju vremena (NN 27/93, 33/96) čl. 1. Summer
  time UTC+2 (CEST), čl. 2.
- The Government sets switch dates by decree at least three months before
  summer time starts (čl. 2 st. 2). Current decree: Uredba o ljetnom računanju
  vremena u 2022.–2026. godini (NN 13/22), transposing Directive 2000/84/EC.
  2026: starts 29 March 02:00 → 03:00, ends **25 October 2026** 03:00 → 02:00
  (the repeated hour is "2A" and "2B"). Pattern = EU rule: last Sunday of March
  and last Sunday of October, 01:00 UTC.
- 2027 onwards: a new decree is needed; none was found on 2026-10-25.
  **VERIFY** in Narodne novine. The EU proposal to abolish seasonal clock
  changes (Commission 2018) has not been adopted as far as research found;
  **VERIFY** current status.
- Developers: store UTC, convert with the IANA zone `Europe/Zagreb`; never
  hard-code +01:00. Date-only values (holidays, deadlines) should be plain dates,
  not midnight timestamps. Formatting: `croatia:hr-localization`.

## 10. Month and weekday names

Months are written in lower case in Croatian. In dates use the genitive:
"1. siječnja 2026." (with the ordinal dot and the dot after the year).

| # | Month (nominative) | Genitive (in dates) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | siječanj | siječnja |
| 2 | veljača | veljače |
| 3 | ožujak | ožujka |
| 4 | travanj | travnja |
| 5 | svibanj | svibnja |
| 6 | lipanj | lipnja |
| 7 | srpanj | srpnja |
| 8 | kolovoz | kolovoza |
| 9 | rujan | rujna |
| 10 | listopad | listopada |
| 11 | studeni | studenoga (studenog) |
| 12 | prosinac | prosinca |

Weekdays (lower case): ponedjeljak, utorak, srijeda, četvrtak, petak, subota,
nedjelja. The week starts on Monday (ISO 8601). Usual abbreviations: pon., uto.,
sri., čet., pet., sub., ned.

## 11. Name days (imendani)

Name days are culturally important in Croatia (people congratulate on the feast
of their name saint, e.g. Ivan – 24. lipnja, Marija – Velika Gospa / 15.
kolovoza, Stjepan – 26. prosinca, Nikola – 6. prosinca). They follow the Catholic
calendar of saints and are not legal days. Use a Catholic liturgical calendar
for a full list; do not invent dates.
