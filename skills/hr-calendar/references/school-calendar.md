# School calendar (školski kalendar, praznici / odmori učenika)

Status: researched 2026-10-25.

## 1. How the school year is set

- Each year the minister responsible for education (Ministarstvo znanosti,
  obrazovanja i mladih, MZOM) adopts an **Odluka o početku i završetku nastavne
  godine, broju radnih dana i trajanju odmora učenika osnovnih i srednjih škola
  za školsku godinu YYYY./YYYY.** under Zakon o odgoju i obrazovanju u osnovnoj
  i srednjoj školi čl. 48 st. 4. It is published in Narodne novine, usually in
  May or June before the school year.
- Structure in the decisions for 2024/25, 2025/26 and 2026/27: **two
  polugodišta (semesters)**, a winter break (sometimes split into two parts, the
  second in February), a spring break around Easter, an autumn break in some
  years, and the summer break.
- Minimum **175 teaching days** (160 for final-year secondary students).
- Each school's annual plan (godišnji plan i program rada škole) adds its own
  non-teaching days: dan škole, dan župe, dan općine/grada, excursions, state
  matura exams, etc. (čl. 7 of each decision). So **exact days off differ by
  school and municipality**: always check the school's own calendar.
- The terms "praznici" (colloquial) and "odmor učenika" (official) both mean
  school holidays.

| Item | Dates |
|---|---|
| Teaching year | **7 Sep 2026 – 15 Jun 2027** (final-year secondary: until 21 May 2027) |
| 1st semester | 7 Sep 2026 – 23 Dec 2026 |
| Winter break, part 1 | 24 Dec 2026 – 5 Jan 2027; classes resume **7 Jan 2027** (6 Jan is a public holiday) |
| 2nd semester | 7 Jan 2027 – 15 Jun 2027 (final-year secondary: 21 May 2027) |
| Winter break, part 2 | 22 Feb 2027 – 26 Feb 2027; classes resume 1 Mar 2027 |
| Spring break | 25 Mar 2027 – 2 Apr 2027; classes resume 5 Apr 2027 (Easter Monday 29 Mar 2027 inside the break) |
| Summer break | from 16 Jun 2027 (except pupils with exams, state matura, practical training) |

Vocational pupils with work-based learning may have a different schedule, but
at least 45 working days of holidays per year (čl. 6).

## 4. Earlier year for reference – 2024/2025, Odluka NN 55/2024

2 Sep 2024 – 18 Jun 2025; autumn break 31 Oct – 1 Nov 2024; winter part 1
23 Dec 2024 – 6 Jan 2025; winter part 2 24–28 Feb 2025; spring break
17–25 Apr 2025; summer from 19 Jun 2025. Shows that the autumn break and the
February break are not fixed: they depend on each year's decision.

## 5. 2027/2028 and later

Not published on 2026-10-25. Expect the decision around May/June 2027 in
Narodne novine. Do not extrapolate dates; mark them **VERIFY**.

## 6. Kindergartens and universities

- Kindergartens (vrtići) work all year; schedules are set by the founder (city
  or municipality). No national holiday calendar.
- Higher education: the **academic year runs 1 October – 30 September**
  (Zakon o visokom obrazovanju i znanstvenoj djelatnosti, NN 119/22, per
  Eurydice; same rule as the former act NN 123/03). Teaching is organised in
  semesters (winter and summer); each university sets its own academic calendar
  (exam periods, breaks). **VERIFY** the article number in NN 119/22.
