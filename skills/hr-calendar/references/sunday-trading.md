# Sunday and holiday trading (radne nedjelje, radno vrijeme trgovina)

Status: researched 2026-10-25. Source: **Zakon o trgovini** (Trade Act),
consolidated text NN 87/08, 96/08, 116/08, 76/09, 114/11, 68/13, 30/14, 32/19,
98/19, 32/20, 33/23, 59/26 (zakon.hr `https://www.zakon.hr/z/175/`, version in
force since 17 June 2026). Article 57 was rewritten by NN 33/23 (Zakon o
izmjenama i dopuni Zakona o trgovini, in force **1 July 2023**,
`https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2023_03_33_580.html`).

General information only. Inspection practice and ministry interpretations
can differ from a plain reading; check with the Državni inspektorat or the
ministry responsible for the economy for a specific shop.

## 1. The rules (Zakon o trgovini čl. 57)

| Stavak | Rule |
|---|---|
| st. 1 | The trader sets shop opening hours **Monday–Saturday, up to 90 hours per week** in total, distributed freely. |
| st. 2 | **Shops (prodajni objekti) are closed on Sundays.** |
| st. 3 | **Shops are closed on public holidays.** The Government may by decision designate shops that must work on holidays, under the holidays act. |
| st. 4 | The trader may itself choose **16 Sundays per year as working Sundays**; in those weeks **15 hours** are added to the 90-hour limit, distributed Monday–Sunday. |
| st. 5 | St. 1–4 do **not** apply to shops located inside or forming part of: railway and bus stations, airports, ports open to public traffic, inland ports, ships, aircraft and ferries carrying passengers and vehicles; **petrol stations**; **hospitals**; hotels, premises of cultural and religious institutions and other cultural entities, museums, visitor/interpretation centres, nautical marinas, campsites, family farms (OPG); declared protected nature areas. |
| st. 6 | St. 1–4 do **not** apply to: purchase of primary agricultural products; sale of own agricultural products on stalls at retail and wholesale markets; occasional sales at fairs and public events; **vending machines**; **distance selling** (online shops, phone orders). |
| st. 7 | Newspaper **kiosks** may open on Sundays and holidays **07:00–13:00**. |
| st. 8 | The trader must keep a **record of working hours for each working Sunday** in the current year (paper or electronic). |
| st. 9 | The record must be shown to the inspector during supervision. |
| st. 10 | Porezna uprava must give inspectors **fiscalization data** on a trader's working Sundays on request. |

Transitional (NN 33/23 čl. 12): Sundays worked in 2023 before 1 July 2023 did
not count towards the 16.

Related:
- Čl. 57.a (NN 32/20): in special circumstances the national civil protection
  HQ (Stožer civilne zaštite RH) sets trading hours.
- Čl. 57.b (NN 59/26, new): a local council may restrict **sale of alcoholic
  drinks** in shops in its whole area between **21:00 and 06:00**, for public
  health, public order, cultural heritage or environment reasons; the decision
  goes to the Državni inspektorat within 15 days.
- Čl. 61: opening hours must be displayed clearly on the shop; the trader warns
  customers 15 minutes before closing.

## 2. Penalties (čl. 70, amounts set in euro by NN 33/23)

Breaches of čl. 57 st. 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8 and 9 are misdemeanours under čl. 70
st. 1 (podstavci 9–15): legal person **660–39,810 EUR**, responsible person
in the legal person **530–9,290 EUR**; natural-person craftsman (obrtnik)
under čl. 70 st. 2: **530–13,270 EUR**. Goods/proceeds may be confiscated
(čl. 75). **VERIFY** the current amounts in the consolidated text before quoting.

## 3. Practical reading

- Online shops may take and ship orders on Sundays (distance selling, st. 6); a
  physical click-and-collect pickup point is a shop and falls under the rule
  (interpretation, **VERIFY** with the inspectorate).
- A holiday that falls on a Sunday: the shop is closed under st. 3 (holiday).
  Whether such a day can be one of the 16 chosen Sundays is not addressed
  expressly; the safe reading is no (**VERIFY**).
- The 16 Sundays are chosen by the trader (trgovac). Whether a trader with
  several shops may pick different Sundays per shop is not spelt out in čl. 57
  (**VERIFY** the ministry's interpretation).
- Catering (cafés, restaurants, bakeries serving food) is governed by the
  catering act and local decisions, not by čl. 57; a bakery that also runs a
  retail shop may be caught by čl. 57 for the shop part (**VERIFY** case by case).
- Employees' Sunday work, weekly rest and pay supplements: Zakon o radu and
  collective agreements; see `croatia:hr-payroll-employment`. Zakon o radu
  (NN 93/14 … 64/23) čl. 94 st. 1: work on Sundays, holidays and statutory
  non-working days is paid at an increased rate set by collective agreement,
  work rules or contract, and the increase for each Sunday hour may **not be
  less than 50 %**. Weekly rest is used on Sunday plus the day before or after
  (čl. 75 st. 3).

## 4. Constitutional review and pending changes

- The Constitutional Court (Ustavni sud) in February 2024 rejected the
  proposals to review the 2023 Sunday-closure amendments (10:3 majority); the
  decision was reported as published in NN 21/2024 (secondary sources:
  Novi list, informator.hr). **VERIFY** the decision number on usud.hr.
- Earlier Sunday bans (2003/2004, 2008/2009) were struck down by the
  Constitutional Court, so the regime has changed several times; always check
  the current consolidated text.
- 2026: the ministry announced amendments to the Zakon o obrtu (Crafts Act)
  that would let **family crafts (obiteljski obrti)** in retail open on
  Sundays outside the čl. 57 limits; status on 2026-10-25 **unconfirmed**
  (reported as sent into procedure; the minister was also quoted as saying the
  Trade Act governs this). **VERIFY** in Narodne novine before relying on it.

## 5. Planning helper

Use `scripts/hr_calendar.py holidays <year>` for the closed holiday dates. A
shop's yearly plan typically picks the 16 Sundays around Christmas/New Year,
Easter and the tourist season; whatever is chosen, record every working Sunday
(st. 8).
