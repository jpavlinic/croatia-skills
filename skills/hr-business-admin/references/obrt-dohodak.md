# Obrt on real income (obrt na dohodak), obrt on profit tax, slobodno zanimanje – 2026

## 1. Who this covers

- **Obrt na dohodak:** obrt (ZPD čl. 29 st. 1) that is not paušal: receipts above €60,000, a VAT payer, or the
  owner chose real income because real expenses are high.
- **Slobodno zanimanje** (liberal profession, ZPD čl. 29 st. 2): doctors, lawyers, notaries, auditors,
  engineers, architects, tax advisers, translators/interpreters, tourist guides, scientists, writers,
  lecturers, journalists, artists, athletes and "similar". Always taxed on real income (no paušal). Registration is
  with the profession's chamber/register, then PU (RPO form) and HZMO/HZZO. Journalists, artists and athletes may
  instead be taxed like drugi dohodak (ZPD čl. 30 st. 2).
- **Obrt u sustavu poreza na dobit:** an obrtnik may elect profit tax; it becomes **mandatory** if receipts in
  the previous year exceeded **€1,000,000** (Zakon o porezu na dobit čl. 2 st. 3–4). See §6.

## 2. Books and records (ZPD čl. 34–35; Pravilnik o porezu na dohodak)

- **KPI – Knjiga primitaka i izdataka** (cash-basis income and expenditure book).
- **DI – Popis dugotrajne imovine** (fixed-asset register): assets over **€665.00** with a useful life over one
  year are depreciated, not expensed (ZPD čl. 35 st. 2).
- **Knjiga prometa** (turnover book) unless daily cash turnover is in the KPI (čl. 34 st. 3).
- **Evidencija o tražbinama i obvezama** (receivables/payables record).
- VAT payers also keep VAT records (URA/IRA ledgers; the URA/PPO forms were abolished from 2026, NN 151/25).
- One set of books per taxpayer even with several activities (čl. 34 st. 5).
- Income = business receipts − business expenses (cash basis, ZPD čl. 30). Some expenses are limited
  (e.g. representation, personal car use) – **VERIFY** with the Pravilnik / an accountant.

## 3. Income tax 2026 – the 2024 reform

- **No more city surtax (prirez).** Since 1.1.2024 cities and municipalities set the **income tax rates
  themselves**, within statutory bands (ZPD čl. 19.a, NN 114/23):

  | Local unit | Lower rate | Higher rate |
  |---|---|---|
  | Općina (municipality) | 15–20% | 25–30% |
  | Grad (city) | 15–21% | 25–31% |
  | Large city / county seat (veliki grad, sjedište županije) | 15–22% | 25–32% |
  | Grad Zagreb | 15–23% | 25–33% |
  | No decision adopted (default) | 20% | 30% |

- **Threshold 2026: lower rate on the annual tax base up to €60,000.00**, higher rate above (ZPD čl. 19, NN
  152/24, from 1.1.2025; it was €50,400 in 2024).
- The rate is that of the taxpayer's **residence** (prebivalište). Rates change: 13 local units changed rates
  from 1.1.2026 (e.g. Rijeka moved to 20% / 25%; Informator, Dec 2025). Look up the current table on
  porezna-uprava.gov.hr ("Pregled stopa poreza na dohodak") – **VERIFY** for the specific city; do not assume.
- **Basic personal allowance (osnovni osobni odbitak) 2026: €600.00 per month** (ZPD čl. 14 st. 1), plus
  allowances for dependants.
- Tax base = income from all sources (employment + self-employment) − allowances. Youth relief (100% up to
  25 / 50% 26–30) applies only to salary income (ZPD čl. 24), not to obrt income.

## 4. Advances (predujmovi) and annual return

- **Monthly income-tax advances** are set from last year's annual return: annual tax ÷ months of activity,
  paid **by the last day of the month for the previous month** (ZPD čl. 37 st. 1, 5).
- **First year: no advances** until the first annual return (čl. 37 st. 4).
- PU may change advances on request (čl. 37 st. 3) – useful when income falls.
- **Annual return (DOH form) by the end of February** for the previous year (ZPD čl. 50 st. 2), via ePorezna.
- VAT payers additionally file PDV returns (see `pdv-vat.md`).

## 5. Contributions 2026 (Zakon o doprinosima čl. 64–67; NN 150/2025)

Monthly base = average wage (€1,993.00 for 2026) × coefficient (ZOD čl. 66). The base is fixed by law, not by
actual income: there is no annual top-up for higher income in 2026, and no reduced base by years of activity
in ZOD čl. 66 (**VERIFY** if a newer amendment introduced one).

| Insured as | Coefficient | Base 2026 | MIO I 15% | MIO II 5% | HZZO 16.5% | **Monthly total** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Obrt na dohodak | 0.65 | 1,295.45 | 194.32 | 64.77 | 213.75 | **472.84** |
| Slobodno zanimanje (general) | 1.1 | 2,192.30 | 328.85 | 109.62 | 361.73 | **≈ 800.19** |
| Slobodno zanimanje: nurse, dental technician, physiotherapist, film worker, journalist | 0.65 | 1,295.45 | 194.32 | 64.77 | 213.75 | **472.84** |
| Obrt na dobit (profit-tax obrt) | 1.1 | 2,192.30 | 328.85 | 109.62 | 361.73 | **≈ 800.19** |

Sources: ZOD čl. 66; NN 150/2025 čl. 6. Pillar-I-only insured pay MIO 20% instead of 15% + 5% (same total).

- Due by the **15th of the month for the previous month** (ZOD čl. 67). Self-calculated and reported (JOPPD).
- Contributions are a deductible expense for income tax (they reduce the tax base).
- Higher voluntary bases are possible (NN 150/2025 čl. 11: 0.5–6.0 × average wage) – raises pension rights.
- Obrt run next to full-time employment (druga djelatnost): annual contributions on the real annual income at
  reduced rates (MIO 10%, HZZO 7.5%; ZOD čl. 13 st. 3, čl. 14 st. 2) with a minimum annual base (NN 150/2025
  čl. 12: €15,545.40 for druga djelatnost on dohodak) – **VERIFY** how PU applies the minimum to your case.

## 6. Obrt on profit tax (obrt na dobit)

- Elected by statement, or mandatory above €1M receipts in the previous year (Zakon o porezu na dobit čl. 2
  st. 3–4). Profit tax 10% (revenue < €1M) or 18% (≥ €1M) (čl. 28).
- Double-entry bookkeeping and annual financial statements under the Zakon o računovodstvu (applies to profit
  taxpayers) – effectively an accountant is needed.
- Owner contributions on €2,192.30 base (table above).
- Profit withdrawn by the owner is taxed as capital income (12%) – **VERIFY** treatment of withdrawals in
  an obrt on profit tax with an accountant.
- Usually chosen when the business is too big for paušal but the owner does not want a company. Compare with
  j.d.o.o./d.o.o. in `legal-forms.md`.

## 7. Withdrawing money

- **Obrt (paušal or dohodak):** the business account is the owner's money; withdrawals are not taxed again
  (the tax is on income, not on withdrawals). Keep business and private payments separate anyway.
- **d.o.o./j.d.o.o.:** money belongs to the company; withdrawal only as salary, dividend (12%), or other taxed
  payment. See `doo-jdoo.md` §5.

## 8. Paušal vs dohodak – how to reason (no fixed rule)

1. Under €60,000 receipts with low expenses, paušal is almost always cheaper and simpler: in 2026 a class-7
   paušalist pays about €1,080 tax + €3,491.76 contributions.
2. On dohodak the same person pays €5,674.08 contributions (472.84 × 12) plus income tax on (receipts − expenses
   − contributions − allowances) at local rates, plus bookkeeping costs.
3. Dohodak can win when **real expenses are high** (materials, subcontractors, equipment) or the activity runs at
   a loss in early years.
4. Above €60,000: compare obrt na dohodak vs d.o.o. (`legal-forms.md` §3). Have an accountant model both with
   real figures; give ranges, not a single "answer".
