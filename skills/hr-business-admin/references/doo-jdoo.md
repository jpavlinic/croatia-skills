## 1. d.o.o. – društvo s ograničenom odgovornošću

- **Minimum share capital (temeljni kapital): €2,500.00** (ZTD čl. 389 st. 2, NN 114/22 – set on euro
  conversion from 1.1.2023; previously HRK 20,000).
- Minimum share (poslovni udio) €10, in multiples of 10 (ZTD čl. 390 st. 1).
- Before registration each founder pays in **at least 1/4 of a cash share**, and total cash paid in is at least
  1/4 of the capital, i.e. at least €625 for a €2,500 company (ZTD čl. 390 st. 2). gov.hr says the rest must be
  paid within one year of registration (**VERIFY** the statutory basis).
- Contributions in kind allowed (čl. 390 st. 3–4).
- **Liability:** members are not personally liable for company debts (beyond unpaid capital), but directors are
  liable for breaches of duty, and tax law can make directors/members liable for unpaid taxes in abuse cases
  (Opći porezni zakon – **VERIFY** article).
- Founding: notarial deed (izjava o osnivanju for one founder, društveni ugovor for several) or electronically
  through **START** without a notary (gov.hr). See `registration-and-closing.md`.

## 2. j.d.o.o. – jednostavno društvo s ograničenom odgovornošću (simple LLC)

ZTD čl. 390.a (NN 40/19, 114/22, 130/23):
- **At most five members and one management board member** (član uprave) (st. 1). (Older sources say three
  members: that was the pre-2019 rule.)
- Founded only with the **statutory template forms** (obrasci zapisnika) by a notary or via START; no deviation
  from the Act (st. 1).
- **Minimum capital €1.00**, minimum share €1.00, in whole euros, cash only, fully paid before registration
  (st. 3–4).
- **Mandatory legal reserves:** 1/4 of annual profit (less prior-year loss) must go to legal reserves (st. 5). The
  reserves may be used only to increase capital or cover losses – so the owners cannot withdraw that quarter as
  dividends.
- If the company is close to insolvency, the general meeting must be called immediately (st. 6).
- **Conversion:** once capital is increased to **€2,500 or more**, the j.d.o.o. rules (st. 3–6) stop applying;
  the company **may keep the "j.d.o.o." name** (st. 7). No separate conversion to d.o.o. is required. Capital is
  often raised by converting the accumulated reserves (st. 5 t. 1).
- Taxes, bookkeeping and director rules are the same as for a d.o.o.

## 3. Corporate income tax (porez na dobit) 2026

- **10%** if revenue in the tax period is **below €1,000,000**; **18%** if revenue is €1,000,000 or more
  (Zakon o porezu na dobit čl. 28; NN 114/23, last amended NN 151/25, in force 1.1.2026).
- Tax base: accounting profit adjusted for non-deductible items (e.g. 50% of representation, private use of
  cars, penalties) – details with an accountant.
- **PD return: within four months after year end** (30 April for a calendar year); the tax difference is due the
  same day; filed electronically (čl. 35, NN 114/23, 151/25).
- **Monthly advances:** last year's tax ÷ 12, paid by the end of the month for the previous month (čl. 34).
  The first year has no advances unless PU sets them.
- 2026 changes (NN 151/25): transfer pricing, sponsorship deduction for sport, foreign tax credit; new PD forms
  (Pravilnik NN 39/2026). No rate change.

## 4. Director and members: contributions

- **Member of the management board (član uprave / direktor) who is not employed** (not insured on another basis,
  e.g. no employment contract with this or another company and not a pensioner): must be insured as a board
  member (ZOD čl. 9 st. 1 t. 15, čl. 90). Monthly base = **average wage × 1.0 = €1,993.00 in 2026** (ZOD čl. 92;
  NN 150/2025 čl. 7). PU sets the obligation by rješenje; no JOPPD for this basis.

  | MIO I 15% | MIO II 5% | HZZO 16.5% | **Monthly total 2026** |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 298.95 | 99.65 | 328.85 | **727.45** (8,729.40 a year) |

  This applies **even if the director takes no salary.** It is the main hidden cost of a one-person d.o.o.
  where the owner has no other job.
- **Director employed by the company full time:** salary with contributions; the minimum contribution base for a
  board member working full time is **€1,295.45/month** (NN 150/2025 čl. 19 st. 1). Payroll detail:
  `croatia:hr-payroll-employment`.
- **Director employed elsewhere full time** (or insured on another basis): no board-member contributions; if
  the company pays him a fee, contributions and tax are calculated on that payment (ZOD čl. 94). **VERIFY**
  structure with an accountant.
- **Director who is a pensioner:** special rules (ZOD čl. 187.c for working pensioners; TEB 2026 article on
  minimum bases for pensioner directors). **VERIFY**.
- **Members (članovi društva) who are not directors** have no contribution obligation from membership alone.

## 5. Taking money out of the company

| Route | Tax 2026 | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Salary** (plaća) | Contributions 20% (employee) + 16.5% (employer) + income tax at city rates after allowance | Deductible for profit tax. See `croatia:hr-payroll-employment`. |
| **Dividend / udio u dobiti** | **12% withholding** (dohodak od kapitala, ZPD čl. 69, čl. 70 st. 19) | Paid from profit after profit tax. No contributions. Final tax (no annual return, ZPD čl. 49). Not taxed if used to increase share capital (čl. 69 st. 2). |
| **Interim dividend** (predujam dobiti) | 12% | If year-end profit is lower, the difference is taxed as other income (Zakon o porezu na dobit čl. 10.a). |
| **Loan to member** | Interest must be charged at least at the prescribed rate (**VERIFY** current rate) | Disguised withdrawals are recharacterised. |
| **Private use of company assets** | Taxable benefit in kind | Accountant. |
| **Taking assets out (izuzimanje)** | 36% (ZPD čl. 66, čl. 70 st. 2) | Avoid. |

Effective tax on distributed profit (small company): 10% profit tax, then 12% on the remaining 90% = **20.8%**
total (0.10 + 0.90 × 0.12). (The dividend rate was 10% plus surtax before 2024; it is 12% flat since 1.1.2024.)
Plus director contributions (§4) if applicable.

j.d.o.o.: only 3/4 of profit is distributable until capital reaches €2,500 (§2).

## 6. Accounting, annual financial statements (GFI), deadlines

Zakon o računovodstvu (ZOR, NN 85/24, 145/24, 151/25, 59/26):
- **Double-entry bookkeeping** (dvojno knjigovodstvo), annual inventory, Croatian financial reporting standards
  (HSFI) for micro/small companies.
- **Micro** = does not exceed two of: total assets €450,000; net revenue €900,000; 10 employees (ZOR čl. 5
  st. 3). Micro and small companies prepare a balance sheet, P&L and notes only (čl. 18 st. 3).
- **GFI for statistical purposes to FINA: by 30 April** for the previous year (balance sheet, P&L, additional
  data) (ZOR čl. 50 st. 1).
- **Public disclosure (javna objava) of annual financial statements: within six months of year end** (30 June)
  (ZOR čl. 47 st. 5); filing by 30 April can satisfy both (čl. 50 st. 2). **VERIFY** which documents your size
  class must publish.
- **Statement of inactivity (Izjava o neaktivnosti)** by 30 April if there were no transactions and no assets or
  liabilities (ZOR čl. 47 st. 9).
- **Retention:** bookkeeping documents behind the journal/general ledger and subsidiary ledgers **at least 11
  years**, payslips 6 years, payroll records permanently (ZOR čl. 10, NN 151/25), unless the fiscalization law
  says otherwise (eRačun 6 years). Annual financial statements permanently.
- In practice nearly every d.o.o. uses an accountant (računovodstveni servis). Market estimates (not official,
  2026): about €100–250/month for a small company with few invoices and no employees; more with payroll, VAT
  and many documents. Get quotes; check the accountant's professional liability insurance.

## 7. Mandatory company data on paper, invoices, e-mails and website

ZTD čl. 21 st. 4–5: company name, seat, registration court and MBS number, bank(s) and account numbers; for a
d.o.o./j.d.o.o. also **share capital and whether fully paid**, and names of the **board members**. The same
applies to the company website. Plus OIB and other data under the Zakon o elektroničkoj trgovini.
Website impressum, privacy and terms: `croatia:hr-legal-compliance`. OIB/MBS validation: `croatia:hr-identifiers`.

## 8. Typical recurring obligations (non-VAT micro d.o.o., no employees, director not employed)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Monthly by 15th | Director contributions (€727.45 in 2026) per PU rješenje |
| Monthly by end of month | Profit tax advance (if set) |
| Monthly, 20th | eRačun payment e-reporting where applicable (`croatia:hr-legal-compliance` 04) |
| Per transaction | Fiscalize B2C invoices; issue/receive eRačun (VAT payers since 2026, all from 2027) |
| Last day of following month | PDV / ZP / PDV-S if VAT-registered or PDV-ID used that month (`pdv-vat.md`) |
| 30 April | GFI to FINA, PD profit-tax return |
| 30 June | Public disclosure (if not filed by 30 April) |
| On change | Court register (director, seat, activities, members), PU (RPO) |

## 9. d.d., udruga, zadruga (brief)

- **d.d. (dioničko društvo):** minimum capital **€25,000** (ZTD čl. 162); shares ≥ €1 (čl. 163). Rarely the right
  choice for a small business.
- **Udruga (association):** non-profit; may carry on economic activity only as defined in its statute and not
  for members' profit; not a profit taxpayer unless the economic activity would distort competition (Zakon o
  porezu na dobit čl. 2 st. 6–7). Not a vehicle for a for-profit business. Grants: `croatia:hr-eu-funding`.
- **Zadruga (cooperative):** at least several members (**VERIFY** current minimum in Zakon o zadrugama); member-
  owned; profit tax applies. Manager (upravitelj) has contribution rules like a director (ZOD čl. 90).
