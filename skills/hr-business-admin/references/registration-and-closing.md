## 1. Opening an obrt

- **Online:** **e-Obrt** (via e-Građani, needs a high-assurance e-identity such as eOI or bank token) or the
  **START** platform (gov.hr: for Croatian citizens with residence in Croatia; covers obrt, j.d.o.o., d.o.o.).
  In person: the county office for the economy (ured državne uprave) or HOK service points.
- Data: name of the obrt (must contain the owner's name and "obrt"), seat (can be at home – **VERIFY** building
  or zoning limits for activities with customers), activities (NKD), start date, tax regime (paušal / dohodak /
  dobit), whether it is main or other activity.
- **Cost:** no administrative fees for registering an obrt since 1.9.2021 (secondary sources: fiskalopedija,
  fiskai – **VERIFY** on gov.hr). The legal deadline for the decision is up to 15 days; online often faster.
- **Vezani obrt:** attach proof of qualification (`legal-forms.md` §5).
- **What follows automatically vs. manually** (**VERIFY** for the route you use – START is designed to do
  more automatically):
  - registration in the Obrtni registar → data flows to PU (RPO), HZMO/HZZO in most cases;
  - choose paušal and register in RPO (form RPO within 8 days if not automatic – ZPD čl. 83);
  - check in ePorezna that the regime and contributions are set correctly.

## 2. Founding a d.o.o. / j.d.o.o.

Routes (gov.hr "Osnivanje društva s ograničenom odgovornošću", "Osnivanje j.d.o.o."):
1. **START** – fully electronic, **no notary**; Croatian citizens with residence in Croatia. Court fee only
   (guides quote €26.55 d.o.o. / €3.98 j.d.o.o. – **VERIFY** current Uredba o tarifi sudskih pristojbi).
2. **e-Sudski registar** – electronic founding for Croatian and EU/EEA citizens; other steps separately.
3. **Notary + commercial court** – for everyone incl. third-country nationals.

In-person steps (gov.hr):
1. Check the company name in the court register (sudreg.pravosudje.hr).
2. Notary: izjava o osnivanju (one founder) / društveni ugovor (several), Po form, statement of no tax debt.
   j.d.o.o.: statutory template only.
3. Pay capital into a temporary account (bank or FINA): d.o.o. at least 1/4 of each cash share, total ≥ €625;
   j.d.o.o. full amount (≥ €1).
4. Court registration → decision + OIB.
5. DZS classification notice (RPS-1 to registar@dzs.hr).
6. Open the business bank account.
7. HZMO/HZZO registration (M-11P, M-1P, Tiskanica 1 and 2) – director/employees.
8. PU: RPO form (with lease, accountant contract etc.); P-PDV if VAT registration or PDV-ID needed.

**HITRO.HR** is still listed on gov.hr as "additional information"; its role has largely moved to START and
e-services. **VERIFY** whether a HITRO.HR office/service is still the right channel before sending anyone there.

- [ ] Business bank account (company: mandatory for capital; obrt: strongly advised – legal obligation **VERIFY**).
- [ ] ePorezna access; check RPO data, tax regime, contributions on the porezna kartica.

## 5. Bookkeeping – what an accountant does

- Paušal: optional – KPR, PO-SD, fiscal software setup, PDV-ID returns if any. Market estimate €25–60/month.
- Obrt na dohodak: KPI, DI, DOH return, advances, VAT if registered, JOPPD. Estimate €60–150/month.
- d.o.o.: double entry, VAT, payroll, GFI, PD, statistics, FINA filings. Estimate €100–250+/month for small
  volumes; payroll and many documents cost more.
- These ranges are **market estimates** (not official) – get several quotes. Check that the service has
  professional liability insurance and what is included (number of documents, payroll slips, VAT returns).

## 6. Record retention

- **Companies (Zakon o računovodstvu čl. 10, NN 151/25):** documents behind journal/general ledger and subsidiary
  ledgers **≥ 11 years**, payslips 6 years, payroll records permanently, annual financial statements permanently;
  unless the fiscalization law provides otherwise (eRačun 6 years, Zakon o fiskalizaciji čl. 35).
- **Obrt (paušal/dohodak):** the Opći porezni zakon limitation rules apply (books kept 10 years from the start of
  the limitation period) – practically **11–12 calendar years**. Legal skill 04 §4 has the table.

## 7. Mirovanje – temporary suspension of an obrt

- An obrt may be temporarily suspended (privremena obustava) by notifying the Obrtni registar (e-Obrt or the
  office); it remains registered (Zakon o obrtu čl. 2 st. 5 treats suspension as continuing operation).
- During suspension: for a main-activity obrt contributions generally **continue** unless a statutory exception
  applies (e.g. HZZO sick leave from day 43, parental leave) – **VERIFY** with PU/HZMO; for paušal next to
  employment, suspension months are excluded from the annual contribution calculation (ZOD čl. 186 st. 3 –
  **VERIFY** article number). HOK fee: may be written off for suspension due to illness, parental leave, force
  majeure (Odluka NN 154/2022 čl. 14).
- Maximum duration and notification deadline: **VERIFY** in the current Zakon o obrtu (secondary sources mention
  up to one year, exceptionally longer).
- Fiscalization: no B2C sales during suspension; keep the premises registration consistent.

## 8. Closing an obrt (odjava)

1. Deregister in the Obrtni registar (e-Obrt or office); free since 1.9.2021 (guides – **VERIFY**).
2. **PU:** RPO deregistration within 8 days (**VERIFY**); paušalist files PO-SD for the part year (by 15 January,
   or earlier as PU requires – **VERIFY**); obrt na dohodak files DOH with a closing inventory; VAT payers file the
   final PDV and deregister from VAT (VAT on remaining assets may apply).
3. **HZMO/HZZO:** deregistration (M-2P, M-12P) if not automatic.
4. Close fiscal premises in FiskAplikacija, revoke certificates, update AMS (eRačun).
5. Settle contributions and taxes up to the closing date; HOK fee ends on deregistration (Odluka čl. 4).
6. Keep records for the retention period (§6).
7. Debts of the obrt remain the owner's personal debts.

## 9. Closing a d.o.o. / j.d.o.o.

- **Regular liquidation (likvidacija)** (ZTD čl. 370–380 and the d.o.o. provisions referring to them): members' decision →
  "u likvidaciji" added to the name (čl. 370) → liquidators (usually directors, čl. 371) → **creditors invited to
  file claims within six months** of publication (čl. 373) → opening liquidation financial statements (čl. 376) →
  collect receivables, pay debts → distribution to members → final report → deletion from the register.
  Practically 7–12 months plus accountant/notary costs.
- **Shortened procedure without liquidation (prestanak po skraćenom postupku, ZTD čl. 472.a–472.b, NN 40/19):**
  possible if **all members agree**, by notarial deed/certified document, with a plan for distributing assets, and
  all members declare that the company has **no debts to employees or other creditors** and that they **jointly and
  severally** take over any debts that appear later. Published on the court-register website, then filed with the
  registry court. Fast, but members become personally liable.
- **Deletion ex officio** for inactive companies under the Zakon o sudskom registru – not a planned exit;
  **VERIFY** conditions.
- **Insolvency:** if the company cannot pay, the director must act (insolvency law) – outside this skill; refer
  to a lawyer.
- Before any exit: final PD and GFI, VAT deregistration, deregister director insurance, close fiscal/eRačun
  settings, keep records (§6).

## 10. Self-employment subsidies

- **HZZ potpora za samozapošljavanje** (Croatian Employment Service grant for unemployed people who start an obrt
  or company) and other start-up grants: see `croatia:hr-eu-funding`. Key admin point: many grants require that
  you are registered as unemployed **before** registering the business and that the business stays active for a
  set period – check conditions **before** opening the obrt.
